Theorem 1.10 (Bochner).

Let \((X,g)\) be compact Ricci-flat Kähler. Every holomorphic tensor formed from \(T_X\) and \(\Omega_X^1\) is parallel. In particular, for \(x\in X\text{,}\) evaluation gives
\begin{equation*} H^0(X,\Omega_X^p)\cong\bigl(\Lambda^p(T_x^{1,0}X)^*\bigr)^{\operatorname{Hol}_x(g)}. \end{equation*}
The group in this formula is the full holonomy group.
