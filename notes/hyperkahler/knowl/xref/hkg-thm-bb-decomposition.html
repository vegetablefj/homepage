Theorem 1.13 (Beauville–Bogomolov).

Let \(X\) be a compact Kähler manifold with \(c_1(X)=0\) in real cohomology. Its universal cover is biholomorphic to
\begin{equation*} \mathbb C^a\times\prod_i Y_i\times\prod_j Z_j, \end{equation*}
where each \(Y_i\) is simply connected and projective, of dimension \(m_i\geq3\text{,}\) with trivial canonical bundle and \(H^0(Y_i,\Omega_{Y_i}^p)=0\) for \(0<p<m_i\text{,}\) and each \(Z_j\) is IHS. There is a finite étale cover
\begin{equation*} T\times\prod_iY_i\times\prod_jZ_j\longrightarrow X, \end{equation*}
where \(T\) is a complex torus. Factors of dimension zero are omitted.
🔗
For a Ricci-flat Kähler metric on \(X\text{,}\) the lifted metric has the corresponding de Rham product decomposition. The nonflat factors have holonomy \(\mathrm{SU}(m_i)\) and \(\mathrm{Sp}(\dim Z_j/2)\text{,}\) respectively.
