Theorem 10.12 (Shen–Yin; Voisin).

Let \(f:X\to B\) be a Lagrangian fibration from an IHS \(2n\)-fold. For every smooth fiber \(F\) over the submersion locus and every Kähler class \(\omega\) on \(X\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{im}\bigl(H^k(X,\R)\to H^k(F,\R)\bigr)= \begin{cases}\R(\omega|_F)^i,&k=2i,\ 0\leq i\leq n,\\0,&k\text{ odd or }k>2n.\end{cases} \end{equation*}
Over \(\Q\text{,}\) the map has rank one in even degree at most \(2n\) and rank zero in odd degree.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose an ample line bundle \(A\) on the projective base \(B\) and let \(\lambda=c_1(f^*A)\text{.}\) Its square for the BBF form is zero. Intersecting general hyperplanes on the base shows that \([F]\) is a nonzero scalar multiple of \(\lambda^n\text{.}\) Fibers over the connected submersion locus have the same cohomology class, so this holds for each such \(F\text{.}\) By Lemma 10.11, the rank of restriction equals the rank of cup product by \(\lambda^n\text{.}\)
🔗
For \(a\in H^2(X,\C)\text{,}\) the matrix entries of \(a^n\smile-\) are homogeneous polynomials in \(a\text{.}\) Consequently the condition that its rank in degree \(k\) is at most a prescribed integer is Zariski closed, being defined by minors. Let
\begin{equation*} \mathscr Q=\{a\in H^2(X,\C):q_X(a)=0\} \end{equation*}
be the affine isotropic cone. It is irreducible because \(q_X\) is nondegenerate and \(b_2(X)\geq3\text{.}\) Local Torelli gives a nonempty analytic open subset of the projective quadric \(\PP(\mathscr Q)\) consisting of period lines \([\sigma_t]\text{.}\) Allowing all nonzero scalar multiples \(a=c\sigma_t\) therefore gives a nonempty analytic open subset of \(\mathscr Q\setminus\{0\}\text{.}\) The differentiable trivialization identifies the cup products on the fibers.
🔗
Since \(\sigma_t^n\) has Hodge type \((2n,0)\text{,}\) cup product by it kills every component \(H^{p,k-p}(X_t)\) with \(p>0\text{.}\) The remaining component \(H^{0,k}(X_t)\) is zero for odd \(k\) and one-dimensional for even \(k\leq2n\text{,}\) by Proposition 1.17. Thus the rank is zero or at most one, respectively; multiplying \(\sigma_t\) by \(c\ne0\) does not change it. This analytic open subset is Zariski dense in the irreducible cone \(\mathscr Q\text{.}\) The closed rank condition therefore holds on the entire cone, in particular at \(a=\lambda\text{.}\) For \(k>2n\text{,}\) restriction vanishes for dimensional reasons.
🔗
In degree \(2i\) with \(0\leq i\leq n\text{,}\) the restriction \((\omega|_F)^i\) is nonzero, because its product with \((\omega|_F)^{n-i}\) has positive integral. Hence restriction has rank exactly one and its image is the displayed line. Extension of scalars preserves the rank of a rational linear map, giving the rational assertion.
