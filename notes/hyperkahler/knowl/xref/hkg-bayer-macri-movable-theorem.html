Theorem 9.15 (Bayer–Macrì).

The chamber maps glue to a continuous piecewise analytic map
\begin{equation*} \ell:\operatorname{Stab}^{\dagger}(S)\longrightarrow\operatorname{NS}(M)_\mathbb R. \end{equation*}
Its image is \(\overline{\operatorname{Mov}}(M)\cap\operatorname{Big}(M)\text{.}\) For each chamber \(C\text{,}\) its image is exactly the ample cone of \(M_C(v)\text{,}\) viewed in this fixed divisor space. Every smooth projective IHS manifold birational to \(M\) occurs as \(M_\tau(v)\) for some generic \(\tau\text{.}\)
