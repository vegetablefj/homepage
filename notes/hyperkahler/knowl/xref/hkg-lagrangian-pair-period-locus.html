Theorem 10.16 (Voisin).

Let \(Y\) be a smooth compact Lagrangian submanifold of a compact IHS manifold \(X\text{.}\) Trivialize the degree-two cohomology local system over a small representative \(V\) of \(\operatorname{Def}(X)\text{,}\) and regard \([\sigma_t]\) as a class in \(H^2(X,\C)\text{.}\) Then, as germs at \(0\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} D_Y=\{t\in V:j^*[\sigma_t]=0\text{ in }H^2(Y,\C)\}.\tag{10.2} \end{equation}
Both \(\operatorname{Def}(X,Y)\) and \(D_Y\) are smooth, and \(p\) is a submersion onto \(D_Y\text{.}\) Moreover,
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{codim}_{\operatorname{Def}(X)}D_Y=r_Y,\qquad T_0D_Y=\ker\rho_Y. \end{equation*}
