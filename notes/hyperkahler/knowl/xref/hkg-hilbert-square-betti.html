Proposition 11.22.

For a K3 surface \(S\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} P_{S^{[2]}}(t)=1+23t^2+276t^4+23t^6+t^8. \end{equation*}
In particular, \(H^*(S^{[2]},\Q)=\SH^*(S^{[2]})\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Only the factors with \(m=1,2\) can contribute to the coefficient of \(u^2\text{.}\) For \(m=1\text{,}\) the factor is \((1-u)^{-1}(1-t^2u)^{-22}(1-t^4u)^{-1}\text{.}\) Its coefficient of \(u^2\) is
\begin{equation*} 1+22t^2+254t^4+22t^6+t^8. \end{equation*}
The term \(254t^4\) consists of \(\binom{23}{2}t^4\) from two degree-two factors and one further \(t^4\) from the degree-zero and degree-four factors. The \(m=2\) factor contributes \((t^2+22t^4+t^6)u^2\) at its first nonconstant term. Adding gives the stated polynomial.
🔗
Here \(n=2\) and \(b=23\text{.}\) Formula (11.7) gives dimensions \(1,23,\binom{24}{2},23,1\text{,}\) with \(\binom{24}{2}=276\text{,}\) for the subalgebra generated by degree two. These equal the full Betti numbers in every degree. Inclusion is therefore equality.
