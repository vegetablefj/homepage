Proposition 8.18.

The sheaf \(i_*L\) is \(H\)-stable for every ample \(H\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
A nonzero subsheaf has generic rank one on the integral curve \(C\text{;}\) its quotient is supported in dimension zero. If the subsheaf is proper, that quotient has positive length. The leading coefficient of the Hilbert polynomial does not change, while its constant term decreases by this length. Its reduced Hilbert polynomial is therefore strictly smaller.
