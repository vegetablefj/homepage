Theorem 11.12.

The Cartan subalgebra, orthogonal coordinates, and positive chamber can be chosen so that the weight \(\nu=(\nu_0,\nu_1,\ldots)\) contributes to
\begin{equation} (p,q)=(n+\nu_0+\nu_1,\ n+\nu_0-\nu_1).\tag{11.8} \end{equation}
In particular, its cohomological degree is \(2n+2\nu_0\text{.}\) See [GKLR22, equations (2.28)–(2.31)].
