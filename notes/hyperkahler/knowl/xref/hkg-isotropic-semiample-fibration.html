Proposition 10.22.

Let \(L\) be a nontrivial nef line bundle on an IHS manifold, with \(q_X(c_1(L))=0\text{.}\) If \(L\) is semiample, a sufficiently divisible positive power defines a Lagrangian fibration after Stein factorization.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
A generated power defines a morphism to projective space. Its Stein factorization is \(f:X\to B\text{,}\) where \(B\) is normal and projective, \(f_*\cO_X=\cO_B\text{,}\) and \(L^{\otimes m}=f^*A\) for an ample line bundle \(A\) on \(B\text{.}\) The class \(c_1(L)\) is nonzero by Proposition 2.6. Its numerical dimension is \(n\) by Proposition 2.14, while the pullback of an ample class has numerical dimension \(\dim B\text{.}\) Hence \(\dim B=n\text{.}\) It is a nontrivial fibration, so Theorem 10.2 makes it Lagrangian.
