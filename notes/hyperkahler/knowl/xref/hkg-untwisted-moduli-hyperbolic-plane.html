Theorem 9.23 (Addington).

Let \(X\) be projective of \(\mathrm{K3}^{[n]}\) type, with \(n\geq2\text{.}\) The lattice \(\widetilde\Lambda_{\mathrm{alg}}\) contains an isometric copy of \(U\) if and only if \(X\) is birational to a moduli space \(M_H(v)\) on a projective K3 surface, for a primitive positive-rank Mukai vector \(v\) with \(v^2=2n-2\) and a \(v\)-generic polarization \(H\text{.}\)
