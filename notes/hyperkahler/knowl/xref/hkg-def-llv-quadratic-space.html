Definition 11.4.

Let \(V=H^2(X,\C)\) and \(W=\C e_-\oplus V\oplus\C e_+\text{,}\) where \(e_-,e_+\) are basis vectors of an auxiliary two-dimensional space. Define a symmetric bilinear form \(Q\) on \(W\) by
\begin{equation} Q|_V=q_X,\qquad Q(e_-,e_-)=Q(e_+,e_+)=0,\qquad Q(e_-,e_+)=1,\qquad Q(e_\pm,V)=0.\tag{11.3} \end{equation}
Thus \(W\) is the orthogonal sum of \((V,q_X)\) and a hyperbolic plane.
