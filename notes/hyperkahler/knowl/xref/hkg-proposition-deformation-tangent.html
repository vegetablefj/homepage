Proposition 4.4.

Choose a generator \(\sigma\in H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)\text{.}\) Contraction with \(\sigma\) induces isomorphisms
\begin{equation*} T_X\xrightarrow{\ \iota_\sigma\ }\Omega_X^1,\qquad H^1(X,T_X)\xrightarrow{\ \iota_\sigma\ }H^{1,1}(X). \end{equation*}
Moreover, \(H^0(X,T_X)=0\) and \(h^1(X,T_X)=b-2\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
At a point \(x\in X\text{,}\) nondegeneracy means exactly that the map \(v\mapsto\sigma_x(v,-)\) from \(T_{X,x}\) to its dual is an isomorphism. The local maps vary holomorphically, so they give an isomorphism of holomorphic vector bundles. Taking cohomology and using the Dolbeault isomorphism gives the second displayed isomorphism. Since \(X\) is simply connected, \(H^1(X,\mathbb C)=0\text{;}\) the Kähler Hodge decomposition then gives \(H^0(X,\Omega_X^1)=0\text{.}\) Thus \(H^0(X,T_X)=0\text{.}\) Finally,
\begin{equation*} b=h^{2,0}(X)+h^{1,1}(X)+h^{0,2}(X)=h^{1,1}(X)+2. \end{equation*}
This proves the dimension assertion.
