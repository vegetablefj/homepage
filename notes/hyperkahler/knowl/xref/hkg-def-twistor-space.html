Definition 3.2.

On the smooth product \(Z=M\times\mathbb P^1\text{,}\) define the almost complex structure
\begin{equation*} \mathcal I_{(x,\zeta)}(v,w)=(I_\zeta v,iw),\qquad (v,w)\in T_xM\oplus T_\zeta\mathbb P^1. \end{equation*}
The resulting complex manifold, once integrability is established, is the twistor space. Projection \(\pi:Z\to\mathbb P^1\) is its twistor family.
