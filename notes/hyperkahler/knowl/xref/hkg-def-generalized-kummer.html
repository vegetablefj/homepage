Definition 5.10.

For \(n\geq1\text{,}\) the generalized Kummer variety is \(K_n(A):=s^{-1}(0)\subset A^{[n+1]}\text{.}\)
 3 
Our index records half the dimension: \(\dim K_n(A)=2n\text{.}\) Some authors write \(K_{n-1}(A)\subset A^{[n]}\) or index by the number of points. Here \(K_1(A)\) is a K3 surface, whereas \(K_2(A)\) is a fourfold.
