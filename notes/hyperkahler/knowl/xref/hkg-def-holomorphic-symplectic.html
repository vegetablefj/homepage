Definition 1.7.

A holomorphic symplectic manifold is a complex manifold \(X\) with a holomorphic two-form \(\sigma\) such that \(d\sigma=0\) and contraction
\begin{equation*} \sigma^\flat:T_X\longrightarrow\Omega_X^1,\qquad v\longmapsto\iota_v\sigma \end{equation*}
is an isomorphism. We also use the term for \(X\) when the form is understood or only its existence is asserted.
