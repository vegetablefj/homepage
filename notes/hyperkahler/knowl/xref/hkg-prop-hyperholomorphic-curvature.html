Proposition 12.4.

For a unitary connection \(\nabla\text{,}\) the following conditions are equivalent: it is hyperholomorphic; its curvature has type \((1,1)\) for every induced complex structure; its curvature is \(SU(2)\)-invariant. Each implies \(\Lambda_LF_\nabla=0\) for every induced \(L\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The integrability identity and its unitary conjugate give the first equivalence. The second is Lemma 12.1, applied to the endomorphism-valued curvature. The contraction vanishes by Lemma 12.2.
