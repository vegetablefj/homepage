Example 3.13.

Let \(X=(M,I)\) be projective IHS with Picard number \(\rho\text{,}\) and choose the hyperkähler metric whose Kähler class is an integral ample class \(h\text{.}\) Since \(P_g\) is spanned by \(h,\operatorname{Re}[\sigma],\operatorname{Im}[\sigma]\text{,}\) we have
\begin{equation*} \Lambda_g=\operatorname{NS}(X)\cap h^\perp. \end{equation*}
The rational linear functional \(q_X(h,-)\) is nonzero on \(\operatorname{NS}(X)\otimes\mathbb Q\text{,}\) because \(q_X(h)>0\text{.}\) Its kernel has dimension \(\rho-1\text{.}\) Thus the very general twistor fiber has Picard number \(\rho-1\text{,}\) and its Picard lattice is negative definite. For \(\rho\geq2\) this supplies nonzero Picard classes that persist even though the very general fiber has no effective divisors. Projective K3 surfaces of Picard number at least two already give examples.
