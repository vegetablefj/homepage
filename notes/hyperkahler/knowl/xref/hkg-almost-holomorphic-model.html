Theorem 10.20 (Greb–Lehn–Rollenske).

Let \(X\) be projective IHS, and let \(f:X\dashrightarrow B\) be an almost holomorphic Lagrangian fibration with normal projective base. There are a projective IHS manifold \(X'\text{,}\) birational maps \(\phi:X\dashrightarrow X'\) and \(\beta:B\dashrightarrow B'\text{,}\) and a holomorphic Lagrangian fibration \(f':X'\to B'\) such that \(f'\circ\phi=\beta\circ f\) as meromorphic maps. The map \(\phi\) is an isomorphism near a general fiber of \(f\text{.}\)
