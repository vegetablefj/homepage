Proposition 12.18.

Let \(E\) be a holomorphic vector bundle of rank \(r\) on a compact hyperkähler manifold. Suppose \(E\) is slope polystable with respect to \(\omega_I\text{.}\) Its Hermitian–Einstein connection is projectively hyperholomorphic if and only if
\begin{equation*} \Delta(E)=2r c_2(E)-(r-1)c_1(E)^2 \end{equation*}
is \(SU(2)\)-invariant in real cohomology.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The induced connection on \(\operatorname{End}E\) is Hermitian–Einstein of slope zero: its contracted curvature sends \(T\) to \([\Lambda_I F_\nabla,T]=0\text{.}\) Thus \(\operatorname{End}E\) is slope polystable by Theorem 12.8. Multiplying \(\operatorname{ch}(E)\) and \(\operatorname{ch}(E^\vee)\) in degrees at most four gives
\begin{equation*} c_1(\operatorname{End}E)=0,\qquad c_2(\operatorname{End}E)=2r c_2(E)-(r-1)c_1(E)^2. \end{equation*}
Apply Theorem 12.13 to this Hermitian–Einstein connection. It is hyperholomorphic exactly when \(\Delta(E)\) is invariant. By the injectivity of the adjoint representation on trace-free endomorphisms, this is equivalent to invariance of \(F_\nabla^0\text{.}\)
