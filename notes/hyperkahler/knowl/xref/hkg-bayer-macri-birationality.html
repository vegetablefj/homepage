Theorem 9.14 (Bayer–Macrì).

For primitive \(v\in N(S)\) of positive square, all moduli spaces \(M_\sigma(v)\) with \(\sigma\in\operatorname{Stab}^{\dagger}(S)\) generic are birational. More precisely, a suitable derived autoequivalence or anti-autoequivalence relates the objects representing corresponding points on common open subsets whose complements have codimension at least two.
