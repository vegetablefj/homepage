Lemma 12.1.

A complex differential form of degree \(2p\) is \(SU(2)\)-invariant if and only if it has type \((p,p)\) for every induced complex structure. In particular, an endomorphism-valued two-form is invariant if and only if it has type \((1,1)\) for every induced complex structure.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For an induced complex structure \(L\text{,}\) consider the circle \(u(t)=\cos t+L\sin t\) of unit quaternions. On forms of type \((a,b)\text{,}\) its action is multiplication by \(e^{-i(a-b)t}\text{.}\) Its fixed subspace in degree \(2p\) is therefore exactly type \((p,p)\text{.}\) Every element of \(SU(2)\) lies in one of these circles. A form is fixed by all circles exactly when it is fixed by the group. The same calculation applies coefficient by coefficient to a bundle-valued form.
