Proposition 10.4 (Voisin).

Every compact Lagrangian submanifold \(F\) of an IHS manifold is projective.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Restriction \(r:H^2(X,\R)\to H^2(F,\R)\) kills \(H^{2,0}(X)\oplus H^{0,2}(X)\text{,}\) since these two summands are generated by \(\sigma\) and \(\bar\sigma\text{.}\) Thus its image lies in \(H^{1,1}(F,\R)\text{.}\) It contains the Kähler class \(r(\omega)\) for any Kähler class \(\omega\) on \(X\text{.}\) Rational classes are dense in \(H^2(X,\R)\text{,}\) and the Kähler cone of \(F\) is open. Consequently some \(\alpha\in H^2(X,\Q)\) has \(r(\alpha)\) Kähler. Multiplying by an integer gives an integral Kähler class on \(F\text{;}\) the Kodaira embedding theorem makes \(F\) projective.
