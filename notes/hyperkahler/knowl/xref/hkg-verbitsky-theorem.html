Theorem 11.9 (Verbitsky–Bogomolov).

The subalgebra \(\SH^*(X,\C)\) is an irreducible \(\mathfrak g\)-module. In the orthogonal highest-weight coordinates specified below its highest weight is \((n,0,\ldots,0)\text{;}\) it occurs with multiplicity one in total cohomology. For \(0\leq k\leq n\text{,}\) the map \(\operatorname{Sym}^kH^2(X,\Q)\to H^{2k}(X,\Q)\) is injective. Over \(\C\text{,}\) the relations in the subalgebra are precisely the ideal generated by \(a^{n+1}\) for \(a\in H^2(X,\C)\) with \(q_X(a)=0\text{:}\)
\begin{equation} \SH^*(X,\C)\cong \operatorname{Sym}^*V/ \left\langle a^{n+1}:a\in V,\ q_X(a)=0\right\rangle.\tag{11.6} \end{equation}
Each element of \(V\) has degree two. The irreducibility and injection statements are due to Verbitsky; the ideal presentation in this form is due to Bogomolov. See [Ver96c] and [GKLR22, Theorem 2.15, Remark 2.17, and Proposition 2.32].
