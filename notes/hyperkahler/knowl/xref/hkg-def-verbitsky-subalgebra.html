Definition 11.7.

The subalgebra \(\SH^*(X)\subset H^*(X,\Q)\) generated by \(H^2(X,\Q)\) is the image of the cup-product map from its symmetric algebra. We retain ordinary cohomological grading:
\begin{equation*} \SH^{2k}(X):= \operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^kH^2(X,\Q) \longrightarrow H^{2k}(X,\Q)\bigr),\qquad \SH^{2k+1}(X)=0. \end{equation*}
In particular, \(\SH^0(X)=\Q\cdot1\) and \(\SH^2(X)=H^2(X,\Q)\text{.}\)
