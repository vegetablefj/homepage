Theorem 11.5 (Looijenga–Lunts–Verbitsky).

The LLV algebra of \(X\) has only degrees \(-2,0,2\) for the adjoint action of \(h\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \begin{gathered}\mathfrak g=\mathfrak g_{-2}\oplus\mathfrak g_0 \oplus\mathfrak g_2,\qquad \mathfrak g_j=\{A\in\mathfrak g:[h,A]=jA\}, \\ \mathfrak g_0=\C h\oplus\overline{\mathfrak g}.\end{gathered} \end{equation*}
Restriction to \(V=H^2(X,\C)\) identifies \(\overline{\mathfrak g}\) with \(\mathfrak{so}(V,q_X)\text{.}\) There is an isomorphism
\begin{equation*} \mathfrak g\cong\mathfrak{so}(W,Q) \cong\mathfrak{so}(b+2,\C) \end{equation*}
under which \(\overline{\mathfrak g}\) annihilates \(e_-,e_+\text{,}\) and \(h\) acts on \(W\) with eigenvalues \(-2,0,2\) on \(\C e_-,V,\C e_+\text{,}\) respectively. The space \(\mathfrak g_2\) consists of all cup-product operators \(L_a\text{,}\) \(a\in V\text{.}\) The corresponding real forms are \(\mathfrak g_{\R}\cong\mathfrak{so}(4,b-2)\) and \(\overline{\mathfrak g}_{\R}\cong\mathfrak{so}(3,b-3)\text{.}\) See [GKLR22, Theorem 2.7 and equations (2.3)–(2.10)].
🔗
Every \(D\in\overline{\mathfrak g}\) acts as a derivation of the cup-product algebra:
\begin{equation} D(u\smile v)=D(u)\smile v+u\smile D(v).\tag{11.4} \end{equation}
Here \(u,v\in H^*(X,\C)\text{;}\) see [GKLR22, equation (2.6)].
