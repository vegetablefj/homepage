Lemma 12.22.

A smooth trianalytic submanifold has even dimension, inherits the ambient hyperkähler structure, and is totally geodesic.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Its real tangent bundle is preserved by \(I,J,K\text{,}\) so each tangent space is quaternionic and has real dimension divisible by four. Its orthogonal normal bundle is also preserved. Orthogonal projection of the ambient Levi-Civita connection to the tangent bundle is the induced Levi-Civita connection and commutes with \(I,J,K\text{.}\) The induced metric and the restricted triple are therefore hyperkähler.
🔗
Let \(B(u,v)\) be the second fundamental form. Symmetry of \(B\text{,}\) parallelness of the ambient structures, and preservation of the normal bundle give \(B(Au,v)=B(u,Av)=AB(u,v)\) for \(A=I,J,K\text{.}\) Hence
\begin{equation*} B(Iu,Jv)=IJ B(u,v)=K B(u,v),\qquad B(Iu,Jv)=JI B(u,v)=-K B(u,v). \end{equation*}
Thus \(B=0\text{,}\) which is total geodesy.
