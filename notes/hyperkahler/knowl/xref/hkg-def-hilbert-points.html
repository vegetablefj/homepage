Definition 5.5.

For a smooth projective surface \(S\) and an integer \(n\geq1\text{,}\) let \(S^{[n]}=\operatorname{Hilb}^n(S)\) be the Hilbert scheme of zero-dimensional closed subschemes \(Z\subset S\) of length \(n\text{.}\) Length means \(\dim_\C H^0(Z,\mathcal O_Z)\text{.}\) The Hilbert–Chow morphism is
\begin{equation*} \rho:S^{[n]}\longrightarrow S^{(n)}:=S^n/\mathfrak S_n, \qquad Z\longmapsto\sum_{p\in\operatorname{Supp}(Z)} \operatorname{length}(\mathcal O_{Z,p})\,p. \end{equation*}
