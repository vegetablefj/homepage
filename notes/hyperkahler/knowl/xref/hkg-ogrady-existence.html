Theorem 5.13 (O’Grady).

  1. Let \(S\) be a projective K3 surface with \(\operatorname{Pic}(S)=\Z H\text{,}\) where \(H\) is ample and \(H^2=2\text{.}\) The moduli space of \(H\)-semistable torsion-free sheaves with Mukai vector \((2,0,-2)\) admits a projective symplectic resolution that is an irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold of dimension ten. Its deformation type is called \(\mathrm{OG}_{10}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Let \(A\) be the Jacobian of a genus-two curve, with \(\operatorname{NS}(A)=\Z H\) for its principal polarization \(H\text{.}\) The zero fibre of the determinant-and-Albanese map on the moduli space of \(H\)-semistable torsion-free sheaves with Mukai vector \((2,0,-2)\) admits a projective symplectic resolution that is an irreducible holomorphic symplectic manifold of dimension six. Its deformation type is called \(\mathrm{OG}_6\text{.}\)
