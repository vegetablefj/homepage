Theorem 14.14 (Oguiso).

For an automorphism \(f\) of an IHS \(2n\)-fold,
\begin{equation*} d_k(f)=d_{2n-k}(f)=d_1(f)^k \qquad(0\leq k\leq n),\qquad h_{\mathrm{top}}(f)=n\log d_1(f). \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
By Verbitsky’s injection, \(\Sym^kH^2(X,\R)\) is an \(f^*\)-stable subspace of \(H^{2k}(X,\R)\) for \(k\leq n\text{.}\) After triangularizing \(f^*\) over \(\C\text{,}\) the eigenvalues on its symmetric power are the products of \(k\) eigenvalues, with repetition allowed. Its spectral radius is therefore \(d_1(f)^k\text{,}\) giving a lower bound for \(d_k(f)\text{.}\) Concavity and \(d_0=1\) give the reverse bound \(\log d_k\leq k\log d_1\text{.}\)
🔗
The BBF form makes \(f^*|_{H^2}\) similar to its inverse transpose, so \(d_1(f^{-1})=d_1(f)\text{.}\) Poincaré duality gives \(d_{2n-k}(f)=d_k(f^{-1})\text{.}\) Apply the first part to \(f^{-1}\text{,}\) and then the entropy theorem. This is the proof of [Ogu09, Theorem 1.1].
