Definition 15.20.

Let \(A:V\to V\) be nilpotent on a finite-dimensional rational vector space, and let \(m\in\Z\text{.}\) Its weight filtration centered at \(m\) is the unique finite increasing filtration \(W_\bullet(A)[m]\text{,}\) with \(W_j=0\) for sufficiently negative \(j\) and \(W_j=V\) for sufficiently positive \(j\text{,}\) such that
\begin{equation*} A(W_j)\subset W_{j-2},\qquad A^r:\operatorname{Gr}_{m+r}^WV\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Gr}_{m-r}^WV\quad(r\geq0). \end{equation*}
Here \(\operatorname{Gr}_j^WV=W_jV/W_{j-1}V\text{.}\) The center is part of the notation: changing it from \(m\) to \(m+c\) replaces \(W_j\) by \(W_{j-c}\text{.}\)
