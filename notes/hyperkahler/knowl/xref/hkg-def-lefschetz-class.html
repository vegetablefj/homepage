Definition 11.1.

A class \(a\in H^2(X,\C)\) is a Lefschetz class if
\begin{equation*} L_a^j:H^{2n-j}(X,\C) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^{2n+j}(X,\C) \qquad(0\leq j\leq2n). \end{equation*}
For such a class, its lowering operator \(\Lambda_a\) is the unique degree-minus-two operator satisfying
\begin{equation} [h,L_a]=2L_a,\qquad [h,\Lambda_a]=-2\Lambda_a,\qquad [L_a,\Lambda_a]=h.\tag{11.1} \end{equation}
