Theorem 9.16 (Mukai–Orlov, Huybrechts–Macrì–Stellari).

A Hodge isometry \(\varphi:\widetilde H(S,\mathbb Z)\to\widetilde H(T,\mathbb Z)\) is induced by an exact equivalence \(D^b(S)\to D^b(T)\) if and only if it preserves the natural positive orientation. Given such an isometry, an inducing equivalence can be chosen to send \(\operatorname{Stab}^{\dagger}(S)\) to \(\operatorname{Stab}^{\dagger}(T)\text{.}\)
