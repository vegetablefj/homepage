Theorem 5.14 (Beauville–Donagi).

For every smooth cubic fourfold \(Y\text{,}\) the variety \(F(Y)\) is a smooth projective irreducible holomorphic symplectic fourfold of \(\mathrm{K3}^{[2]}\) type. The incidence correspondence of a point on a line induces an isomorphism between the primitive rational Hodge structures \(H^4(Y,\Q)(1)_{\mathrm{prim}}\) and \(H^2(F(Y),\Q)_{\mathrm{prim}}\text{.}\) Primitivity on \(Y\) is with respect to the hyperplane class, and on \(F(Y)\) with respect to the Plücker class. See [BD85].
