Definition 5.1.

A K3 surface is a simply connected compact Kähler surface \(S\) with trivial canonical bundle \(K_S\text{.}\)
 1 
A common equivalent definition over \(\C\) requires a connected compact complex surface with \(K_S\cong\mathcal O_S\) and \(H^1(S,\mathcal O_S)=0\text{.}\) The equivalence, including the Kähler and simple-connectedness assertions, is a theorem about complex surfaces. Over a general field the algebraic definition uses a smooth proper geometrically connected surface, trivial canonical bundle, and vanishing \(H^1(\mathcal O_S)\text{;}\) it does not use the topological fundamental group. See [Huy16, Chapter 1].
