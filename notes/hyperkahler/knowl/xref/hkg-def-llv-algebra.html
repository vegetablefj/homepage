Definition 11.3.

The Looijenga–Lunts–Verbitsky algebra is the complex Lie subalgebra
\begin{equation*} \mathfrak g\subset\operatorname{End}_{\C}H^*(X,\C) \end{equation*}
generated by \(L_a,h,\Lambda_a\) for all Lefschetz classes \(a\text{.}\) Its degree-zero subalgebra and its reduced LLV algebra are
\begin{equation} \mathfrak g_0:=\{D\in\mathfrak g:[h,D]=0\}, \qquad \overline{\mathfrak g}:=[\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak g_0].\tag{11.2} \end{equation}
The second expression means the linear span of the commutators of pairs of elements of \(\mathfrak g_0\text{.}\)
