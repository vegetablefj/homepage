Theorem 15.28 (Harder–Li–Shen–Yin).

For every Lagrangian fibration \(f:X\to B\) with \(X\) projective IHS, there exist a projective type III degeneration \(\pi:\mathcal Y\to\Delta\) with smooth fibers deformation equivalent to \(X\) and an isomorphism of graded rational cohomology rings
\begin{equation*} \Psi:H^*(X,\Q)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^*_{\mathrm{lim}}(\mathcal Y,\Q) \end{equation*}
such that, in every degree,
\begin{equation} \Psi(P_iH^d(X,\Q))=W_{2i}H^d_{\mathrm{lim}}(\mathcal Y,\Q)=W_{2i+1}H^d_{\mathrm{lim}}(\mathcal Y,\Q).\tag{15.7} \end{equation}
