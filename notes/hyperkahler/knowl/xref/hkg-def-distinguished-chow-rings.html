Definition 13.12.

Define
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \operatorname{DCH}^*(X) &:=\mathbb Q[c_1(L):L\in\operatorname{Pic}(X)],\\ R^*(X) &:=\mathbb Q[c_1(L),c_i(T_X): L\in\operatorname{Pic}(X),\ 1\leq i\leq2n] \ \subseteq\ \operatorname{CH}^*(X)_{\mathbb Q}. \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
Both rings include the identity. For a K3 surface, they agree with Definition 13.5.
