Theorem 9.20 (Markman).

Every manifold \(X\) of \(\mathrm{K3}^{[n]}\) type, \(n\geq2\text{,}\) has a distinguished \(O(\widetilde\Lambda)\)-orbit \(\mathcal I_X\) of primitive isometric embeddings
\begin{equation*} i_X:H^2(X,\Z)\hookrightarrow\widetilde\Lambda. \end{equation*}
Its orthogonal complement is generated by a primitive vector \(v_X\) of square \(2n-2\text{.}\) For two such manifolds \(X,Y\) and an integral isometry \(g:H^2(X,\Z)\to H^2(Y,\Z)\text{,}\) the map \(g\) is parallel transport if and only if it preserves positive orientation and
\begin{equation} \mathcal I_X=\mathcal I_Y\circ g.\tag{9.3} \end{equation}
The equality means that, for representatives \(i_X,i_Y\text{,}\) there exists \(\widetilde g\in O(\widetilde\Lambda)\) with \(\widetilde g\circ i_X=i_Y\circ g\text{.}\)
