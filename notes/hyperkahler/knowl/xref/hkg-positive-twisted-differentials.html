Lemma 10.9.

Let \(m>0\) and \(0\leq p\leq n\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} H^a(\PP^n,\Omega_{\PP^n}^p(m))=0\quad(a>0), \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} h^0(\PP^n,\Omega_{\PP^n}^p(m))=\binom{m-1}{p}\binom{m+n-p}{n-p}. \end{equation*}
Here \(\binom{m-1}{p}=0\) for \(p>m-1\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \(V=\C^{n+1}\text{.}\) Exterior powers of the Euler sequence give an exact complex
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow\Omega^p(m)\longrightarrow {\textstyle\bigwedge^p}V^*\otimes\cO(m-p)\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow\cO(m)\longrightarrow0. \end{equation*}
Every line-bundle twist in this complex is at least \(1-n\text{.}\) Its higher cohomology therefore vanishes, by the line-bundle cohomology of projective space, Stacks, Tag 01XT. Thus the cohomology of \(\Omega^p(m)\) is computed by the complex of global sections of the displayed terms.
🔗
Let \(x_0,\ldots,x_n\) be a basis of \(V^*\text{,}\) also regarded as polynomial coordinates. The global-section complex is the truncation of the Koszul complex in total degree \(m\text{,}\) where total degree is polynomial degree plus exterior degree. Its differential is \(d=\sum_j x_j\iota_j\text{,}\) with \(\iota_j\) contraction of the exterior factor by the dual basis vector. The operator \(s=\sum_j(x_j\wedge-)\partial/\partial x_j\) satisfies \(ds+sd=m\id\) on total degree \(m\text{:}\) the two terms sum to polynomial degree plus exterior degree. Since \(m>0\text{,}\) the full complex is exact. Truncation can leave cohomology only at its first term. This proves the higher-cohomology vanishing; it also gives \(H^0=0\) when \(m\leq p\text{,}\) since that first term is zero or has zero kernel.
🔗
For the dimension, the short exact sequence obtained from the same Euler complex is
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow\Omega^p(m)\longrightarrow {\textstyle\bigwedge^p}V^*\otimes\cO(m-p)\longrightarrow\Omega^{p-1}(m)\longrightarrow0. \end{equation*}
With \(a_p=h^0(\Omega^p(m))\) and the vanishing just proved, it yields
\begin{equation*} a_p=\binom{n+1}{p}\binom{n+m-p}{n}-a_{p-1},\qquad a_0=\binom{n+m}{n}. \end{equation*}
A binomial coefficient for a negative line-bundle degree is read as zero in this recurrence. Substituting the stated formula verifies the recurrence and initial value, proving the formula by induction on \(p\text{.}\)
