Theorem 15.23 (Schmid).

There is a limiting Hodge filtration \(F^\bullet_{\mathrm{lim}}\) for which \((H^d(X,\Q),W_\bullet,F^\bullet_{\mathrm{lim}})\) is a mixed Hodge structure. This means that the induced Hodge filtration on \(\operatorname{Gr}_j^WH^d\) defines a pure rational Hodge structure of weight \(j\text{.}\) The operator \(N_d\) lowers both Hodge indices by one and lowers weight by two.
