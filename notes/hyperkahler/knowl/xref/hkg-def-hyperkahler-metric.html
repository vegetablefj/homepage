Definition 1.3.

A hyperkähler structure on a smooth manifold \(M\) is a Riemannian metric \(g\) with orthogonal endomorphisms \(I,J,K\) of \(TM\) satisfying the quaternion relations and
\begin{equation*} \nabla I=\nabla J=\nabla K=0, \end{equation*}
where \(\nabla\) is the Levi-Civita connection. A metric admitting such a triple is called a hyperkähler metric. Compactness and irreducibility are not part of this definition.
