Theorem 9.25 (Minamide–Yanagida–Yoshioka).

Let \(v\in N(A)\) be primitive with \(v^2=2m\geq6\text{,}\) and let \(\sigma\in\operatorname{Stab}^{\dagger}(A)\) be \(v\)-generic. The moduli space \(M_\sigma(v)\) is nonempty, smooth, and projective of dimension \(2m+2\text{.}\) The map \(a_\sigma:M_\sigma(v)\to A\times\widehat A\) is a surjective Albanese morphism and an étale locally trivial fibration. Its fibre
\begin{equation*} K_\sigma(v):=a_\sigma^{-1}(0) \end{equation*}
is an IHS manifold of dimension \(2m-2\text{,}\) deformation equivalent to \(K_{m-1}(A)\text{.}\) The Mukai homomorphism, followed by restriction to the fibre, is an integral Hodge isometry
\begin{equation} \theta_{\sigma,v}:v^\perp\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }H^2(K_\sigma(v),\Z),\tag{9.5} \end{equation}
with the Mukai pairing on the left and \(q_{K_\sigma(v)}\) on the right.
