Proposition 13.3.

If \(X\) is a projective IHS manifold, the cycle class map is injective on \(\operatorname{CH}^1(X)_{\mathbb Q}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a smooth variety, Cartier divisors modulo rational equivalence identify \(\operatorname{CH}^1(X)\) with \(\operatorname{Pic}(X)\text{.}\) The exponential sequence shows that the kernel of \(c_1:\operatorname{Pic}(X)\to H^2(X,\mathbb Z)\) is the image of \(H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)\text{.}\) This group vanishes for an IHS manifold. Passing to rational coefficients preserves injectivity, since \(\mathbb Q\) is flat over \(\mathbb Z\text{.}\)
