Theorem 15.26.

The perverse filtration of \(f\text{,}\) normalized to range from zero to \(2n\text{,}\) is characterized by
\begin{equation} P_iH^d(X,\Q)=\mathcal W^\beta_{i-d+2n}\cap H^d(X,\Q).\tag{15.6} \end{equation}
It is independent of the chosen ample class on the base. Thus \(P_i=0\) for \(i<0\) and \(P_i=H^*\) for \(i\geq2n\text{.}\)
