Lemma 1.5.

Let \(A\) be an orthogonal endomorphism of a Riemannian tangent bundle with \(A^2=-\operatorname{id}\) and \(\nabla A=0\text{.}\) Then \(A\) is an integrable complex structure, and \(g\) is Kähler for \(A\text{,}\) with Kähler form \(\omega_A(u,v)=g(Au,v)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The Levi-Civita connection is torsion free, so \([U,V]=\nabla_UV-\nabla_VU\text{.}\) Substitute this into the Nijenhuis tensor
\begin{equation*} N_A(U,V)=[AU,AV]-A[AU,V]-A[U,AV]-[U,V]. \end{equation*}
Move each \(A\) through \(\nabla\) using \(\nabla A=0\text{.}\) The terms cancel in pairs. Hence \(N_A=0\text{,}\) and the Newlander–Nirenberg integrability theorem gives complex coordinates for \(A\text{;}\) see [NN57].
🔗
The form \(\omega_A\) is parallel because \(g\) and \(A\) are parallel. For a torsion-free connection, exterior differentiation is the alternation of covariant differentiation, so \(d\omega_A=0\text{.}\) Orthogonality gives type \((1,1)\text{,}\) and \(\omega_A(u,Au)=g(Au,Au)=g(u,u)>0\) for \(u\ne0\text{.}\) These are the Kähler conditions.
