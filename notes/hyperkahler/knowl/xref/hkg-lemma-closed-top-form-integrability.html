Lemma 3.3.

Let \(A\) be an almost complex structure on a smooth manifold of real dimension \(2d\text{.}\) If every point has a neighborhood with a nowhere vanishing closed form \(\Psi\) of type \((d,0)\text{,}\) then \(A\) is integrable.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The subbundle \(T^{0,1}\) consists precisely of the complexified tangent vectors \(U\) satisfying \(\iota_U\Psi=0\text{.}\) For local sections \(U,V\) of this subbundle, Cartan’s formula gives
\begin{equation*} \iota_{[U,V]}\Psi=\mathcal L_U(\iota_V\Psi)-\iota_V(\mathcal L_U\Psi)=0, \end{equation*}
because \(\mathcal L_U\Psi=\iota_Ud\Psi+d(\iota_U\Psi)=0\text{.}\) Thus \(T^{0,1}\) is closed under the Lie bracket. The Newlander–Nirenberg theorem gives integrability.
