Definition 5.2 Projective and injective resolutions.

Let \(\mathcal A\) be abelian and let \(A\in\mathcal A\text{.}\)
  1. A projective resolution is a quasi-isomorphism \(\epsilon:P\to A[0]\text{,}\) where every \(P^n\) is projective and \(P^n=0\) for \(n>0\text{.}\) Thus \(\cdots\to P^{-1}\to P^0\to A\to0\) is exact.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. An injective resolution is a quasi-isomorphism \(\eta:A[0]\to I\text{,}\) where every \(I^n\) is injective and \(I^n=0\) for \(n<0\text{.}\) Thus \(0\to A\to I^0\to I^1\to\cdots\) is exact.
