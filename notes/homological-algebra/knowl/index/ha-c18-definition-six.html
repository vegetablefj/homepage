Definition 18.2 Six-operation data on \((\mathcal B,E)\).

In addition to Definition 18.1, require the following data.
🔗
  1. For every \(f:X\to Y\) in \(E\text{,}\) an exact adjunction \(f_!:\mathcal D(X)\rightleftarrows\mathcal D(Y):f^!\text{.}\) The functors \(f_!\) have composition isomorphisms \(g_!f_!\simeq(gf)_!\) and identity isomorphisms satisfying associativity and units. Those for \(f^!\) are their mates.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For every Cartesian square displayed above with \(f\in E\text{,}\) a natural isomorphism \(\beta_\square:g^*f_!\xrightarrow{\sim}f'_!g'^*\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. For \(A\in\mathcal D(Y)\) and \(M\in\mathcal D(X)\text{,}\) a natural isomorphism \(\pi^!_{f,A,M}:A\otimes_Y f_!M\xrightarrow{\sim}f_!(f^*A\otimes_X M)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
These isomorphisms must respect the specified suspensions and satisfy the following compatibilities; every comparison is taken with the specified composition, unit, and monoidal isomorphisms.
🔗
  1. For an identity square, \(\beta\) is the identity comparison. For a horizontal or vertical pasting of Cartesian squares, applying the two exchange isomorphisms successively equals the exchange isomorphism of the outer square. Vertical pasting is required when both vertical arrows belong to \(E\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For a fixed \(f\text{,}\) the projection isomorphisms satisfy the unit and tensor-associativity conditions of Chapter 17. For composable \(f,g\in E\text{,}\) the projection isomorphism for \(gf\) equals the composite obtained by first moving the tensor factor through \(g_!\) and then through \(f_!\text{.}\) For an identity arrow it is the identity comparison.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. In a Cartesian square, the following two maps from \(g^*A\otimes_{Y'}g^*f_!M\) to \(f'_!(f'^*g^*A\otimes_{X'}g'^*M)\) agree. One applies \(g^*\pi^!_f\) followed by \(\beta\text{,}\) using \(g'^*f^*\simeq f'^*g^*\text{.}\) The other applies \(\id\otimes\beta\) followed by \(\pi^!_{f'}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
