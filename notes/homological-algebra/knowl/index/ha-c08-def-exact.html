Definition 8.3 Exact and cohomological functors.

  1. An exact functor \(F:\mathcal T\to\mathcal S\) is additive, has a specified natural isomorphism \(F(X[1])\simeq F(X)[1]\text{,}\) and takes distinguished triangles to distinguished triangles using this isomorphism on the last map.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. An additive functor \(H:\mathcal T\to\mathcal A\) to an abelian category is cohomological if each distinguished triangle induces an exact sequence \(H(X)\to H(Y)\to H(Z)\text{.}\) Rotation then gives its long exact sequence.
