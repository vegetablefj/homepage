Definition 18.7 Localization for a decomposition.

Require that \(i_*,j_!,j_*\) be fully faithful, that \(j^*i_*=0\text{,}\) and that the adjunction maps extend to functorial distinguished triangles
\begin{equation*} j_!j^*M\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow i_*i^*M\longrightarrow(j_!j^*M)[1] \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} i_*i^!M\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow j_*j^*M\longrightarrow(i_*i^!M)[1]. \end{equation*}
If decompositions are to vary under base change, require their pullbacks to be designated decompositions. Compatibility of localization triangles means compatibility with the comparisons constructed from the adjunctions and exchange maps; it does not require every component of those comparison morphisms to be invertible.
🔗
For the tensor compatibility used below, require also that the counit \(q_A:j_!j^*A\to A\) satisfy \(q_A\pi^!_{j,A,\mathbf1_U}=\id_A\otimes q_{\mathbf1_X}\text{,}\) after the unit identifications. This specifies compatibility of the localization map with the exceptional tensor action.
