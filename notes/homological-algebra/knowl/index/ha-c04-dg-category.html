Definition 4.6 Differential graded categories and their degree-zero categories.

A differential graded category, or dg category, over \(\Z\) consists of objects, a complex of abelian groups \(\mathcal C(X,Y)\) for each pair, associative composition maps of complexes
\begin{equation*} \mathcal C(Y,Z)\otimes_{\Z}\mathcal C(X,Y)\longrightarrow\mathcal C(X,Z), \end{equation*}
and identity elements that are degree-zero cycles. Equivalently it is a category enriched over \(\Ch(\Ab)\) with its tensor product and unit \(\Z[0]\text{.}\) Its category \(Z^0\mathcal C\) has degree-zero cycles as arrows; its category \(H^0\mathcal C\) has degree-zero cohomology classes as arrows. The composition formula in Proposition 4.5 makes both compositions well-defined.
