Definition 2.11 Serre and localizing subcategories.

A Serre subcategory \(\mathcal S\subseteq\mathcal A\) is a full subcategory closed under isomorphisms, containing zero, and closed under subobjects, quotient objects, and extensions. When \(\mathcal A\) is Grothendieck, we call \(\mathcal S\) localizing if it is also closed under small coproducts.
