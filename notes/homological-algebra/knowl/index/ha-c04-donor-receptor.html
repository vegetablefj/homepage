Definition 4.20 Donors, receptors, and their maps.

Let \(C\) be a commuting double complex in an abelian category. At \(A=C^{p,q}\) write \(h:A\to C^{p+1,q}\) and \(v:A\to C^{p,q+1}\) for the outgoing maps, and \(h_-:C^{p-1,q}\to A\text{,}\) \(v_-:C^{p,q-1}\to A\) for the incoming maps. Let \(a:C^{p-1,q-1}\to A\) and \(b:A\to C^{p+1,q+1}\) be the diagonal composites. Define
\begin{gather*} H_h(A)=\ker h/\im h_-,\qquad H_v(A)=\ker v/\im v_-,\\ R(A)=(\ker h\cap\ker v)/\im a,\\ D(A)=\ker b/(\im h_-+\im v_-). \end{gather*}
The last two objects are the receptor and donor at \(A\text{.}\) Identity on the relevant subobjects of \(A\) induces maps \(R(A)\to H_h(A)\to D(A)\) and \(R(A)\to H_v(A)\to D(A)\text{.}\) They form a commuting diamond. Every horizontal or vertical differential \(u:A\to B\) induces a map \(\overline u:D(A)\to R(B)\text{.}\)
