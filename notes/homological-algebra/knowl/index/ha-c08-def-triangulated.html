Definition 8.2 Triangulated categories.

A triangulated structure on \(\mathcal T\) specifies distinguished triangles with the following properties.
  1. The class is closed under isomorphism. The triangle \(X\xrightarrow{\id}X\to0\to X[1]\) is distinguished, and every map is the first map of a distinguished triangle.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. A triangle \((u,v,w)\) is distinguished if and only if its rotation \((v,w,-u[1])\) is distinguished.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. A commutative square on the first two objects of distinguished triangles extends to a morphism of triangles.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. For composable maps \(X\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{g}Z\text{,}\) choose distinguished triangles with third objects \(C_f,C_{gf},C_g\text{.}\) Write their second and third maps as \(i_f,p_f\text{,}\) \(i_{gf},p_{gf}\text{,}\) and \(i_g,p_g\text{.}\) There exist maps \(a:C_f\to C_{gf}\) and \(b:C_{gf}\to C_g\) such that \((\id_X,g,a)\) and \((f,\id_Z,b)\) are morphisms of the chosen triangles and
    \begin{equation*} C_f\xrightarrow{a}C_{gf}\xrightarrow{b}C_g\xrightarrow{i_f[1]p_g}C_f[1] \end{equation*}
    is distinguished.
    🔗
    🔗
