Definition A.6 Quasi-abelian categories.

A preabelian category is quasi-abelian if every pullback of a strict epimorphism is a strict epimorphism, and every pushout of a strict monomorphism is a strict monomorphism. A strict short exact sequence is a kernel–cokernel pair, written \(0\to A\to B\to C\to0\text{.}\)
