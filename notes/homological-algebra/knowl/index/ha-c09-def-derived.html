Definition 9.1 Derived categories and boundedness.

Let \(\mathcal A\) be abelian. Write \(\operatorname{Ac}(\mathcal A)\) for the strictly full subcategory of acyclic complexes in \(\K(\mathcal A)\text{.}\) The derived category is
\begin{equation*} \D(\mathcal A)=\K(\mathcal A)/\operatorname{Ac}(\mathcal A). \end{equation*}
Its full subcategories \(\D^+(\mathcal A)\text{,}\) \(\D^-(\mathcal A)\text{,}\) and \(\D^b(\mathcal A)\) consist respectively of objects with cohomology bounded below, above, and on both sides.
