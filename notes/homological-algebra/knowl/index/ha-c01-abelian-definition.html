Definition 1.5 Abelian categories and exact sequences.

Let \(\mathcal A\) be additive.
🔗
  1. A kernel of \(f:X\to Y\) represents morphisms into \(X\) annihilated by \(f\text{;}\) a cokernel represents morphisms out of \(Y\) annihilating \(f\text{.}\) When they exist, set \(\im(f)=\ker(\coker f)\) and \(\operatorname{coim}(f)=\coker(\ker f)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The category \(\mathcal A\) is abelian if all kernels and cokernels exist and the canonical morphism \(\operatorname{coim}(f)\to\im(f)\) is an isomorphism for every \(f\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. In an abelian category, a sequence \(X\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{g}Z\) is exact at \(Y\) if \(\im(f)=\ker(g)\) as subobjects of \(Y\text{.}\) A short exact sequence is an exact sequence \(0\to X\to Y\to Z\to0\text{.}\)
