Definition 13.1 t-structures and boundedness.

A t-structure on a triangulated category \(\mathcal T\) is a pair of strictly full subcategories \((\mathcal T^{\leq0},\mathcal T^{\geq0})\) such that:
🔗
  1. \(\mathcal T^{\leq0}[1]\subseteq\mathcal T^{\leq0}\) and \(\mathcal T^{\geq0}[-1]\subseteq\mathcal T^{\geq0}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\Hom(U,V)=0\) for \(U\in\mathcal T^{\leq0}\) and \(V\in\mathcal T^{\geq1}\text{,}\) where \(\mathcal T^{\geq1}=\mathcal T^{\geq0}[-1]\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Every \(X\) occurs in a distinguished triangle \(U\to X\to V\to U[1]\) with \(U\in\mathcal T^{\leq0}\) and \(V\in\mathcal T^{\geq1}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Set \(\mathcal T^{\leq n}=\mathcal T^{\leq0}[-n]\text{,}\) \(\mathcal T^{\geq n}=\mathcal T^{\geq0}[-n]\text{,}\) and \(\mathcal T^{[a,b]}=\mathcal T^{\geq a}\cap\mathcal T^{\leq b}\text{.}\) The heart is \(\mathcal A=\mathcal T^{\leq0}\cap\mathcal T^{\geq0}\text{.}\) The t-structure is bounded if every object belongs to some \(\mathcal T^{[a,b]}\text{.}\) It is nondegenerate if both \(\bigcap_n\mathcal T^{\leq n}\) and \(\bigcap_n\mathcal T^{\geq n}\) contain only zero objects.
