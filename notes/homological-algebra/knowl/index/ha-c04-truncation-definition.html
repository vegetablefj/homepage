Definition 4.16 Brutal and canonical truncation.

Let \(X\) be a complex in an abelian category and let \(n\in\Z\text{.}\) The brutal truncations \(\sigma_{\leq n}X\) and \(\sigma_{\geq n}X\) retain the terms in the indicated degrees and replace all other terms by zero. The canonical, or smart, truncations are
\begin{equation*} (\tau_{\leq n}X)^i=\begin{cases}X^i&i<n,\\ \ker d_X^n&i=n,\\0&i>n,\end{cases}\qquad (\tau_{\geq n}X)^i=\begin{cases}0&i<n,\\ \coker d_X^{n-1}&i=n,\\X^i&i>n.\end{cases} \end{equation*}
Their differentials are induced by those of \(X\text{.}\)
