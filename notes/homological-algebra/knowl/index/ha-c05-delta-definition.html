Definition 5.13 Cohomological delta-functors.

A cohomological \(\delta\)-functor \(T\) consists of additive functors \(T^n:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B\) for \(n\geq0\) and connecting maps \(T^n(A'')\to T^{n+1}(A')\) for short exact sequences, natural under morphisms of those sequences, giving long exact sequences beginning with \(0\to T^0(A')\to T^0(A)\to T^0(A'')\text{.}\) A morphism \(T\to S\) is a family of natural transformations \(T^n\to S^n\) commuting with all connecting maps. It is universal if every natural transformation \(T^0\to S^0\) extends uniquely to such a morphism. A functor \(G:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B\) is effaceable if, for every \(A\text{,}\) some monomorphism \(A\to B\) induces the zero map \(G(A)\to G(B)\text{.}\)
