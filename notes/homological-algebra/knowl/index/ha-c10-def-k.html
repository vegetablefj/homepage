Definition 10.1 K-injective and K-projective complexes.

A complex \(I\) is K-injective if \(\Hom_{\K(\mathcal A)}(N,I)=0\) for every acyclic complex \(N\text{.}\) A complex \(P\) is K-projective if \(\Hom_{\K(\mathcal A)}(P,N)=0\) for every acyclic \(N\text{.}\) Equivalently, the complexes \(\underline{\Hom}^{\bullet}(N,I)\) and \(\underline{\Hom}^{\bullet}(P,N)\text{,}\) respectively, are acyclic for all such \(N\text{.}\) A K-injective replacement is a quasi-isomorphism \(X\to I\) with \(I\) K-injective; a K-projective replacement is a quasi-isomorphism \(P\to X\) with \(P\) K-projective.
