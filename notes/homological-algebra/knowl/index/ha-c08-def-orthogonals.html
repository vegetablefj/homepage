Definition 8.14 Orthogonal subcategories.

For a triangulated subcategory \(\mathcal N\text{,}\) its right orthogonal \(\mathcal N^\perp\) consists of objects \(I\) such that \(\Hom(N,I[n])=0\) for every \(N\in\mathcal N\) and \(n\in\Z\text{.}\) Its left orthogonal \({}^{\perp}\mathcal N\) consists of \(P\) such that \(\Hom(P,N[n])=0\) for those objects and shifts. Both are thick in \(\mathcal T\text{.}\)
