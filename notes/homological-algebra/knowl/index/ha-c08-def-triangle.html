Definition 8.1 Triangles and their morphisms.

Let \(\mathcal T\) be additive with an additive autoequivalence \([1]\text{.}\) A triangle is a diagram \(X\xrightarrow{u}Y\xrightarrow{v}Z\xrightarrow{w}X[1]\text{.}\) A morphism to a triangle \(X'\xrightarrow{u'}Y'\xrightarrow{v'}Z'\xrightarrow{w'}X'[1]\) is a triple \((a,b,c)\) satisfying \(bu=u'a\text{,}\) \(cv=v'b\text{,}\) and \(a[1]w=w'c\text{.}\)
