Definition 5.6 Syzygies and cosyzygies.

For a projective resolution \(P\to A\text{,}\) set \(\Omega^0A=A\) and define \(\Omega^{j+1}A\) by the short exact sequences \(0\to\Omega^{j+1}A\to P^{-j}\to\Omega^jA\to0\text{.}\) For an injective resolution \(A\to I\text{,}\) set \(K^0A=A\) and use \(0\to K^jA\to I^j\to K^{j+1}A\to0\text{.}\) These objects depend on the resolution; we use syzygy and cosyzygy with that choice understood.
