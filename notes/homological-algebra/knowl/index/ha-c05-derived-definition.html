Definition 5.9 Object-valued derived functors.

Let \(F:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B\) be additive, with \(\mathcal A,\mathcal B\) abelian.
  1. If \(\mathcal A\) has enough injectives, choose an injective resolution \(A\to I_A\) and put \(R^nF(A)=H^n(FI_A)\) for \(n\geq0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(\mathcal A\) has enough projectives, choose a projective resolution \(P_A\to A\) and put \(L_nF(A)=H^{-n}(FP_A)\) for \(n\geq0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
Morphisms are defined by lifting to resolutions and passing to cohomology.
