Definition 4.9 Stalk and disk complexes.

Write \(S^n(A)=A[-n]\) for \(A\) in degree \(n\) with all other terms zero. The disk complex \(D^n(A)\) has \(A\) in degrees \(n,n+1\text{,}\) identity differential between them, and zero elsewhere. It is contractible. There are natural bijections
\begin{gather*} \Hom_{\Ch(\mathcal A)}(D^n(A),X)\simeq\Hom_{\mathcal A}(A,X^n),\\ \Hom_{\Ch(\mathcal A)}(X,D^n(A))\simeq\Hom_{\mathcal A}(X^{n+1},A). \end{gather*}
