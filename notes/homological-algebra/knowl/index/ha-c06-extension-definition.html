Definition 6.3 Yoneda extensions.

For \(n\geq1\text{,}\) an \(n\)-extension of \(A\) by \(B\) is an exact sequence
\begin{equation*} 0\to B\to E_{n-1}\to\cdots\to E_0\to A\to0. \end{equation*}
Equivalence is the relation generated by morphisms of these sequences which are the identity on the two endpoints. For \(n=1\text{,}\) such a morphism is an isomorphism. The Baer sum is obtained by taking the direct sum of extensions, pulling back along the diagonal of \(A\text{,}\) and pushing out along the addition map of \(B\text{.}\) Splicing extensions gives the Yoneda product
\begin{equation*} \Ext^p(B,C)\times\Ext^q(A,B)\longrightarrow\Ext^{p+q}(A,C). \end{equation*}
