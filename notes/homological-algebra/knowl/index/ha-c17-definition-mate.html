Definition 17.1 Mates.

The mate of \(\alpha:F'H\to KF\) is the transformation
\begin{equation*} \alpha^\flat:HG\xrightarrow{\eta'}G'F'HG\xrightarrow{G'\alpha G}G'KFG\xrightarrow{G'K\epsilon}G'K. \end{equation*}
Conversely, the mate of \(\beta:HG\to G'K\) is
\begin{equation*} \beta^\sharp:F'H\xrightarrow{F'H\eta}F'HGF\xrightarrow{F'\beta F}F'G'KF\xrightarrow{\epsilon'}KF. \end{equation*}
