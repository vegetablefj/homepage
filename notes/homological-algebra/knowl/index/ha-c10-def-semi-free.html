Definition 10.13 Semi-free complexes.

A semi-free complex of \(R\)-modules is a complex with an exhaustive filtration \(0=P_{-1}\subset P_0\subset P_1\subset\cdots\) by subcomplexes such that the inclusions split as maps of graded modules and each quotient \(P_r/P_{r-1}\) is a direct sum of shifts of \(R\) with zero differential. Equivalently, a graded basis can be assigned stages so that the differential of each basis vector is a finite combination of vectors from earlier stages.
