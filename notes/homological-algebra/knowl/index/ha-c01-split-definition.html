Definition 1.10 Split sequences and idempotents.

A sequence \(0\to X\xrightarrow{i}Y\xrightarrow{p}Z\to0\) in an additive category is split exact if it is isomorphic to the biproduct sequence. An idempotent \(e:Y\to Y\) splits if there are \(j:X\to Y\) and \(r:Y\to X\) with \(rj=\id_X\) and \(jr=e\text{.}\)
