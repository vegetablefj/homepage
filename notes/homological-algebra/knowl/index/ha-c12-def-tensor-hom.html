Definition 12.6 Derived tensor and derived Hom.

  1. For a complex \(M\) of right \(R\)-modules and a complex \(N\) of left \(R\)-modules, choose K-flat replacements \(P\to M\) and \(Q\to N\text{.}\) Their derived tensor product is the object \(M\Ltensor_R N=P\otimes_R Q\) of \(\D(\Ab)\text{.}\) A specified central base ring can be retained in the target.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For complexes \(M,N\) of left \(R\)-modules choose a K-projective replacement \(P\to M\) and a K-injective replacement \(N\to I\text{.}\) Define \(\RHom_R(M,N)\) by the complex \(\underline{\Hom}_R^{\bullet}(P,I)\text{,}\) viewed in \(\D(\Ab)\text{.}\)
