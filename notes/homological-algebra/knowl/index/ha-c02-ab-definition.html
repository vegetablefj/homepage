Definition 2.1 Grothendieck’s conditions.

Let \(\mathcal A\) be an abelian category. All indexing sets and indexing categories in the following conditions are small.
🔗
  1. AB3 means that all coproducts exist. AB4 means AB3 and exactness of coproducts. AB5 means AB3 and exactness of filtered colimits.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. AB3* means that all products exist. AB4* means AB3* and exactness of products.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. An object \(G\) is a generator if \(\Hom_{\mathcal A}(G,-)\) is faithful. A Grothendieck category is an abelian category satisfying AB5 and having a generator.
    🔗
    🔗
