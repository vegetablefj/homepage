Definition 10.10 K-flat complexes.

A complex \(F\) of left \(R\)-modules is K-flat if \(N\otimes_R F\) is acyclic for every acyclic complex \(N\) of right \(R\)-modules. Use direct-sum totalization in this tensor product. The definition for right modules is obtained by reversing the sides.
