Proposition 2.3.

AB5 implies AB4. If \((X_i)\) is a filtered diagram in an AB5 category and \(N\subseteq X=\colim_iX_i\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} N\simeq\colim_i(X_i\times_XN). \end{equation*}
In particular, a directed union of subobjects is a subobject, and taking that union commutes with intersection with a fixed subobject.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
A coproduct is the filtered colimit of its finite partial biproducts. Finite biproducts are exact, so AB5 proves AB4. For the second assertion, apply exactness of the filtered colimit to the kernels defining \(X_i\times_XN\) inside \(X_i\oplus N\text{.}\) The colimit of the constant diagram \(N\) is \(N\text{,}\) since a filtered category is nonempty and connected. The resulting kernel is the pullback of \(\id_X\) along \(N\to X\text{,}\) hence is \(N\text{.}\)
