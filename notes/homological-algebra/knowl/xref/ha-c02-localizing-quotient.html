Theorem 2.13.

For a Serre subcategory \(\mathcal S\) of a Grothendieck category, closure under coproducts is equivalent to the existence of a right adjoint \(S\) to \(Q\text{.}\) In that case \(\mathcal A/\mathcal S\) is Grothendieck, \(S\) is fully faithful and left exact, and the counit \(QS\to\id\) is an isomorphism. The kernel and cokernel of the unit \(X\to SQX\) belong to \(\mathcal S\text{.}\)
