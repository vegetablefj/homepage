Proposition 4.5.

The Hom differential has square zero. Composition of homogeneous families satisfies
\begin{equation*} d(vu)=d(v)u+(-1)^s v\,d(u),\qquad |v|=s. \end{equation*}
Consequently homotopy is compatible with composition and addition, and \(\K(\mathcal A)\) is additive. The shift of a homotopy \(h\) between \(f\) and \(g\) is the homotopy \((-1)^r h\) between \(f[r]\) and \(g[r]\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For \(|u|=r\text{,}\) expansion of \(d^2(u)\) leaves two mixed terms with coefficients \(-(-1)^r\) and \(-(-1)^{r+1}\text{;}\) they cancel, and the remaining terms contain the square of a differential. Expanding the right side of the composition formula gives \(d_Zvu-(-1)^{r+s}vu d_X\text{.}\) Boundaries therefore form an ideal among degree-zero cocycles. The shift assertion follows from \(d_{X[r]}=(-1)^r d_X\text{.}\)
