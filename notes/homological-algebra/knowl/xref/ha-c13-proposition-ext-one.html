Proposition 13.6.

For \(A,C\in\mathcal A\text{,}\) there is a natural isomorphism
🔗
\begin{equation*} \Ext^1_{\mathcal A}(C,A)\simeq\Hom_{\mathcal T}(C,A[1]), \end{equation*}
🔗
where the left side denotes Yoneda extensions.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
An extension gives the connecting map of its distinguished triangle. Conversely, complete a morphism \(C\to A[1]\) to a triangle \(A\to B\to C\to A[1]\text{.}\) Extension-closure places \(B\) in the heart, and its cohomology sequence is a short exact sequence with ends \(A,C\text{.}\) The axiom on morphisms of triangles identifies equivalent extensions. Pullback and pushout of extensions correspond to composition at the two ends of the connecting map. This also identifies Baer addition with addition of morphisms.
