Proposition 5.8.

Given a short exact sequence \(0\to A'\to A\to A''\to0\) and projective resolutions \(P'\to A'\text{,}\) \(P''\to A''\text{,}\) there is a projective resolution \(P\to A\) fitting into a degreewise split short exact sequence \(0\to P'\to P\to P''\to0\) compatible with the augmentations. One may take \(P^n=P'^n\oplus P''^n\text{.}\) The dual assertion holds for injective resolutions.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Lift \(P''^0\to A''\) to \(A\) and combine it with \(P'^0\to A'\to A\text{.}\) The resulting map \(P'^0\oplus P''^0\to A\) is an epimorphism. The snake lemma gives a short exact sequence of its kernel between the kernels of the two given augmentations. Repeat the same construction with these kernels, then continue in decreasing degrees. The resulting maps have square zero because each factors through the preceding kernel. The inclusions and projections give the asserted split sequence in each degree.
