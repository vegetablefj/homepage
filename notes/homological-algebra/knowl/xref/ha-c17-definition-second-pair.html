Definition 17.5 Fixed-functor duality data.

In addition to \(f^*\dashv f_*\text{,}\) suppose that \(f_!:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D\) has a right adjoint \(f^!:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C\text{.}\) We specify a natural isomorphism
🔗
\begin{equation*} \pi^!_{A,M}:A\otimes f_!M\xrightarrow{\sim}f_!(f^*A\otimes M). \end{equation*}
🔗
It must respect the unit and associativity of the tensor action. Explicitly, start with \(A\otimes(B\otimes f_!M)\text{.}\) Apply \(\id_A\otimes\pi^!_{B,M}\) and then \(\pi^!_{A,f^*B\otimes M}\text{.}\) After the monoidal identifications, this composite must agree with \(\pi^!_{A\otimes B,M}\text{.}\) Its unit instance is the unit isomorphism. When the categories are triangulated, exact fixed-functor data also require these comparisons to respect the specified suspension isomorphisms in both variables.
