Proposition 1.22.

Let \(\mathcal A\) be a locally small abelian category. Every finite diagram in \(\mathcal A\) is contained in an essentially small full abelian subcategory whose inclusion is exact. One may include any specified finite collection of its kernels, cokernels, and image factorizations. Consequently a finite diagram argument involving these constructions and their exactness can be checked after a fully faithful exact embedding of that subcategory into a module category.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Start with the objects of the diagram and the specified additional objects. Add a zero object and representatives of all finite biproducts, kernels, and cokernels of maps between the objects already chosen. Repeat this operation countably many times and take the full subcategory on the resulting objects. At each stage there is only a small set of maps by local smallness, so the union has a small set of objects up to the chosen size convention. Every finite construction involving objects of the union occurs at a later stage. The subcategory is additive and closed under the ambient kernels and cokernels. Its coimage-to-image maps are the ambient isomorphisms, so it is abelian and its inclusion is exact. Apply Theorem 1.21 to a small equivalent category.
