For discriminant twenty-six, take a general three-nodal septic scroll \(S_{26}\subset\mathbb P^5\) in the family studied by Farkas and Verra. It is obtained by projecting a smooth rational normal scroll of degree seven in \(\mathbb P^8\) from a suitable plane, producing exactly three transverse double points. A general cubic of \(\mathcal C_{26}\) contains such a scroll [FV18, Theorem 1.2]. Its degree is seven and its self-intersection in the cubic is twenty-five, giving \(3\cdot25-7^2=26\text{.}\)
