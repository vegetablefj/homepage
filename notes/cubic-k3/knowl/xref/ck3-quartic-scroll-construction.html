Let \(\Sigma\subset\mathbb P^5\) be a smooth quartic rational normal scroll. Its ideal is generated by six quadrics. The linear system of these quadrics defines
\begin{gather*} \phi_\Sigma:\mathbb P^5\dashrightarrow Q^4\subset\mathbb P^5, \end{gather*}
where \(Q^4\) is a smooth quadric. A general fiber is a secant line of \(\Sigma\text{,}\) and a general point of \(\mathbb P^5\) lies on exactly one such line. These assertions hold for both smooth scroll types \(S(2,2)\) and \(S(1,3)\) [BRS19, Proposition 2.1].
