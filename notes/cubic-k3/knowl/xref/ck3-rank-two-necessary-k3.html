Corollary 8.7 Consequence of the OpenAI preprint.

Assume the integral decomposition in [OAI26, Theorem 3.3 and Lemma 3.8]. If a smooth complex cubic fourfold \(X\) is rational and \(\operatorname{rank}A_X=2\text{,}\) then \(d=\det A_X\) is admissible and \(\Ku(X)\simeq D^b(S)\) for some smooth projective K3 surface \(S\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose a smooth weak factorization of a birational map \(\mathbb P^4\dashrightarrow X\text{.}\) Its intermediate varieties are rationally connected. The cited integral decomposition expresses their transcendental lattices as orthogonal sums of entire transcendental lattices of surface centers, shifted by \((-1)\text{,}\) and identifies each summand removed by a blowdown. Each nonzero summand has a nonzero \((3,1)\) part. Since \(h^{3,1}(X)=1\text{,}\) exactly one summand survives at \(X\text{.}\) Thus, for a smooth projective surface center \(Z\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} T_X\simeq T_Z(-1) \end{equation*}
as integral Hodge lattices, with the negative of the surface cup product on the right. Here \(p_g(Z)=1\) and \(\operatorname{rank}T_Z=23-2=21\text{.}\)
🔗
By the first assertion of [OAI26, Lemma 3.9], \(Z\) is birational to a K3 surface of Picard rank one. Point blowups preserve its transcendental lattice. Addington’s integral criterion now gives an admissible label on \(X\) [Add16, Theorem 1]. Its only saturated rank-two label is \(A_X\text{,}\) so that label has discriminant \(d\text{.}\) The categorical conclusion follows from Theorem 7.3 and Section 7.5.
