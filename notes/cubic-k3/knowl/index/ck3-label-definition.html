Definition 5.1.

A cubic fourfold is special if \(\operatorname{rank}A_X\geq2\text{.}\) A labelled cubic is a pair \((X,K)\text{,}\) where \(K\subset A_X\) is a saturated rank-two sublattice containing \(h^2\text{.}\) Its discriminant is \(d=\det K\text{.}\) An isomorphism of labelled cubics is a projective isomorphism whose pullback sends one specified sublattice to the other.
🔗
The locus \(\mathcal C_d\subset\mathcal C\) consists of the unlabelled cubics \(X\) for which such a sublattice of discriminant \(d\) exists.
