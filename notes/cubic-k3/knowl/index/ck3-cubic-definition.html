Let \(V\) be a six-dimensional complex vector space. A cubic fourfold is a smooth hypersurface \(X=V(f)\subset\mathbb P(V)\) defined by a homogeneous cubic polynomial. Write \(h=c_1(\mathcal O_X(1))\text{.}\) In particular, \(\int_X h^4=3\text{,}\) since four general hyperplanes cut the cubic in three points.
