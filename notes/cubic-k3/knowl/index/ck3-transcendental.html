The integral Hodge lattice is \(A_X=L_X\cap H^{2,2}(X)\text{.}\) Its orthogonal complement \(T_X=A_X^\perp\subset L_X\) is the transcendental lattice. Since \(h^2\in A_X\text{,}\) one has \(T_X\subset L_X^0\text{.}\) If \(r=\operatorname{rank}A_X\text{,}\) then \(\operatorname{rank}T_X=23-r\text{.}\) When \(A_X=\mathbb Zh^2\text{,}\) primitive and transcendental cohomology coincide. Additional integral Hodge classes reduce the rank of \(T_X\text{,}\) while \(L_X^0\) still has rank twenty-two. The very general and special loci are described in Chapter 5.
