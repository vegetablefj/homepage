On the open subset of \(S^{[2]}\) parametrizing distinct pairs, take two points \(U_1,U_2\in S\) with direct sum \(A=U_1\oplus U_2\text{.}\) Restriction defines a map from \(W\) to alternating forms on \(A\text{.}\) Since these forms already vanish on each \(U_i\text{,}\) their restrictions lie in the four-dimensional space of cross terms. The kernel is generically two-dimensional. Its projectivization is a line in \(\mathbb P(W)\text{.}\) Every form in that pencil vanishes on the four-dimensional subspace \(A\text{.}\) A nondegenerate alternating form on a six-dimensional space has isotropic subspaces of dimension at most three, so every member of the pencil has rank at most four. The line is therefore contained in \(X\text{.}\)
