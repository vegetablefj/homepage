Let \(\mathcal H\) be an irreducible four-dimensional family of integral curves in \(\mathbb P^5\text{,}\) with universal family \(\mathcal U\) and dominant evaluation map \(\operatorname{ev}:\mathcal U\to\mathbb P^5\text{.}\) This is a congruence; its order is the degree of evaluation. An order-one congruence has birational evaluation, so there is an open set \(\Omega\subset\mathbb P^5\) over which a point determines a unique curve.
