Write \(L_X=H^4(X,\mathbb Z)\) and equip it with the cup-product pairing \((a,b)_X=\int_X ab\text{.}\) We write \(\oplus\) for orthogonal direct sums of lattices. The primitive lattice is
\begin{gather*} L_X^0=(h^2)^\perp=\{a\in L_X:(a,h^2)_X=0\}. \end{gather*}
Over \(\mathbb Q\) there is an orthogonal decomposition \(L_{X,\mathbb Q}=\mathbb Q h^2\oplus L_{X,\mathbb Q}^0\text{.}\) The projection of a class \(a\) to the primitive part is \(a-\frac{(a,h^2)_X}{3}h^2\text{.}\) The denominator explains why this rational decomposition need not be an integral direct sum.
