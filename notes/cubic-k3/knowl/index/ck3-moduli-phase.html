Fix a nonzero \(v\in N_X\text{,}\) a numerical Bridgeland stability condition \(\sigma=(Z,\mathcal P)\) on \(\Ku(X)\) with \(Z:N_X\to\C\text{,}\) and a real lift \(\phi\) with \(Z(v)\in\R_{>0}e^{i\pi\phi}\text{.}\) The notation \(M_\sigma(v,\phi)\) denotes the coarse moduli space of stable objects \(E\in\mathcal P(\phi)\) with \(\mathbf v(E)=v\text{,}\) when the moduli theorem supplies it. We suppress \(\phi\) in \(M_\sigma(v)\) after fixing this choice. For primitive \(v\text{,}\) the condition that \(\sigma\) is \(v\)-generic means that all semistable objects of this class and phase are stable. Such stability conditions exist in the component used in the theorem.
