For each of these three line bundles \(E\text{,}\) the left mutation used in the projection is given by the evaluation triangle
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{RHom}(E,F)\otimes E\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow L_E(F). \end{equation*}
Mutation removes the part detected by morphisms from \(E\text{.}\) Successive mutations give the projection
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{pr}=L_{\cO_X}\circ L_{\cO_X(1)}\circ L_{\cO_X(2)}:D^b(X)\longrightarrow\Ku(X). \end{equation*}
This projection is the left adjoint of the inclusion \(i:\Ku(X)\hookrightarrow D^b(X)\text{.}\) The adjunction and the triangles are explained in Appendix A.
