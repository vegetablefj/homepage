We use Springer’s theorem in the following form: over a field of characteristic different from two, a smooth projective quadric with a point over a finite extension of odd degree already has a point over the original field. Consequently a zero-cycle of odd degree suffices. A zero-cycle of odd degree contains a closed point of odd degree among its terms. For this fibration such a point is the generic point of an odd-degree multisection, obtained by closure. This is the quadratic-form input in Hassett’s criterion [Has99, Theorem 3.1].
