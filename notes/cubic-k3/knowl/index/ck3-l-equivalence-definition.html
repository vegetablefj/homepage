Let \(\mathbb L=[\mathbb A^1]\in K_0(\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb C})\text{.}\) We write \(X\sim_{\mathbb L}Y\) if
\begin{equation*} \mathbb L^n([X]-[Y])=0 \end{equation*}
for some integer \(n\geq0\) [MM26, Definition 1.1]. Meinsma and Moschetti propose the following conjecture [MM26, Conjecture 1.3], also formulated independently by Billi and Li Bassi [BLB26, Conjecture 1.6].
