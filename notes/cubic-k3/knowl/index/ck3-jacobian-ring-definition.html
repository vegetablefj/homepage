The Hodge numbers can be calculated from the defining equation. Let \(S=\mathbb C[x_0,\ldots,x_5]\) with its usual grading, and define the Jacobian ring
\begin{gather*} R_f=S/(\partial f/\partial x_0,\ldots,\partial f/\partial x_5). \end{gather*}
Smoothness says that the six partial derivatives have no common zero in projective space. Their affine common zero is therefore the origin. Since the polynomial ring is Cohen–Macaulay, these six quadrics form a regular sequence. The Hilbert series of the quotient is
\begin{gather*} \sum_{j\geq0}\dim(R_f)_j\,t^j=\frac{(1-t^2)^6}{(1-t)^6}=(1+t)^6. \end{gather*}
Every coefficient can now be read from the binomial expansion.
