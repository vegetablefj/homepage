Definition 5.3.

A polarized K3 surface \((S,\ell)\text{,}\) with \(\ell\) primitive and ample, is associated to the labelled cubic \((X,K_d)\) if \(\ell^2=d\) and there exists an integral Hodge isometry
\begin{gather*} K_d^\perp\simeq H^2(S,\mathbb Z)_{\mathrm{prim}}(-1). \end{gather*}
On the right the pairing is the negative of the K3 cup product. The primitive group is \(\ell^\perp\subset H^2(S,\mathbb Z)\text{.}\)
🔗
An unlabelled cubic admits an associated polarized K3 surface if there exist a label and a polarized K3 surface satisfying this condition [Has17, Section 3.2].
