The period of a cubic fourfold records its Hodge decomposition inside an integral lattice. A marking specifies that lattice identification. Fix a pair \((L,c)\) isometric to \((H^4(X,\mathbb Z),h^2)\) for one smooth cubic, with the cup-product form. Thus \(L\) has signature \((21,2)\text{,}\) \(c^2=3\text{,}\) and \(L_0=c^\perp\) has signature \((20,2)\text{.}\) A marking of another cubic is an integral isometry \(\mu:H^4(X,\mathbb Z)\to L\) sending \(h^2\) to \(c\text{.}\) Two marked cubics are equivalent when a projective isomorphism between them respects their markings on cohomology.
