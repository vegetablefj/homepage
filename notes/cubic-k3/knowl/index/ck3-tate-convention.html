The weight-two Hodge structure \(L_X^0(1)\) has Hodge numbers \((1,20,1)\text{.}\) In comparisons with weight-two polarized structures, we equip it with the negative of the cubic cup form, of signature \((2,20)\text{.}\) This pairing convention will also be used for the Abel–Jacobi and Mukai comparisons.
