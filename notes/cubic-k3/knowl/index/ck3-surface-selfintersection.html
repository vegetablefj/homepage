For any smooth surface \(S\subset X\text{,}\) let \(H=h|_S\) and let \(K_S\) be its canonical class. Expanding \(c(\mathcal T_X|_S)=(1+H)^6/(1+3H)\) gives \(c_1(\mathcal T_X|_S)=3H\) and \(c_2(\mathcal T_X|_S)=6H^2\text{.}\) The tangent and normal sequence gives the degree formula
\begin{gather*} [S]^2=\int_Sc_2(N_{S/X})=6H^2+3H\cdot K_S+K_S^2-c_2(S). \end{gather*}
This is the normal bundle calculation used for the later rationality constructions.
