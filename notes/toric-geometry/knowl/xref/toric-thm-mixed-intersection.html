Theorem 5.9 Intersection numbers and mixed volumes.

Let \(X\) be a smooth projective toric variety of dimension \(n\geq1\text{,}\) and let \(D_1,\ldots,D_n\) be nef Cartier divisors. Then
\begin{equation*} \deg(D_1\cdots D_n)=n!\,V_M(P_{D_1},\ldots,P_{D_n}). \end{equation*}
The mixed-volume normalization is specified by \(V_M(P,\ldots,P)=\vol_M(P)\text{;}\) equivalently, \(n!V_M(P_1,\ldots,P_n)\) is the coefficient of \(t_1\cdots t_n\) in \(\vol_M(t_1P_1+\cdots+t_nP_n)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For globally generated invariant Cartier divisors on a complete fan, addition of divisors corresponds to Minkowski addition of their polytopes. Their support functions are the minima of their linear pairings, so equality of support functions gives this assertion. Apply the degree–volume formula to \(t_1D_1+\cdots+t_nD_n\) for nonnegative integers \(t_i\text{.}\) Both sides are polynomial in the \(t_i\text{;}\) equality on these tuples implies equality of coefficients. The coefficient of \(t_1\cdots t_n\) in the self-intersection is \(n!\deg(D_1\cdots D_n)\text{,}\) and the coefficient on the volume side is \(n!\cdot n!V_M\text{.}\) Cancelling the common factor gives the claim.
