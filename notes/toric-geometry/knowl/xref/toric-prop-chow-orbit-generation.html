Proposition 5.1 Orbit closures generate.

The group \(A_j(X_\Sigma)\) is generated by the classes \([V(\sigma)]\) with \(\dim\sigma=n-j\text{.}\) Completeness and smoothness are not required.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
A split torus \(\Gm^d\) is an open subset of \(\A^d\text{.}\) The localization sequence and homotopy invariance give \(A_j(\Gm^d)=0\) for \(j\ne d\) and \(A_d(\Gm^d)=\Z[\Gm^d]\text{.}\) The equality in top dimension also follows directly because there is one irreducible component and no subvariety of larger dimension that could supply a relation.
🔗
Filter \(X_\Sigma\) by the closed subsets consisting of orbits of dimension at most \(d\text{.}\) Each successive open stratum is a disjoint union of \(d\)-dimensional tori. The localization sequence at each step shows that its only possible new Chow generators are the closures of those tori in dimension \(d\text{.}\) Induction gives precisely the asserted orbit closures.
