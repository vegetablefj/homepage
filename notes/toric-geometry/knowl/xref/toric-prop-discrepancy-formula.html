Proposition 7.8 The discrepancy coefficient.

A new primitive ray \(v\) in an original cone \(\sigma\) gives
\begin{equation} \operatorname{coeff}_{E_v}(K_Y-f^*K_X)=\langle m_\sigma,v\rangle-1.\tag{7.2} \end{equation}
The expression is independent of the cone containing \(v\text{.}\) A smooth subdivision is crepant exactly when all new primitive rays have height one.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The coefficient in \(K_Y\) is \(-1\text{.}\) Rational Cartier pullback gives coefficient \(-\psi_{K_X}(v)=-\langle m_\sigma,v\rangle\) in \(f^*K_X\text{.}\) Subtraction proves the formula; overlap agreement proves independence. Coefficients on original rays vanish, so equality of the canonical divisors is exactly height one on every new ray.
