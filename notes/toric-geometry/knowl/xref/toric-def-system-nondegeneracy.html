Definition 5.10 Newton polytopes and the condition at infinity.

For a nonzero Laurent polynomial \(f=\sum c_m\chi^m\in k[M]\text{,}\) its Newton polytope is
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{Newt}(f)=\operatorname{conv}\{m:c_m\ne0\}. \end{equation*}
For \(w\in N_{\R}\text{,}\) its initial form in direction \(w\) is the sum of terms where \(\langle m,w\rangle\) is minimal:
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{in}_w(f)= \sum_{\langle m,w\rangle=\min_{\operatorname{Newt}(f)} \langle\,\cdot\,,w\rangle}c_m\chi^m. \end{equation*}
A system \(f_1,\ldots,f_n\) is nondegenerate at infinity if for every nonzero \(w\in N_{\R}\) the initial forms \(\operatorname{in}_w(f_1),\ldots,\operatorname{in}_w(f_n)\) have no common zero in \(T_N\text{.}\)
