Theorem 4.22 Finite generation produces a toric degeneration.

Let \(X\) be a projective variety over \(\C\text{,}\) and let a very ample linear system \(V\subseteq H^0(X,L)\) give a closed immersion. For its homogeneous coordinate ring
\begin{equation*} R(V)=\bigoplus_{q\geq0} \operatorname{image}\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}^qV\to H^0(X,L^q)\bigr), \end{equation*}
take the valuation from an admissible complete flag. If the semigroup
\begin{equation*} \Gamma(V)=\{(q,\nu(s)):0\ne s\in R(V)_q\} \end{equation*}
is finitely generated, there is a flat family over \(\A^1\) whose nonzero fibers are isomorphic to \(X\) and whose zero fiber is \(\operatorname{Proj}\C[\Gamma(V)]\text{.}\) The special fiber can be nonnormal. Its normalization is the normal toric variety determined by the value polytope with its intrinsic lattice. Here that polytope is
\begin{equation*} \Delta(V)=\{v\in\R^n:(1,v)\in\overline{\Cone(\Gamma(V))}\}, \end{equation*}
the degree-one slice of the closed value cone; it uses values of the stated homogeneous coordinate ring.
