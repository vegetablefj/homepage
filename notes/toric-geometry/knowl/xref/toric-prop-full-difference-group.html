Proposition 1.13 The full character lattice is recovered.

If \(\sigma\subseteq N_{\R}\) is strongly convex and rational polyhedral, then
\begin{equation*} S_\sigma=\sigma^\vee\cap M,\qquad \operatorname{gp}(S_\sigma)=M. \end{equation*}
No full-dimensionality assumption on \(\sigma\) is needed.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose \(m_0\in M\) strictly positive on the nonzero vectors of \(\sigma\text{,}\) using Proposition 1.4. For any \(u\in M\text{,}\) the inequalities
\begin{equation*} \langle u+qm_0,v_i\rangle\geq0 \end{equation*}
hold on every nonzero generator \(v_i\) of \(\sigma\) once the integer \(q\geq0\) is sufficiently large. Thus both \(u+qm_0\) and \(qm_0\) lie in \(S_\sigma\text{,}\) and their difference is \(u\text{.}\) For \(\sigma=\{0\}\text{,}\) one already has \(S_\sigma=M\text{.}\)
