Theorem 16.4 Interior points and reciprocity.

For every positive integer \(q\text{,}\) let \(E_P^\circ(q)\) count the lattice points in the relative interior of \(qP\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation} E_P^\circ(q)=(-1)^dE_P(-q) =\sum_{j=0}^d h_j^*\binom{q+j-1}{d}.\tag{16.3} \end{equation}
In particular \(h_d^*\) counts the interior lattice points of \(P\) when \(d\geq1\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Repeat the half-open construction with \(-w\text{.}\) Its pieces partition \(\operatorname{Int}(C_P)\text{:}\) an interior point stays in the cone after a small displacement by \(-w\text{,}\) whereas at a boundary point some supporting functional is zero and becomes negative. In each simplicial cone the open and closed facets are exchanged. Reflection
\begin{equation*} p\longmapsto\sum_j a_{\nu,j}-p \end{equation*}
exchanges its two half-open parallelepipeds and changes height \(j\) into \(d+1-j\text{.}\) Thus the interior generating series is
\begin{equation} \sum_{q\geq1}E_P^\circ(q)z^q =\frac{z^{d+1}h_P^*(z^{-1})}{(1-z)^{d+1}}.\tag{16.4} \end{equation}
Its height-\(q\) slice is the interior of \(qP\) for \(q>0\text{.}\) Coefficient extraction gives the last expression in the statement. Finally the polynomial identity
\begin{equation*} \binom{-q+d-j}{d}=(-1)^d\binom{q+j-1}{d} \end{equation*}
proves reciprocity from the binomial formula for \(E_P\text{.}\) Setting \(q=1\) leaves only \(j=d\text{,}\) proving the interior coefficient assertion.
