Proposition 7.6 The fan of the normalized blowup.

The normalization of this blowup has subdivision cones
\begin{equation*} \sigma_i=\{v\in\sigma:\langle m_i,v\rangle\leq\langle m_j,v\rangle\text{ for every }j\} \end{equation*}
and their faces: the domains on which a chosen exponent realizes the minimum pairing.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The chart semigroup is generated by \(S_\sigma\) and the differences \(m_j-m_i\text{,}\) and its group of differences is \(M\text{.}\) Its cone has dual \(\sigma_i\text{.}\) The affine semigroup normalization theorem therefore gives normal ring \(k[\sigma_i^\vee\cap M]\text{.}\) The cones cover \(\sigma\) because a finite list of pairings always has a minimum. Their intersections are common faces defined by equalities of competing minima. The chart normalizations agree under the corresponding character localizations and glue. Their finiteness follows from the semigroup normalization theorem, identifying the glued variety with the normalization of the blowup.
