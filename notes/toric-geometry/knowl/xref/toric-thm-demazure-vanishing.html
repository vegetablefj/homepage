Theorem 5.22 Vanishing for a nef toric line bundle.

Let \(X_\Sigma\) be complete and \(D\) a nef Cartier divisor. Then \(H^p(X,\mathcal O(D))=0\) for every \(p>0\text{.}\) Smoothness and characteristic zero are not required.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The nef criterion in the positivity chapter makes \(D\) globally generated. Its support function is concave in our sign convention, and
\begin{equation*} \psi_D(u)=\min_{\sigma\ \mathrm{maximal}}\langle m_\sigma,u\rangle\qquad(u\in N_\R). \end{equation*}
Thus \(V_{D,m}\) is the intersection of the finitely many open half-spaces
\begin{equation*} \langle m-m_\sigma,u\rangle<0. \end{equation*}
It is convex, and is either empty or contractible. Its reduced cohomology in every nonnegative degree is zero. The preceding comparison therefore makes every weight space of every positive cohomology group zero. The direct-sum decomposition proves the claim.
