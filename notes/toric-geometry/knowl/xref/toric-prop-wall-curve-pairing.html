Proposition 7.12 A wall relation gives a curve functional.

For \(C=V(\tau)\text{,}\) the vector \((D_\rho\cdot C)_\rho\) is a positive rational multiple of this relation, with zero entries on other rays. Consequently
\begin{equation*} D\cdot C=\lambda\sum_\rho a_\rho c_\rho,\qquad D=\sum_\rho a_\rho D_\rho,\quad\lambda>0. \end{equation*}
If both adjacent cones are smooth, normalize \(c_n=c_{n+1}=1\text{;}\) then \(\lambda=1\text{.}\) For singular cones, lattice indices determine the factor and it must not be omitted.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Divisors outside the two adjacent cones are disjoint from \(C\text{.}\) Intersecting principal character divisors with \(C\) gives a relation among the remaining ray vectors. This relation space is one dimensional. Each extra divisor meets \(C\) at its corresponding endpoint without containing the curve. A positive Cartier multiple therefore restricts to a nonzero effective divisor, giving positive intersection and fixing the sign of proportionality. In smooth coordinates each endpoint intersection is transverse with multiplicity one, proving that normalization.
