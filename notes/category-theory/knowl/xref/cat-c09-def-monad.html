Definition 9.1 Monad.

A monad on \(\mathcal C\) consists of an endofunctor \(T:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C\) and natural transformations \(\eta:\id_{\mathcal C}\Rightarrow T\text{,}\) \(\mu:T^2\Rightarrow T\text{,}\) such that for every object \(X\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} \mu_XT(\eta_X)&=\id_{TX}=\mu_X\eta_{TX},\\ \mu_XT(\mu_X)&=\mu_X\mu_{TX}. \end{aligned}\tag{9.1} \end{equation}
The first line comprises maps \(TX\to TX\text{,}\) and the second comprises maps \(T^3X\to TX\text{.}\) We call \(\eta\) the unit and \(\mu\) the multiplication.
