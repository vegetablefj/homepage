Proposition 6.22.

Let \(T\) be a small category with finite products. The full subcategory of \(\Fun(T,\Set_\U)\) consisting of finite-product-preserving functors is closed under sifted colimits computed in that functor category.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a sifted diagram of such functors \(M_i\text{,}\) form its pointwise colimit \(M\text{.}\) For every finite family \((t_s)\) in \(T\text{,}\) the maps supplied by its product projections give natural comparison bijections for each \(M_i\text{.}\) Taking their colimit and using siftedness gives
\begin{equation*} M\left(\prod_s t_s\right)\cong\operatorname{colim}_i\prod_s M_i(t_s)\cong\prod_s M(t_s). \end{equation*}
The composite is precisely the comparison induced by those projections. The empty family is included, so the terminal object is preserved too. Thus \(M\) belongs to the full subcategory. Its cocone is still universal there, since it is already universal against every vertex in the ambient functor category.
