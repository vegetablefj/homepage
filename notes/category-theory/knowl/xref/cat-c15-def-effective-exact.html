Definition 15.13 Effectiveness and exact category.

  1. An internal equivalence relation on \(X\) is effective if it is isomorphic, as a relation over \(X\times X\text{,}\) to the kernel pair of some arrow out of \(X\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. A Barr-exact category is a regular category in which every internal equivalence relation is effective.
