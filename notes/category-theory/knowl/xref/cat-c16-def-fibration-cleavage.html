Definition 16.3 Fibrations and cleavages.

A Grothendieck fibration is a functor \(p:\mathcal E\to\mathcal B\) such that every \(u:a\to b\) and every \(y\in\mathcal E_b\) admit a cartesian lift. A cleavage is a choice of one such lift for every pair:
\begin{equation*} \chi_{u,y}:u^*y\longrightarrow y,\qquad p(\chi_{u,y})=u. \end{equation*}
It is normalized if \((\id_b)^*y=y\) and \(\chi_{\id_b,y}=\id_y\text{.}\)
