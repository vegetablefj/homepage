Theorem 9.10.

Let \((T,\eta,\mu)\) be a monad on a set-encoded category \(\mathcal C\text{,}\) and let \(D:J\to\mathcal C^T\) be a diagram, with \(J\) any set-encoded category. Write \(D_j=(A_j,a_j)\text{.}\) Suppose the underlying diagram has a specified colimit cocone \(i_j:A_j\to X\text{,}\) and both \(T\) and \(T^2\) preserve this cocone. There is a unique algebra structure \(a:TX\to X\) making every \(i_j\) an algebra map. With this structure, the cocone is a colimit in \(\mathcal C^T\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The maps \(i_ja_j:TA_j\to X\) form a cocone on \(TUD\text{.}\) Indeed, for a diagram arrow \(f:A_j\to A_k\text{,}\) its algebra equation and cocone compatibility give
\begin{equation*} i_ka_kT(f)=i_kfa_j=i_ja_j. \end{equation*}
Since \((T(i_j):TA_j\to TX)\) is a colimit cocone, it determines a unique map \(a\) with
\begin{equation*} aT(i_j)=i_ja_j. \end{equation*}
This is precisely the condition that the specified legs become algebra maps.
🔗
Every colimit cocone is jointly epic: two maps out of its vertex agree if they agree after all its legs. To verify the unit equation, calculate
\begin{equation*} a\eta_Xi_j=aT(i_j)\eta_{A_j} =i_ja_j\eta_{A_j}=i_j. \end{equation*}
Joint epicity of the original legs gives \(a\eta_X=\id_X\text{.}\) For associativity, naturality of \(\mu\) and the defining equation for \(a\) give
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} a\mu_XT^2(i_j)&=i_ja_j\mu_{A_j},\\ aT(a)T^2(i_j)&=aT\bigl(aT(i_j)\bigr) =i_ja_jT(a_j). \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
These are equal by the algebra law at \(A_j\text{.}\) The legs \(T^2(i_j)\) are jointly epic by the second preservation hypothesis, so \(a\mu_X=aT(a)\text{.}\)
🔗
Now take an algebra cocone \(c_j:(A_j,a_j)\to(B,b)\text{.}\) Its underlying maps induce a unique \(h:X\to B\) with \(hi_j=c_j\text{.}\) After each leg \(T(i_j)\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} haT(i_j)=c_ja_j=bT(c_j)=bT(h)T(i_j). \end{equation*}
These legs are jointly epic, so \(ha=bT(h)\text{.}\) Thus \(h\) is an algebra map and gives the required factorization in the algebra category. Uniqueness follows from uniqueness of its underlying map.
🔗
Finally, any other structure making the specified legs algebra maps satisfies the same equation \(aT(i_j)=i_ja_j\text{.}\) Joint epicity of \(T(i_j)\) makes it equal to the constructed structure. This proves both the colimit property and the asserted uniqueness of its lift.
