Example 9.15 An underlying filtered union that is not an algebra colimit.

Let \(P\) be the covariant powerset monad of Example 9.22. For the finite initial segments \(\underline n=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\text{,}\) direct images along their inclusions give a filtered diagram of free algebras \(F^P(\underline n)\text{.}\) Its underlying sets are \(P(\underline n)\text{.}\) Their colimit in sets is
\begin{equation*} \bigcup_{n\in\N}P(\underline n)=P_{\mathrm{fin}}(\N), \end{equation*}
the finite subsets of \(\N\text{.}\) In contrast, applying \(P\) to the colimit of the initial segments gives \(P(\N)\text{.}\) The comparison includes the finite subsets and misses every infinite subset, so \(P\) is not finitary.
🔗
The free-algebra functor is a left adjoint by Proposition 9.4, and therefore preserves this colimit. Consequently the colimit of the displayed algebra diagram is
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{colim}_nF^P(\underline n)\cong F^P(\N), \end{equation*}
the complete join-semilattice \(P(\N)\text{,}\) whose joins are unions. The canonical comparison from the underlying finite-subset union to this underlying algebra colimit is the proper inclusion \(P_{\mathrm{fin}}(\N)\to P(\N)\text{.}\) Thus the forgetful functor does not preserve this filtered colimit.
🔗
In fact no powerset-algebra structure on that finite-subset set makes all its inclusion legs algebra maps. Two finite subsets lie in a common \(P(\underline n)\text{,}\) so such a structure would have to give their union as binary join. Its induced order would therefore be inclusion. But the family of all singleton subsets has no finite upper bound, so the required join does not exist there. The preservation hypothesis of Theorem 9.10 fails on this underlying cocone.
 6 
For this particular underlying cocone, applying \(P\) gives maps \(P(P(\underline n))\to P(P_{\mathrm{fin}}(\N))\text{.}\) Every family in the image has all its members contained in one common \(\underline n\text{.}\) The family \(\{\{k\}:k\in\N\}\) lies in no such image, so the canonical filtered-colimit comparison is not surjective. Thus the preservation condition fails on the actual cocone being tested.
🔗
This failure does not mean that the algebra diagram lacks a filtered colimit: its colimit has already been constructed by the free adjunction. What fails is the assertion that the forgetful functor creates it on the particular colimit set computed underneath. Existence in the algebra category and creation from the underlying category remain separate claims.
