Proposition 5.31.

Suppose \(\mathcal C\) has small products and \((Q_s)_{s\in S}\) is a small cogenerating family. For every \(Y\text{,}\) let
\begin{equation*} T_Y=\prod_{s\in S}\prod_{a\in\Hom(Y,Q_s)}Q_s. \end{equation*}
The canonical map \(\nu_Y:Y\to T_Y\text{,}\) whose \((s,a)\)-component is \(a\text{,}\) is monic. Conversely, monicity of all these maps implies that the family is cogenerating.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The equation \(\nu_Yf=\nu_Yg\) is equivalent to all equations \(af=ag\text{,}\) by the product property. The cogenerating condition therefore gives exactly left cancellation by \(\nu_Y\text{.}\) The index set of the product is small because \(S\in\U\) and all its indicated Hom sets belong to \(\U\text{.}\)
