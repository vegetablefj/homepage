Proposition 16.9.

For coherent indexing data, use the same objects and arrows as in the strict Grothendieck construction, but define
\begin{equation*} (v,\beta)\circ(u,\alpha) =(vu,c_{u,v,z}\,u^*(\beta)\,\alpha),\qquad \id_{(b,y)}=(\id_b,e_{b,y}^{-1}), \end{equation*}
where \((v,\beta):(b,y)\to(c,z)\text{.}\) This defines a fibration with chosen cartesian lifts \((u,\id_{u^*y})\text{.}\) When the data come from a cleavage of \(p:\mathcal E\to\mathcal B\text{,}\) the functor
\begin{equation*} (b,y)\longmapsto y,\qquad (u,\alpha)\longmapsto\chi_{u,y}\alpha \end{equation*}
is an isomorphism of categories over \(\mathcal B\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a third arrow \((w,\delta):(c,z)\to(d,t)\text{,}\) the second components of the two triple composites have the common initial factor \(u^*v^*(\delta)\,u^*(\beta)\,\alpha\text{.}\) Naturality moves this factor past the relevant comparison, and the remaining equality is
\begin{equation*} c_{u,wv}\,u^*(c_{v,w}) =c_{vu,w}\,(c_{u,v}w^*). \end{equation*}
This proves associativity. For the right identity, naturality of \(e_a\) and \(c_{\id_a,u}=e_a u^*\) cancel the factor \(e_{a,x}^{-1}\text{.}\) For the left identity, \(c_{u,\id_b}=u^*e_b\) cancels \(u^*(e_{b,y}^{-1})\text{.}\) Thus the displayed arrows are identities.
🔗
For an arrow \((us,\gamma):(d,t)\to(b,y)\text{,}\) its unique factor over \(s:d\to a\) through \((u,\id_{u^*y})\) has second component
\begin{equation*} c_{s,u,y}^{-1}\gamma:t\longrightarrow s^*u^*y. \end{equation*}
The composition formula proves existence and uniqueness, so projection is a fibration.
🔗
For data obtained from a cleavage, the proposed recovery functor preserves identities because \(\chi_{\id_b,y}=e_{b,y}\text{.}\) It preserves composition by the defining equation for \(c\) and the defining naturality equation for restriction. Every object of \(\mathcal E\) has exactly one base, so its object assignment is bijective. Unique vertical factorization makes its map on each hom-set bijective. It is therefore an isomorphism, and it sends the displayed lifts to the chosen cartesian arrows.
