Proposition 1.18.

A transformation \(\eta:F\Rightarrow G\) is an isomorphism in \(\Fun(\mathcal C,\mathcal D)\) if and only if every component \(\eta_X\) is invertible. Its inverse then has components \(\eta_X^{-1}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
An inverse transformation gives inverse component arrows by the definition of vertical composition. Conversely, if every component is invertible, the naturality equation implies
\begin{equation*} F(f)\eta_X^{-1}=\eta_Y^{-1}G(f) \end{equation*}
after multiplication by the inverse components on the two sides. Thus the inverse family is natural, and its vertical composites with \(\eta\) have identity components.
