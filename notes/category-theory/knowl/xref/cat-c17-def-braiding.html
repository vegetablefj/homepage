Definition 17.8 Braided and symmetric monoidal categories.

A braiding is a natural isomorphism \(\beta_{X,Y}:X\otimes Y\to Y\otimes X\) satisfying the two hexagon equations
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \beta_{X,Y\otimes Z} ={}&\alpha_{Y,Z,X}^{-1}(\id_Y\otimes\beta_{X,Z})\, \alpha_{Y,X,Z}(\beta_{X,Y}\otimes\id_Z)\, \alpha_{X,Y,Z}^{-1},\\ \beta_{X\otimes Y,Z} ={}&\alpha_{Z,X,Y}(\beta_{X,Z}\otimes\id_Y)\, \alpha_{X,Z,Y}^{-1}(\id_X\otimes\beta_{Y,Z})\, \alpha_{X,Y,Z}, \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
and the unit compatibilities
\begin{equation*} \rho_X\beta_{I,X}=\lambda_X,\qquad \lambda_X\beta_{X,I}=\rho_X. \end{equation*}
A symmetric monoidal category is a braided monoidal category satisfying
\begin{equation*} \beta_{Y,X}\beta_{X,Y}=\id_{X\otimes Y} \end{equation*}
for every pair. A lax monoidal functor between braided categories is braided if
\begin{equation*} F(\beta^{\mathcal C}_{X,Y})\phi_{X,Y} =\phi_{Y,X}\beta^{\mathcal D}_{FX,FY}. \end{equation*}
