Definition 6.18 Sifted category.

A small category \(I\) is sifted if \(\operatorname{colim}_I:\Fun(I,\Set_\U)\to\Set_\U\) preserves finite products. Explicitly, for every finite family \((F_s:I\to\Set_\U)_{s\in S}\text{,}\) the canonical map
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{colim}_{i\in I}\prod_{s\in S}F_s(i)\longrightarrow\prod_{s\in S}\operatorname{colim}_{i\in I}F_s(i) \end{equation*}
is bijective. The empty family is included. A sifted colimit in any category means a colimit indexed by a small sifted category.
