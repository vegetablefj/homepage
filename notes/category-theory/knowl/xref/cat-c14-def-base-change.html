Definition 14.6 Change of base and dependent sum.

Suppose \(\mathcal C\) has pullbacks, and let \(f:A\to B\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. The pullback functor \(f^*:\mathcal C/B\to\mathcal C/A\) sends \(q:Y\to B\) to the projection \(A\times_B Y\to A\text{.}\) On slice arrows, it uses the unique map induced between these pullbacks.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The composition functor, also called dependent sum, is
    \begin{equation*} \Sigma_f:\mathcal C/A\longrightarrow\mathcal C/B,\qquad (p:X\to A)\longmapsto(fp:X\to B). \end{equation*}
    It sends each underlying arrow to itself.
    🔗
    🔗
