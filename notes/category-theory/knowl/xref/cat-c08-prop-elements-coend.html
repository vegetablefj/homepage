Proposition 8.7.

Let \(\pi:\operatorname{el}(P)\to\mathcal C\) be the projection for the contravariant element category. Then
\begin{equation*} P\star A\cong\operatorname{colim}_{(c,x)\in\operatorname{el}(P)}A(c). \end{equation*}
The element category is \(\U\)-small under the present assumptions.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
An arrow \((c,x)\to(d,y)\) is an \(f:c\to d\) with \(P(f)(y)=x\text{.}\) A cocone on \(A\pi\) is therefore exactly a family \(t_{c,x}\) satisfying the preceding equation. The two universal problems agree. The object set of \(\operatorname{el}(P)\) is a small union of the sets \(P(c)\) and its arrow set a small union of subsets of the Hom sets of \(\mathcal C\text{,}\) with the same small labels attached; both belong to \(\U\text{.}\)
