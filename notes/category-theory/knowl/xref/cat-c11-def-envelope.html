Definition 11.5 The Karoubi envelope.

The category \(\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)\) has objects \((c,e)\text{,}\) where \(e:c\to c\) is idempotent, and
\begin{equation*} \Hom_{\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)}((c,e),(d,e'))=\{f:c\to d:f=e'fe\}. \end{equation*}
Composition is inherited from \(\mathcal C\text{.}\) The identity on \((c,e)\) is \(e\text{,}\) and the canonical functor is \(J:\mathcal C\to\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)\text{,}\) \(J(c)=(c,\id_c)\text{,}\) \(J(f)=f\text{.}\)
