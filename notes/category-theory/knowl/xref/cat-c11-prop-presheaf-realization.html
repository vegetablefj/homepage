Proposition 11.8.

Set
\begin{equation*} P_e(x)=\{a:x\to c:ea=a\}. \end{equation*}
Precomposition makes this a presheaf. The assignment \((c,e)\mapsto P_e\text{,}\) sending \(f\) to postcomposition by \(f\text{,}\) is a fully faithful functor \(R:\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)\to\widehat{\mathcal C}\text{.}\) It extends Yoneda, and its essential image is precisely the retracts of representable presheaves.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
If \(ea=a\text{,}\) then \(e(ab)=ab\text{,}\) which proves closure under restriction. There are natural maps \(i_e:P_e\to h_c\) by inclusion and \(r_e:h_c\to P_e\) by \(a\mapsto ea\text{,}\) satisfying \(r_ei_e=\id\) and \(i_er_e=h_e\text{.}\) Thus \(P_e\) is the pointwise image of \(h_e\) and is a retract of \(h_c\text{.}\) The condition \(e'f=f=fe\) ensures that postcomposition carries \(P_e\) to \(P_{e'}\text{,}\) and composition and identities follow immediately.
🔗
For a transformation \(\beta:P_e\to P_{e'}\text{,}\) form \(i_{e'}\beta r_e:h_c\to h_d\text{.}\) Yoneda identifies it with a unique \(f:c\to d\text{.}\) Its invariance under \(h_e\) on the right and \(h_{e'}\) on the left says exactly \(fe=f=e'f\text{.}\) Conversely, \(\beta=r_{e'}h_fi_e\text{,}\) so every such transformation comes from exactly one arrow in the envelope. This proves full faithfulness. For identities, \(P_{\id_c}=h_c\text{,}\) giving the Yoneda restriction.
🔗
Finally, suppose \(P\xrightarrow{i}h_c\xrightarrow{r}P\) has \(ri=\id_P\text{.}\) The composite \(ir\) is an idempotent on \(h_c\text{,}\) hence is \(h_e\) for an idempotent \(e:c\to c\) by full faithfulness of Yoneda. Uniqueness of splittings gives \(P\cong P_e\text{.}\) The reverse inclusion was proved in the first paragraph.
