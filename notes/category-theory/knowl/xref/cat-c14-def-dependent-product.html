Definition 14.8 Dependent product and local closure.

For \(f:A\to B\) in a category with pullbacks, a dependent-product functor is a right adjoint
\begin{equation*} f^*\dashv\Pi_f,\qquad \Pi_f:\mathcal C/A\longrightarrow\mathcal C/B. \end{equation*}
It has natural bijections
\begin{equation*} \Hom_{\mathcal C/A}(f^*q,p) \cong\Hom_{\mathcal C/B}(q,\Pi_f p). \end{equation*}
An arrow for which this right adjoint exists is called exponentiable. In these notes, a category is locally cartesian closed if it has finite limits and every slice \(\mathcal C/B\) is cartesian closed.
