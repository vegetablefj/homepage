Definition 6.20 Reflexive pair and its indexing category.

A parallel pair \(d_0,d_1:A\rightrightarrows B\) is reflexive if it has a common section \(s:B\to A\text{,}\) so \(d_0s=d_1s=\id_B\text{.}\) A reflexive coequalizer is a coequalizer of such a pair. Let \(\mathcal R\) be the category presented by these two objects, three generating arrows, and those two relations; no further relations are imposed.
