Definition 2.5 Arrow, slice, and coslice categories.

  1. The arrow category \(\operatorname{Arr}(\mathcal C)=\Fun([1],\mathcal C)\) has arrows \(f:A\to B\) as objects. A morphism from \(f\) to \(f':A'\to B'\) is a pair \((a:A\to A',b:B\to B')\) satisfying \(bf=f'a\text{.}\) Composition is \((a',b')(a,b)=(a'a,b'b)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For \(X\in\mathcal C\text{,}\) the slice \(\mathcal C/X\text{,}\) or category over \(X\text{,}\) has objects \((A,p:A\to X)\text{.}\) A morphism \((A,p)\to(B,q)\) is an arrow \(a:A\to B\) with \(qa=p\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The coslice \(X/\mathcal C\text{,}\) or category under \(X\text{,}\) has objects \((A,i:X\to A)\text{.}\) A morphism \((A,i)\to(B,j)\) is an arrow \(a:A\to B\) with \(ai=j\text{.}\) In both slice constructions, identities and composition are inherited from \(\mathcal C\text{.}\)
