Definition 15.4 Relations expressed by finite limits.

Suppose \(\mathcal C\) is finitely complete.
🔗
  1. The kernel pair of \(f:X\to Y\) is \(\operatorname{Eq}(f)=X\times_YX\text{,}\) with projections \(r_0,r_1\) to \(X\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. An internal relation on \(X\) is a subobject \((r_0,r_1):R\rightarrowtail X\times X\text{.}\) It is an internal equivalence relation if there are maps
    \begin{equation*} d:X\to R,\qquad s:R\to R,\qquad t:R\times_XR\to R \end{equation*}
    satisfying \(r_0d=r_1d=\id_X\text{,}\) \(r_0s=r_1\text{,}\) \(r_1s=r_0\text{,}\) and \(r_0t=r_0\pi_1\text{,}\) \(r_1t=r_1\pi_2\text{.}\) In the last fiber product, \(r_1\pi_1=r_0\pi_2\text{.}\)
    🔗
