Definition 5.15 Parametrized and two-variable adjunctions.

For \(F:\mathcal A\times\mathcal B\to\mathcal C\text{,}\) a parametrized right adjoint is a bifunctor \(G:\mathcal A^\op\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal B\) with bijections
\begin{equation*} \Phi_{a,b,c}:\Hom_{\mathcal C}(F(a,b),c) \cong\Hom_{\mathcal B}(b,G(a,c)) \end{equation*}
natural in all three variables. If there is also \(H:\mathcal B^\op\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal A\) and natural bijections
\begin{equation*} \Hom_{\mathcal C}(F(a,b),c) \cong\Hom_{\mathcal B}(b,G(a,c)) \cong\Hom_{\mathcal A}(a,H(b,c)), \end{equation*}
these data form a two-variable adjunction.
