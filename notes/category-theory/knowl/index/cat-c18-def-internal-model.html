Definition 18.6 Internal model.

Let \(\mathcal C\) have finite products. A model of \(\mathbb L\) in \(\mathcal C\) is a finite-product-preserving functor \(\mathbb L\to\mathcal C\text{,}\) with natural transformations as morphisms. We denote this category by \(\operatorname{Mod}(\mathbb L,\mathcal C)\text{.}\)
