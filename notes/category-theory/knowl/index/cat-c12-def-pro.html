Definition 12.10 Pro objects.

Define
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{Pro}_{\U}(\mathcal C)= \operatorname{Ind}_{\U}(\mathcal C^{\op})^{\op}. \end{equation*}
A presentation can be written as a small cofiltered diagram \(X:I\to\mathcal C\text{,}\) denoted \(\text{``}\lim_{i\in I}X_i\text{''}\text{.}\) Its associated covariant functor is
\begin{equation*} c\longmapsto\operatorname{colim}_{i\in I^{\op}}\Hom_{\mathcal C}(X_i,c). \end{equation*}
The Pro category is the opposite of the full subcategory of \(\Fun(\mathcal C,\Set_{\U})\) on these functors. Constant presentations give a fully faithful embedding \(\iota_{\mathrm{pro}}:\mathcal C\to\operatorname{Pro}(\mathcal C)\text{.}\)
