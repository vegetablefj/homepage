Definition 10.9 A right multiplicative system, in the Stacks convention.

A subset \(S\subseteq\Ar(\mathcal C)\) is a right multiplicative system for the conventions of this chapter if it satisfies the following conditions.
🔗
  1. Every identity belongs to \(S\text{,}\) and composites of composable members of \(S\) belong to \(S\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. For \(f:A\to B\) and \(s:B'\to B\) with \(s\in S\text{,}\) there exist \(t:A'\to A\) in \(S\) and \(g:A'\to B'\) such that \(ft=sg\text{.}\) This is the Ore square condition.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. If \(f,g:A\to B\) and \(s:B\to B'\) in \(S\) satisfy \(sf=sg\text{,}\) there is \(t:A'\to A\) in \(S\) with \(ft=gt\text{.}\) This is the cancellation condition.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
A left multiplicative system means a right multiplicative system in \(\mathcal C^\op\text{,}\) with all arrows reversed. A system satisfying both sets of axioms is called a two-sided multiplicative system.
