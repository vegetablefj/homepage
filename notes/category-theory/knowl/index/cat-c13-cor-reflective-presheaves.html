Corollary 13.9.

Let \(\mathcal E\) be locally \(\kappa\)-presentable, and let \(K:\mathcal A\to\mathcal E\) be the small category of chosen generators above. Then \(N_K\) has a left adjoint
\begin{equation*} L_K(P)=\int^{a\in\mathcal A}P(a)\cdot K(a) \cong\operatorname{colim}_{(a,x)\in\operatorname{el}(P)}K(a). \end{equation*}
Its counit \(L_KN_K\Rightarrow\id_{\mathcal E}\) is invertible. Thus every locally presentable category is equivalent to a reflective full subcategory of a presheaf category on a small category. For a small diagram \(D:J\to\mathcal E\text{,}\) its colimit is given by
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{colim}_{j\in J}D_j \cong L_K\left(\operatorname{colim}_{j\in J}N_KD_j\right), \end{equation*}
where the colimit inside parentheses is computed pointwise in \(\widehat{\mathcal A}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Cocompleteness of \(\mathcal E\) gives \(L_K\dashv N_K\) by Theorem 8.9. The preceding proposition makes \(N_K\) fully faithful, so its counit is invertible by Proposition 5.17. The last formula follows because \(L_K\) preserves small colimits and \(L_KN_KD_j\cong D_j\text{.}\)
