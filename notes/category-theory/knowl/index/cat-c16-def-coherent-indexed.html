Definition 16.8 Coherent contravariant indexing data.

A coherent indexed category over \(\mathcal B\) consists of categories \(F(b)\text{,}\) functors \(u^*:F(b)\to F(a)\) for \(u:a\to b\text{,}\) and natural isomorphisms \(c_{u,v}:u^*v^*\to(vu)^*\) and \(e_b:(\id_b)^*\to\id_{F(b)}\) satisfying exactly the associativity and unit equations in Proposition 16.5. These data are also called a contravariant pseudofunctor to categories.
