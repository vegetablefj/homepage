Proposition 9.26.

The functor \(R_K:\mathcal D\to\mathcal D_K\text{,}\) \(X\mapsto(KX,\delta_X)\text{,}\) is right adjoint to \(U_K\text{.}\) Its bijection and inverse are
\begin{equation*} \Hom_{\mathcal D_K}((A,a),R_KX)\cong\Hom_{\mathcal D}(A,X),\qquad h\longmapsto\varepsilon_Xh,\quad f\longmapsto K(f)a. \end{equation*}
The forgetful functor creates all existing small colimits of underlying diagrams. An adjunction \(F:\mathcal C\rightleftarrows\mathcal D:G\text{,}\) with \(F\dashv G\text{,}\) induces the comonad
\begin{equation*} K=FG,\qquad \delta_X=F(\eta_{GX}), \end{equation*}
with the adjunction counit, and the comparison functor \(\mathcal C\to\mathcal D_K\) sends \(A\) to \((FA,F(\eta_A))\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Apply the free-algebra adjunction and creation-of-limits theorem to the monad on \(\mathcal D^\op\text{,}\) then reverse arrows. The transpose formulas become exactly those displayed. Applying the adjunction-monad construction to \(G^\op\dashv F^\op\) gives the indicated comultiplication and comparison after reversing arrows. Thus no preservation of colimits by \(K\) is needed for the creation assertion.
