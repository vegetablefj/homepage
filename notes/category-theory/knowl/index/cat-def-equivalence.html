Definition 1.19 Equivalence of categories.

A functor \(F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D\) is an equivalence if there is a functor \(G:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C\) and natural isomorphisms
\begin{equation*} \eta:\id_{\mathcal C}\Rightarrow GF,\qquad \varepsilon:FG\Rightarrow\id_{\mathcal D}. \end{equation*}
Such a functor \(G\) is called a quasi-inverse. We write \(\mathcal C\simeq\mathcal D\) when an equivalence exists.
