Definition 3.4 Representability and a chosen representation.

  1. A presheaf \(F:\mathcal C^\op\to\Set_\U\) is representable if there exist \(X\in\mathcal C\) and a natural isomorphism \(\rho:h_X\cong F\text{.}\) A chosen representation is the pair \((X,\rho)\text{.}\) Its universal element is \(u=\rho_X(\id_X)\in F(X)\text{.}\)

    🔗
  2. A covariant functor \(G:\mathcal C\to\Set_\U\) is representable if there exist \(X\in\mathcal C\) and a natural isomorphism \(\rho:h^X\cong G\text{.}\) Its universal element is again \(u=\rho_X(\id_X)\in G(X)\text{.}\)
