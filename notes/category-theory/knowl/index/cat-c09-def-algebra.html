Definition 9.3 Algebras for a monad.

A \(T\)-algebra is a pair \((A,a)\) with \(a:TA\to A\) satisfying
\begin{equation*} a\eta_A=\id_A,\qquad a\mu_A=aT(a):T^2A\to A. \end{equation*}
A morphism \(f:(A,a)\to(B,b)\) is an arrow \(f:A\to B\) with \(fa=bT(f)\text{.}\) The Eilenberg–Moore category of these algebras is denoted \(\mathcal C^T\text{;}\) its forgetful functor is \(U^T:\mathcal C^T\to\mathcal C\text{.}\)
