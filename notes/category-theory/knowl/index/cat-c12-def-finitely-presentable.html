Definition 12.5 Finite presentability at the chosen scale.

In a locally \(\U\)-small category with small filtered colimits, an object \(K\) is finitely presentable if \(\Hom(K,-)\) preserves those colimits. Thus the canonical map from the filtered colimit of hom-sets to the hom-set into the colimit must be a bijection.
