Proposition 10.10.

The category \(S/X\) is cofiltered. For each \(Y\text{,}\) roofs \((s:A\to X,f:A\to Y)\) are equivalent precisely when they have a common refinement: two roofs \((s_1,f_1)\text{,}\) \((s_2,f_2)\) are equivalent if there exist \(u:B\to A_1\text{,}\) \(v:B\to A_2\) with
\begin{equation*} s_1u=s_2v\in S,\qquad f_1u=f_2v. \end{equation*}
This is an equivalence relation, and the roof classes are the ordinary set colimit
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{colim}_{(s:A\to X)\in(S/X)^\op} \Hom_{\mathcal C}(A,Y). \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The identity of \(X\) is a denominator. For two denominators \(s:A\to X\text{,}\) \(t:B\to X\text{,}\) the Ore condition gives \(r:C\to A\) in \(S\) and \(v:C\to B\) with \(sr=tv\text{.}\) This common composite belongs to \(S\text{,}\) providing a common source in \(S/X\text{.}\) For parallel arrows \(u,v:s\to t\text{,}\) the equation \(tu=tv\) and cancellation give \(r:C\to A\) in \(S\) with \(ur=vr\text{.}\) The denominator \(sr\) and its map to \(s\) equalize the pair. Thus \((S/X)^\op\) is filtered.
🔗
On that opposite category the displayed Hom functor acts by precomposition. Its common-stage relation from Theorem 6.10 is exactly the common-refinement condition. The proof of that relation is an equivalence relation uses only finite witnesses, so it applies at the ordinary-set level even if the denominator category is not \(\U\)-small.
