Definition 3.6 Categories of elements, in both variances.

For either a presheaf \(F\) or a covariant set-valued functor \(G\) on \(\mathcal C\text{,}\) objects of its category of elements are pairs \((T,t)\text{,}\) where \(T\in\mathcal C\) and \(t\) belongs to the functor’s value at \(T\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. In \(\operatorname{el}(F)\text{,}\) an arrow \((T,t)\to(S,s)\) is an arrow \(a:T\to S\) such that \(F(a)(s)=t\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. In \(\operatorname{el}(G)\text{,}\) an arrow \((T,t)\to(S,s)\) is an arrow \(a:T\to S\) such that \(G(a)(t)=s\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Identities and composition are inherited from \(\mathcal C\text{.}\) In both cases the projection sending \((T,t)\) to \(T\) and \(a\) to \(a\) is a functor to \(\mathcal C\text{.}\)
