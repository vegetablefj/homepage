Definition 16.13 Cartesian functors.

A cartesian functor, also called a fibered functor, between fibrations over \(\mathcal B\) is a functor over \(\mathcal B\) that sends cartesian arrows to cartesian arrows. A natural transformation over \(\mathcal B\) has vertical components. An equivalence over \(\mathcal B\) consists of functors over the base with inverse comparison natural isomorphisms over the base.
