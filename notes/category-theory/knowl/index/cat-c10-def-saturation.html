Definition 10.7 Localization saturation.

The saturation of \(W\) is
\begin{equation*} W^{\mathrm{sat}}= \{f\in\Ar(\mathcal C):Q(f)\text{ is an isomorphism in }\mathcal C[W^{-1}]\}. \end{equation*}
We call \(W\) saturated when \(W=W^{\mathrm{sat}}\text{.}\) A compatible equivalence between localization models gives the same subset \(W^{\mathrm{sat}}\) of \(\Ar(\mathcal C)\text{.}\)
