Definition 3.4.

A subsheaf \(\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal F\) is a subpresheaf which is a sheaf. A morphism \(u:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G\) is locally surjective if, for every \(t\in\mathcal G(U)\text{,}\) there is an open cover \(U=\bigcup_i U_i\) and sections \(s_i\in\mathcal F(U_i)\) with \(u(s_i)=t|_{U_i}\text{.}\) We use surjective morphism of sheaves in this local sense, and explicitly say sectionwise surjective when that stronger condition is intended.
