Definition 3.10.

For an open subset \(U\subseteq X\text{,}\) let \(\mathcal P_U\) be the sheafification of the free \(\Lambda\)-module presheaf on \(h_U\text{.}\) Explicitly, this presheaf takes \(\Lambda\) on \(V\subseteq U\) and zero on other opens, with the induced restrictions. We call \(\mathcal P_U\) the free sheaf associated with \(U\text{.}\)
