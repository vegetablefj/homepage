Definition 7.10.

For a sheaf \(F\text{,}\) define
\begin{equation*} T(F)(U)=\prod_{x\in U}F_x. \end{equation*}
Restrictions discard the components outside the smaller open set. The map \(\eta_F:F\to T(F)\) sends a section to its family of germs. Set \(Q(F)=\coker(\eta_F)\text{,}\) \(Q^0(F)=F\text{,}\) and
\begin{equation*} G^n(F)=T(Q^n(F)),\qquad n\geq0. \end{equation*}
The differential \(G^n(F)\to G^{n+1}(F)\) is the quotient onto \(Q^{n+1}(F)\) followed by its germ embedding. The augmented complex \(F\to G^\bullet(F)\) is the Godement resolution used here.
