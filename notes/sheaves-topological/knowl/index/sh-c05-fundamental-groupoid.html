Definition 5.11 Fundamental groupoid and monodromy.

The fundamental groupoid \(\Pi_1(X)\) has points as objects and endpoint-preserving homotopy classes of paths as morphisms. Composition follows the convention of Proposition 5.10. Transport defines a functor
\begin{equation*} \Pi_1(X)\longrightarrow\Mod(\Lambda),\qquad x\longmapsto L_x. \end{equation*}
We call it the monodromy functor of \(L\text{.}\) A space is semilocally simply connected if every point has an open neighbourhood whose loops based at that point become null-homotopic in \(X\text{.}\)
