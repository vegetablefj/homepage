Definition 10.8 Crossed homomorphisms.

A crossed homomorphism for \(\rho\) is a map \(c:\pi_1(X,x)\to M\) satisfying
\begin{equation*} c(gh)=c(g)+\rho(g)c(h). \end{equation*}
Two such maps are equivalent if, for one \(b\in M\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} c'(g)=c(g)+\rho(g)b-b\qquad\text{for all }g. \end{equation*}
Their quotient group, under addition, is the degree-one group cohomology \(H^1(\pi_1(X,x),M)\) for this action.
