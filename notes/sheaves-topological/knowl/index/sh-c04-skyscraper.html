Definition 4.6 Skyscraper sheaf.

Let \(i_x:\{x\}\to X\) be the inclusion of a point, and let \(M\) be a \(\Lambda\)-module. The skyscraper sheaf \((i_x)_*M\) has sections \(M\) on opens containing \(x\) and zero on opens not containing \(x\text{.}\) Its restrictions are identities or zero maps as required.
