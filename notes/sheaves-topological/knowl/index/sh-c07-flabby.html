Definition 7.4.

A sheaf \(F\) is flabby, or flasque, if every restriction \(F(U)\to F(V)\) for open sets \(V\subseteq U\) is surjective. It is equivalent to require surjectivity of \(F(X)\to F(V)\) for every open \(V\text{.}\) A sheaf is acyclic for sections on \(U\) if its positive right derived functors for \(\Gamma(U,-)\) vanish.
