Definition 7.17.

A complex \(P\) is K-flat if \(\Tot(A\otimes_\Lambda P)\) is acyclic for every acyclic complex \(A\) of sheaves. Tensor totalization always uses direct sums:
\begin{equation*} (K\otimes_\Lambda L)^n=\bigoplus_{p+q=n}K^p\otimes_\Lambda L^q,\qquad d(k\otimes l)=dk\otimes l+(-1)^p k\otimes dl. \end{equation*}
Here \(k\) has degree \(p\text{.}\) A K-flat resolution is a quasi-isomorphism \(P\to K\) with \(P\) K-flat.
