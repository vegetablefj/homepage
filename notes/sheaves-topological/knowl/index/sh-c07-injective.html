Definition 7.1.

A sheaf \(I\) is injective if every morphism \(A\to I\) extends across every monomorphism \(A\hookrightarrow B\text{.}\) Equivalently, the contravariant functor \(\Hom_{\Sh(X;\Lambda)}(-,I)\) is exact. An injective resolution of \(F\) is an exact sequence
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow I^0\longrightarrow I^1\longrightarrow\cdots \end{equation*}
with injective terms. The category \(\Sh(X;\Lambda)\) has enough injectives by Section 3.4.
