Definition 2.1.

Let \(\mathcal F\) be a presheaf on \(X\) and let \(x\in X\text{.}\) Its stalk at \(x\) is
\begin{equation*} \mathcal F_x=\colim_{x\in U}\mathcal F(U), \end{equation*}
where an arrow from \(U\) to \(V\) means \(x\in V\subseteq U\) and induces restriction. For a section \(s\in\mathcal F(U)\text{,}\) its image \(s_x\) is its germ at \(x\). Explicitly, \((U,s)\) and \((V,t)\) determine the same germ if there is an open \(W\) with \(x\in W\subseteq U\cap V\) and \(s|_W=t|_W\text{.}\)
