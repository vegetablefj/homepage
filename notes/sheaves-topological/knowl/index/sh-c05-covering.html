Definition 5.8 Covering space.

A covering space over \(X\) is a continuous map \(p:E\to X\) such that every point has an open neighbourhood \(U\) with an isomorphism over \(U\)
\begin{equation*} p^{-1}U\simeq U\times A \end{equation*}
for a discrete set \(A\text{.}\) We allow \(A=\varnothing\text{;}\) thus a covering is not required to be surjective. Morphisms are continuous maps over \(X\text{.}\)
