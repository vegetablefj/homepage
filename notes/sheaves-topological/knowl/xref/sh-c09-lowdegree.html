Corollary 9.8.

For every fixed cover there is an exact sequence
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow\check H^1(\mathcal U,F)\longrightarrow H^1(X,F)\longrightarrow\check H^0(\mathcal U,\underline H^1_F)\longrightarrow\check H^2(\mathcal U,F)\longrightarrow H^2(X,F). \end{equation*}
In particular the degree-one fixed-cover comparison is injective. Passing to all refinements gives isomorphisms \(\check H^n(X,F)\simeq H^n(X,F)\) for \(n=0,1\text{,}\) and an injection for \(n=2\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The five-term sequence is the low-degree sequence of Theorem 9.7. To pass to refinements, note that an element of \(\check H^0(\mathcal U,\underline H^1_F)\) is a family of local degree-one classes. Each class vanishes on a cover of its open set, because the associated sheaf of \(\underline H^1_F\) is zero. Refining by all those smaller sets kills the family. Its colimit is therefore zero. Filtered colimits of modules are exact, which proves the last assertions. The degree-zero assertion is already the sheaf axiom.
