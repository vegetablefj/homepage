Theorem 4.19.

Restriction from sheaves on \(X\) to gluing data on an open covering is an equivalence of categories. A sheaf realizing a given datum, together with the specified identifications on the covering, is unique up to a unique isomorphism respecting those identifications. This holds for sets, Abel groups, and \(\Lambda\)-modules.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For the given datum define, for every open \(V\subset X\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} F(V)= \left\{(s_a)\in\prod_aF_a(V\cap U_a): s_a|_{V\cap U_a\cap U_b} =\theta_{ab}(s_b|_{V\cap U_a\cap U_b})\right\}. \end{equation*}
Restrict tuples componentwise. A matching family of tuples on a covering of \(V\) glues in every \(F_a\text{.}\) The resulting components satisfy the overlap conditions because the conditions hold locally. Thus \(F\) is a sheaf.
🔗
When \(V\subset U_c\text{,}\) projection to \(s_c\) is a bijection from \(F(V)\) to \(F_c(V)\text{.}\) Its inverse sends \(s_c\) to the tuple with components \(\theta_{ac}(s_c|_{V\cap U_a})\text{.}\) The cocycle condition gives precisely the required compatibility. These maps identify \(F|_{U_c}\) with \(F_c\text{.}\)
🔗
A morphism of data acts on the tuples componentwise and hence gives a morphism of glued sheaves. Conversely, maps of sheaves on the covering that agree on overlaps glue uniquely: apply the sheaf axiom in the target to the images of each source section. This proves full faithfulness and the specified uniqueness. All operations are componentwise algebraic operations for groups or modules.
