Definition 11.10 A local vanishing condition.

For an Abelian group \(M\text{,}\) consider the condition
\begin{equation*} \colim_{U\ni x}H^q_{\mathrm{sing}}(U,\{x\};M)=0\qquad\text{for every }x\in X\text{ and }q\geq0. \end{equation*}
We call this cohomological local connectedness with coefficients in \(M\), in the colimit sense. We say that \(X\) is semilocally contractible if for every \(x\in U\text{,}\) with \(U\) open, there is an open \(x\in V\subset U\) such that the inclusion \(V\to U\) is null-homotopic.
