Proposition 10.1.

Choose an open covering \(\mathcal U=(U_i)\) and lifts \(g_i\in G(U_i)\) of \(h|_{U_i}\text{.}\) Define \(c_{ij}\in F(U_i\cap U_j)\) by
\begin{equation*} \iota(c_{ij})=g_j-g_i. \end{equation*}
Then \(c_{ij}+c_{jk}=c_{ik}\) on triple intersections. Its image under the Čech-to-derived comparison is \(\partial h\text{.}\) Replacing \(g_i\) by \(g_i+\iota(b_i)\) replaces \(c_{ij}\) by \(c_{ij}+b_j-b_i\text{.}\)

🔗

Proof.
The difference \(g_j-g_i\) belongs to the kernel of \(q\text{,}\) so exactness determines \(c_{ij}\text{.}\) The identity
\begin{equation*} (g_j-g_i)+(g_k-g_j)=g_k-g_i \end{equation*}
gives the cocycle relation. A connecting class is obtained by lifting a cocycle and applying the differential. Applying this construction in the Čech resolution and an injective resolution gives the displayed representative of \(\partial h\text{;}\) the comparison of the two constructions is the naturality of the connecting homomorphism.
 1 
With the total differential used in Chapter 9, resolution preimages \(u_i\) satisfying \(d u_i=b\) represent \([b]\) by \(u_i-u_j\text{.}\) There is no sign conflict here: if \(\widetilde h\) is a global resolution lift of \(h\text{,}\) then \(u_i=\widetilde h-g_i\text{,}\) so \(g_j-g_i=u_i-u_j\text{.}\) Genuine local lifts in \(G\) and local preimages in a resolution of \(F\) are different choices.
The formula for a change of lifts follows by subtraction.
