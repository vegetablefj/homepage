Example 3.2.

Let \(X=\N\) be discrete and let \(\Lambda\ne0\text{.}\) Consider the coproduct of countably many copies of \(\Lambda_X\text{.}\) Its sections on \(X\) include the function assigning to the point \(n\) the \(n\)-th standard basis vector in \(\bigoplus_{i\geq1}\Lambda\text{.}\) This section is not in \(\bigoplus_i\Gamma(X,\Lambda_X)\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
A sheaf on a discrete space has arbitrary independent sections at the individual points. Thus the section in question is obtained by gluing its values on singletons. A section in the raw direct sum of global section modules has nonzero entries in only finitely many summand indices, uniformly over \(X\text{.}\) Our section has no such uniform finite set.
