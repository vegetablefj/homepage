Lemma 11.4.

If \(X\) is locally contractible, the augmented sheaf complex
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow L\longrightarrow\mathcal S^0(L)\xrightarrow{d}\mathcal S^1(L)\xrightarrow{d}\cdots \end{equation*}
is exact.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Fix \(x\in X\) and trivialize \(L\) on a neighborhood of \(x\text{,}\) with value \(M=L_x\text{.}\) Restrict to a cofinal basis of contractible open neighborhoods inside it. On each such neighborhood the augmented singular cochain complex has cohomology \(M\) in degree zero and zero in positive degrees. This is the ordinary singular contraction argument, with the trivialized coefficients. Filtered colimits of modules are exact and commute with taking cohomology. Since sheafification preserves stalks, the same statement holds for the stalk of \(\mathcal S^\bullet(L)\) at \(x\text{.}\) The augmentation identifies the degree-zero stalk with \(L_x\text{.}\) Exactness on all stalks proves the claim.
