Proposition 7.7.

A flabby sheaf is acyclic for \(\Gamma(U,-)\) on every open \(U\text{,}\) and for \(f_*\) under every continuous map \(f\text{.}\) Consequently, a bounded below resolution by flabby sheaves computes these right derived functors.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Embed a flabby sheaf \(F\) into an injective \(I\text{.}\) Its cokernel is flabby by Lemma 7.6, and taking sections on any open preserves the resulting short exact sequence. Repeating gives an injective resolution whose successive kernels are flabby. Its section complex is exact in positive degrees. The same argument applies to \(f_*\text{,}\) since its sections on \(V\subseteq Y\) are sections on \(f^{-1}V\text{.}\) The assertion about resolutions is the acyclic resolution theorem.
