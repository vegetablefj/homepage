Proposition 6.19.

For every open \(U\subset X\text{,}\) the sheaf \(j_{U!}\Lambda_U\) is flat and satisfies
\begin{equation*} \Hom_X(j_{U!}\Lambda_U,F)\simeq F(U). \end{equation*}
Every module sheaf admits an epimorphism
\begin{equation*} \bigoplus_{(U,s),\ s\in F(U)}j_{U!}\Lambda_U \longrightarrow F. \end{equation*}
Consequently every module sheaf is a quotient of a flat sheaf and admits a flat resolution.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The first sheaf has stalk \(\Lambda\) on \(U\) and zero outside, so it is flat. Its Hom formula follows from \(j_{U!}\dashv j_U^{-1}\) and the constant-sheaf adjunction. For each pair \((U,s)\text{,}\) use the corresponding map to \(F\text{.}\) Every germ of \(F\) is represented by one of these sections and hence is in the image. The direct sum is flat, and the map is an epimorphism by stalks. Repeating the construction on successive kernels gives an exact complex of flat sheaves resolving \(F\text{.}\)
