Definition 9.1.

Let \(\mathcal U=(U_i)_{i\in I}\) cover \(X\text{,}\) and write \(U_{i_0\ldots i_p}=U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}\text{.}\) For an abelian or module presheaf \(F\text{,}\) its Čech complex is
\begin{equation*} \check C^p(\mathcal U,F)=\prod_{(i_0,\ldots,i_p)\in I^{p+1}}F(U_{i_0\ldots i_p}),\qquad p\geq0, \end{equation*}
with differential
\begin{equation*} (\delta c)_{i_0\ldots i_{p+1}}=\sum_{a=0}^{p+1}(-1)^a c_{i_0\ldots\widehat{i_a}\ldots i_{p+1}}|_{U_{i_0\ldots i_{p+1}}}. \end{equation*}
Its cohomology is denoted \(\check H^p(\mathcal U,F)\text{.}\) A cochain in the kernel of \(\delta\) is a Čech cocycle; one in the image is a Čech coboundary. The augmentation \(F(X)\to\check C^0(\mathcal U,F)\) sends a section to its restrictions.
