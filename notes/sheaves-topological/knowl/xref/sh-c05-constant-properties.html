Proposition 5.2.

Let \(a:X\to\{*\}\text{.}\) Constant sheaves satisfy
\begin{equation*} M_X\simeq a^{-1}M,\qquad \Hom_X(M_X,F)\simeq\Hom_\Lambda(M,\Gamma(X,F)). \end{equation*}
The functor \(M\mapsto M_X\) is exact, preserves all colimits and finite limits, and has stalk \(M\) at each point. For every continuous \(f:X\to Y\text{,}\) there is a canonical isomorphism \(f^{-1}M_Y\simeq M_X\text{.}\) If \(X\) is nonempty and connected, \(M\mapsto M_X\) is fully faithful.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The first assertion follows by applying the inverse image construction to a point. The adjunction and exactness follow from Theorem 4.3 and Proposition 4.5. Compatibility with \(f\) follows from \(a_Yf=a_X\text{.}\) On a nonempty connected space, every locally constant function to a discrete set is constant. Hence \(\Gamma(X,N_X)=N\text{,}\) and the adjunction identifies morphisms between constant sheaves with module homomorphisms.
