Lemma 9.6.

For an injective sheaf \(I\text{,}\) the augmented complex
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow I(X)\longrightarrow\check C^0(\mathcal U,I)\longrightarrow\check C^1(\mathcal U,I)\longrightarrow\cdots \end{equation*}
is exact for every open cover.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For an open set \(W\text{,}\) write \(\Lambda_{W!}=j_{W!}\Lambda_W\text{.}\) Form the augmented chain complex of sheaves
\begin{equation*} \cdots\longrightarrow P_1\longrightarrow P_0\longrightarrow\Lambda_X\longrightarrow0,\qquad P_p=\bigoplus_{i_0,\ldots,i_p}\Lambda_{U_{i_0\ldots i_p}!}, \end{equation*}
with boundary the alternating sum of the maps that delete one index. This complex is exact. Indeed, its stalk at \(x\) is the augmented chain complex on all tuples of indices whose open sets contain \(x\text{;}\) choose one such index and insert it at the start to contract that complex. Stalks commute with the direct sums used here.
🔗
Apply the exact contravariant functor \(\Hom(-,I)\text{.}\) Since \(\Hom(\Lambda_{W!},I)=I(W)\text{,}\) the resulting augmented cochain complex is the one in the statement. Injectivity is used for exactness of Hom, rather than for any projectivity assertion about the sheaves \(\Lambda_{W!}\text{.}\)
