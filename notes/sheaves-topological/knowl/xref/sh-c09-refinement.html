Definition 9.4.

A cover \(\mathcal V=(V_j)_{j\in J}\) refines \(\mathcal U\) if each \(V_j\) is contained in some \(U_i\text{.}\) A choice of indices \(r:J\to I\) with \(V_j\subseteq U_{r(j)}\) induces a cochain map
\begin{equation*} r^*:\check C^\bullet(\mathcal U,F)\longrightarrow\check C^\bullet(\mathcal V,F),\qquad(r^*c)_{j_0\ldots j_p}=c_{r(j_0)\ldots r(j_p)}|_{V_{j_0\ldots j_p}}. \end{equation*}
The Čech cohomology of the space is
\begin{equation*} \check H^p(X,F)=\colim_{\mathcal U}\check H^p(\mathcal U,F), \end{equation*}
with transition maps given by refinements.
