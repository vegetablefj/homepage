Theorem 11.11.

Let \(M\) be an Abelian group and let \(X\) satisfy the colimit local-vanishing condition in Definition 11.10. Then singular cohomology with coefficients in \(M\) and sheaf cohomology of \(M_X\) are naturally isomorphic in every degree. No Hausdorff or paracompactness assumption is required. In particular, this holds for every semilocally contractible space.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
This is [Pet21, Theorem 1.2]. The semilocally contractible version is proved by [Sel16, the theorem in the introduction] using a different flabby resolution. We use these broader results as external comparison theorems. Their proofs remove the covering assumptions by changing the method, rather than by assuming that ordinary sheafification preserves flabby presheaves.
