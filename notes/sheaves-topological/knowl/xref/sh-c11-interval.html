Corollary 11.9.

For every interval \(J\subset\R\text{,}\) including half-open and closed intervals, and every constant coefficient module \(M\text{,}\) one has \(H^q(J,M_J)=0\) for \(q>0\text{.}\) If \(J\) is nonempty, evaluation gives \(H^0(J,M_J)=M\text{.}\) Every locally constant sheaf on a nonempty interval is constant.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
An interval is metrizable and has a basis of contractible neighborhoods. Singular contraction and Theorem 11.8 give the cohomology assertion. Triviality of a local system follows from parallel transport on the interval: paths with fixed endpoints are homotopic, and transport from one base point gives local trivializations which agree. Equivalently, apply the monodromy description from Theorem 5.12.
