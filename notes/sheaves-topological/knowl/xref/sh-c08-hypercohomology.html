Theorem 8.8.

Let \(K^\bullet\) be a complex with \(K^p=0\) for \(p<p_0\text{.}\) There is a natural spectral sequence
\begin{equation*} E_1^{p,q}=H^q(X,K^p)\ \Longrightarrow\ H^{p+q}(X,K),\qquad p\geq p_0,\ q\geq0. \end{equation*}
For \(K\in\D^+(X;\Lambda)\text{,}\) there is also a natural spectral sequence
\begin{equation*} E_2^{p,q}=H^p(X,\mathcal H^q(K))\ \Longrightarrow\ H^{p+q}(X,K),\qquad p\geq0. \end{equation*}
In each case \(d_r\) has bidegree \((r,1-r)\text{,}\) and the abutment in each degree has a finite filtration with successive quotients \(E_\infty^{p,n-p}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For the first sequence, use the Godement double complex \(\Gamma(X,G^q(K^p))\) from Proposition 7.12. Taking cohomology in the resolution direction gives \(H^q(X,K^p)\text{.}\) The induced first differential is the one induced by \(d_K\text{.}\) The total complex computes \(R\Gamma(X,K)\text{.}\) Each diagonal is finite, which proves the claimed convergence.
🔗
For the second sequence, apply derived sections to the canonical truncation triangles
\begin{equation*} \tau_{\leq q-1}K\longrightarrow\tau_{\leq q}K\longrightarrow \mathcal H^q(K)[-q]\longrightarrow(\tau_{\leq q-1}K)[1]. \end{equation*}
The associated exact couple, with the usual reindexing, has the displayed \(E_2\) page. Boundedness below and the absence of negative cohomology for sections of a sheaf leave only finitely many terms in each total degree. The exact-couple construction and finite-filtration convergence are the general results of the spectral sequence chapter. A sheaf reference with these two filtrations is Stacks, Examples 20.29.3–20.29.4.
