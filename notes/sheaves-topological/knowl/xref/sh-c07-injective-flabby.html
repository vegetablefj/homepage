Proposition 7.5.

Every injective sheaf is flabby. Restrictions and direct images of flabby sheaves are flabby. Products of flabby sheaves are flabby.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Write \(\Lambda_{U!}=j_{U!}\Lambda_U\) for the open extension by zero of the constant sheaf on \(U\text{.}\) A section of \(I\) on \(U\) is a morphism \(\Lambda_{U!}\to I\text{.}\) For \(V\subseteq U\text{,}\) the natural map \(\Lambda_{V!}\to\Lambda_{U!}\) is a monomorphism, as one sees on stalks. Injectivity of \(I\) therefore makes \(I(U)\to I(V)\) surjective. The other assertions follow from the formulas for restriction, direct image, and products of sheaves.
