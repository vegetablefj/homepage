Proposition 3.7.

For a sequence of module sheaves
\begin{equation*} \mathcal F\xrightarrow{u}\mathcal G\xrightarrow{v}\mathcal H, \end{equation*}
exactness at \(\mathcal G\) is equivalent to exactness of the sequence of stalk modules at every point. Equivalently, \(vu=0\) and every section of \(\ker v\) locally lifts through \(u\text{.}\) In particular, module-sheaf monomorphisms are sectionwise and stalkwise injections, and epimorphisms are locally surjective and stalkwise surjective.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Exactness implies stalkwise exactness by the preceding theorem. Conversely, the quotient of \(\ker v\) by the sheaf image of \(u\) has zero stalks, and hence is zero by Proposition 2.11. This proves exactness. The description of the image gives the local lifting form. The last assertions follow from the kernel and cokernel tests in an abelian category.
