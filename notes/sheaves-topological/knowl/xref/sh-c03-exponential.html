Example 3.8.

Let \(X=\R/\Z\) be the circle. Let \(\mathcal C_{\R}\) and \(\mathcal C_{\R/\Z}\) be the sheaves of continuous functions from opens of \(X\) to the indicated topological abelian groups. Reduction modulo integers gives a short exact sequence of abelian sheaves
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow\Z_X\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{\R}\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{\R/\Z}\longrightarrow0. \end{equation*}
Its map on global sections is not surjective.
🔗
Solution.
The kernel consists of continuous integer-valued functions, which are locally constant. Every continuous circle-valued function has real lifts near each point because \(\R\to\R/\Z\) is a local homeomorphism. This proves local surjectivity.
🔗
The identity of \(\R/\Z\) has no global continuous real lift. If \(h:\R/\Z\to\R\) were such a lift, the continuous function \(t\mapsto h(t\bmod\Z)-t\) would be integer-valued on the connected space \(\R\text{,}\) and therefore constant. It follows that \(h(t\bmod\Z)=t+n\text{,}\) contradicting periodicity in \(t\text{.}\)
