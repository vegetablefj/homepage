Lemma 11.13.

If \(H\) is locally constant on \(X\times I\text{,}\) then \(R^q p_*H=0\) for \(q>0\text{,}\) \(p_*H\) is locally constant, and the counit
\begin{equation*} p^{-1}p_*H\xrightarrow{\sim}H \end{equation*}
is an isomorphism. Restriction to either endpoint induces isomorphisms on derived global sections.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
On each fiber, \(H\) is a locally constant sheaf on an interval, hence is constant and acyclic. The compact-projection formula used in Lemma 11.12 identifies \((p_*H)_x\) with its sections on the fiber and kills all higher direct images. Evaluation of these sections at \((x,t)\) is an isomorphism. These evaluations are the stalk maps of the counit, proving it is an isomorphism. Pulling back along \(i_t\) identifies \(p_*H\) with \(i_t^{-1}H\text{,}\) which is locally constant. Applying Lemma 11.12 to \(p_*H\) proves the final assertion.
