Proposition 4.6.

If \(P\) is not generated by its global sections at \(x\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation} \liminf_{j\to\infty}\frac1{jN_j} \sum_{\gamma\in H_j}|\gamma|_1 >\frac n{n+1}.\tag{4.9} \end{equation}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Suppose that (4.9) fails. The lower bound of Lemma 4.2 forces \(L^n=1\) and a subsequence with normalized mean tending to \(n/(n+1)\text{.}\) Choose any rational \(t'\) between \(n\) and \(n+1\text{.}\) Lemma 4.3 constructs the sections with the stated convex-hull property. Lemma 4.4 gives the strict threshold bound for the restricted base ideal, and Lemma 4.5 gives generation of \(P\) at \(x\text{,}\) a contradiction.
