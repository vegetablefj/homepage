Lemma 2.2.

Let \(D_1,\ldots,D_N\) be effective divisors on \(X\text{,}\) and let \(Y\to X\) be a log resolution of their union. Include all exceptional divisors in the simple normal crossing divisor, and denote its components by \(E_j\text{.}\) For a positive multiple \(v\) of a prime-divisor order over \(Y\text{,}\) let \(u_j=v(E_j)\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} v(D_i)&=\sum_j u_j\ord_{E_j}(D_i),\\ \A(v)&\geq\sum_j u_j\A(E_j). \end{aligned}\tag{2.3} \end{equation}
If at least one \(u_j\) is positive, the monomial order determined by these weights at the stratum containing the generic center of \(v\) has the same values on the \(D_i\text{,}\) no larger discrepancy, and a closed center on \(X\) containing \(c_X(v)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Locally at the generic center on \(Y\text{,}\) the pullback of \(D_i\) is a monomial in the equations of the \(E_j\) times a unit. Taking \(v\) gives the equality in (2.3).
🔗
The reduced simple normal crossing divisor \(\sum_j E_j\) is log canonical on \(Y\text{.}\) Hence the log discrepancy of \(v\) relative to \(Y\) is at least \(\sum_j u_j\text{.}\) Equivalently, a logarithmic volume form has at most a simple pole along any prime on a higher smooth model. The discrepancy relative to \(X\) is obtained by adding \(v(K_{Y/X})\text{.}\) Since every exceptional component was included among the \(E_j\text{,}\) this gives
\begin{equation*} \A(v)\geq\sum_j u_j+ \sum_j u_j\ord_{E_j}(K_{Y/X}) =\sum_j u_j\A(E_j). \end{equation*}
The last expression is the discrepancy of the retracted monomial order. The closure of the stratum defining that order contains the generic center of \(v\) on \(Y\text{.}\) Properness then gives the asserted inclusion of closed centers on \(X\text{.}\)
