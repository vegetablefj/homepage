Lemma 8.4.

There are constants \(C'_d\) and \(H'_d\text{,}\) depending only on \((X,L,k,d)\text{,}\) such that for every nonempty \(G_p(j)\) and every \(h\ge H'_d\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} b_p(j+h)\ge b_p(j)+h-C'_d.\tag{8.11} \end{equation}
These constants are independent of \(j\text{,}\) \(p\text{,}\) the center, the test, and the coordinates chosen above.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \(C'_d=C_d+h_0\text{,}\) and choose an integer
\begin{equation*} H'_d\ge\max\{H_d+h_0,\lceil C'_d\rceil+1\}. \end{equation*}
By (8.2) and (8.9),
\begin{equation*} G_p(j)+G_0(h-h_0)+\{0,1\}^d\subset G_p(j+h). \end{equation*}
For \(h\ge H'_d\text{,}\) the estimate (8.8) gives \(b_0(h-h_0)\ge h-C'_d>0\text{.}\) The set \(G_0(h-h_0)\) is therefore nonempty. Applying Lemma 8.3 and multiplying by \((d!)^{1/d}\text{,}\) we obtain
\begin{equation*} b_p(j+h)\ge b_p(j)+b_0(h-h_0) \ge b_p(j)+h-h_0-C_d. \end{equation*}
This proves (8.11), and the constants have the stated uniformity.
