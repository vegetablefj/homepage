Theorem 3.1 Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing.

Let \(T\) be smooth and projective over \(\C\text{.}\) Let \(\Delta\) be an effective rational divisor with simple normal crossing support and coefficients in \([0,1)\text{,}\) and let \(D\) be an integral divisor. If \(D-(K_T+\Delta)\) is nef and big, then
\begin{equation*} H^i(T,\cO_T(D))=0\qquad(i>0). \end{equation*}
