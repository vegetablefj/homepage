Lemma 4.3.

Suppose that the normalized means in (4.2) tend to \(n/(n+1)\) along a subsequence. For any rational \(t'\) with \(n<t'<n+1\text{,}\) there are a positive integer \(h\) with \(ht'\in\Z\) and finitely many sections \(r_\ell\in H^0(X,ht'L)\) whose initial exponents \(\beta_\ell=\operatorname{in}(r_\ell)\) satisfy
\begin{equation} |\beta_\ell|_1=nh.\tag{4.3} \end{equation}
The convex hull of the \(\beta_\ell/h\) contains \(\mathbf1=(1,\ldots,1)\) in its interior relative to the full hyperplane \(|u|_1=n\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The point \(c=(1/t',\ldots,1/t')\) belongs to the interior of \(\Delta\text{.}\) Choose a small simplex containing \(c\) in its interior and contained in the interior of \(\Delta\text{.}\) Choose small balls around its vertices such that any choice of one point from each ball still has convex hull containing \(c\) in its interior. By Lemma 4.2, choose a sufficiently large common degree \(j\) in the subsequence and initial exponents \(\gamma_\nu\in H_j\) with \(\gamma_\nu/j\) in those balls. Thus the rational polytope
\begin{equation*} Q=\conv\{\gamma_\nu/j\} \end{equation*}
contains \(c\) in its interior.
🔗
The intersection of \(Q\) with the hyperplane \(|u|_1=n/t'\) contains a relative neighborhood of \(c\) in this hyperplane. Choose rational points \(p_1,\ldots,p_b\) in that intersection whose convex hull contains \(c\) in its interior relative to this hyperplane. When \(n=1\text{,}\) the intersection is the singleton \(\{c\}\text{,}\) which has the required relative-interior property.
🔗
For each \(\ell\text{,}\) express \(p_\ell\) as a rational convex combination of the \(\gamma_\nu/j\text{,}\) and choose \(s_\nu\in R_j\) with those initial exponents. Write \(t'=a/b'\) in lowest terms. Choose \(Q_0\) divisible by \(a\) and by all denominators in the convex coefficients. Form \(r_\ell\) as a product of \(Q_0\) sections chosen from the \(s_\nu\text{,}\) with the multiplicities prescribed by that convex combination. The multiplicativity in Lemma 4.1 gives
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{in}(r_\ell)=jQ_0p_\ell. \end{equation*}
Set \(h=jQ_0/t'\text{,}\) an integer by the choice of \(Q_0\text{.}\) Then \(r_\ell\in H^0(X,jQ_0L)\) and \(|jQ_0p_\ell|_1=nh\text{.}\) Finally, division by \(h\) multiplies the convex hull of the \(p_\ell\) by \(t'\text{,}\) carrying \(c\) to \(\mathbf1\) and the slice hyperplane to \(|u|_1=n\text{.}\)
