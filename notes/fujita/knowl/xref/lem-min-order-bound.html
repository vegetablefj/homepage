Lemma 5.1.

If \(x\in c_X(v)\text{,}\) then every nonzero section \(s\) satisfies
\begin{equation} v(s)\leq\A(v)\mult_x(s).\tag{5.2} \end{equation}
In particular, for \(s\in R_m\setminus\{0\}\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} 0\leq\frac{v(s)}{\A(v)}\leq Cm, \end{equation*}
where \(C\) is the constant of Lemma 4.1.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a local equation \(g\) of an effective divisor at \(x\text{,}\) let \(r=\mult_x(g)\text{.}\) If \(r>0\text{,}\) we first prove
\begin{equation*} \lct_x(g)\geq1/r. \end{equation*}
Choose a general coordinate direction in which the initial homogeneous form is nonzero. Weierstrass preparation writes \(g\text{,}\) on a sufficiently small polydisc, as a unit times a monic polynomial of degree \(r\) in that coordinate. With the other coordinates fixed, factor this polynomial as \(\prod_{\ell=1}^r(z-a_\ell)\text{.}\) The roots remain uniformly bounded as the complementary coordinates vary.
🔗
For \(0<c<1/r\text{,}\) Hölder’s inequality gives
\begin{equation*} \int \prod_{\ell=1}^r|z-a_\ell|^{-2c}\,dV(z) \leq\prod_{\ell=1}^r \left(\int|z-a_\ell|^{-2cr}\,dV(z)\right)^{1/r}. \end{equation*}
The integrals on the right are uniformly finite: \(cr<1\text{,}\) and after translation by the root each integration domain lies inside a fixed disc centered at the origin. The absolute value of the unit factor is bounded above and away from zero. Fubini’s theorem then gives local integrability of \(|g|^{-2c}\text{.}\) Letting \(c\) tend to \(1/r\) proves the threshold bound. If \(r=0\text{,}\) \(g\) is a unit near \(x\) and the threshold is infinite.
🔗
For a divisorial order whose closed center contains \(x\text{,}\) the valuative threshold formula consequently gives \(v(g)\leq\A(v)r\text{.}\)

This includes orders along positive-dimensional centers containing \(x\text{.}\) For the corresponding estimate at the generic center of a valuation, see also [JM12, Proposition 5.10].
Rational approximation on a fixed monomial support proves the same inequality for all our tests. Applying (4.1) proves the final bound.
