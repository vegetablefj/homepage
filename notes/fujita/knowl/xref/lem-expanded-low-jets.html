Lemma 5.2.

Fix \(0<t_0<n+1\text{.}\) There are a constant \(\kappa\in(0,1)\) and an integer \(m_1\) such that for \(m\geq m_1\) and \(t\in[t_0,n+1]\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} \codim_{R_m}\{s:\mult_x(s)>m/(2t)\} \geq\kappa N_m.\tag{5.3} \end{equation}
The constants do not depend on \(m\) or \(t\) in these ranges.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose \(k\) such that \(kL\) is very ample. Take sections \(s_0,s_1,\ldots,s_n\) of \(kL\) for which \(s_0(x)\ne0\) and \(z_i=s_i/s_0\) are regular parameters at \(x\text{.}\) For each residue class \(r\) modulo \(k\text{,}\) choose a sufficiently large fixed integer \(b_r\) in that class such that \(b_rL\) is globally generated. Choose \(\tau_r\in H^0(X,b_rL)\) nonzero at \(x\text{.}\) Such \(b_r\) exist by ampleness; see [Stacks, Tag 01Q3, (7)]. For \(m\) in class \(r\text{,}\) write \(m=qk+b_r\text{.}\) For \(m\) sufficiently large, \(q\geq0\text{.}\)
🔗
For every multi-index \(\alpha\) with \(|\alpha|_1\leq q\text{,}\) the section
\begin{equation*} \tau_r s_0^{q-|\alpha|_1} s_1^{\alpha_1}\cdots s_n^{\alpha_n} \end{equation*}
has, in a frame of \(mL\text{,}\) germ equal to a common unit times \(z^\alpha\text{.}\) Let
\begin{equation*} r_m=\min\left(q, \left\lfloor\frac{m}{2(n+1)}\right\rfloor\right). \end{equation*}
The classes of these sections for \(|\alpha|_1\leq r_m\) are linearly independent in the quotient by sections of ordinary order greater than \(m/(2t)\text{.}\) Indeed, multiplication by the common unit preserves ordinary order, and a nonzero linear combination of the monomials has total degree at most \(r_m\leq m/(2t)\text{.}\) There are \(\binom{r_m+n}{n}\) such classes.
🔗
Because the choices \(b_r\) are fixed and finite in number, \(q=m/k+O(1)\) uniformly in \(r\text{.}\) Thus \(r_m\) is at least a fixed positive multiple of \(m\) for all large \(m\text{.}\) Comparing \(\binom{r_m+n}{n}\) with the Hilbert asymptotic (2.1) yields (5.3) for a fixed \(\kappa\in(0,1)\text{.}\)
