Definition 1.5.

A fibration from \(X\) is a proper surjective holomorphic map \(f:X\to B\) to a normal complex space, with \(f_*\cO_X=\cO_B\) and \(0<\dim B<2n\text{.}\) It is Lagrangian if every irreducible component of every fiber, with its reduced structure, has dimension \(n\) and the restriction of \(\sigma\) to its smooth locus is zero.
