Definition 5.5.

The Shafarevich–Tate group of \(f\) is
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{Sha}(f)=H^1(B,\mathcal A_f). \end{equation*}
For \(\phi\in\operatorname{Sha}(f)\text{,}\) the twist \(f^\phi:X^\phi\to B\) is obtained by gluing the restrictions \(X|_{B_i}\) by a cocycle representing \(\phi\text{.}\)
