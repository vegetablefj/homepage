Theorem 5.3.

There is a unique projective abelian scheme \(P\to U\) for which \(X_U\) is an analytic \(P\)-torsor. Its dual is
\begin{equation*} P^\vee\simeq\operatorname{Pic}^0(X_U/U). \end{equation*}
The family \(P\) has a distinguished primitive polarization. On a sufficiently small open subset of \(U\text{,}\) the action-angle description identifies it with \(T^*U/\Lambda\text{.}\)
