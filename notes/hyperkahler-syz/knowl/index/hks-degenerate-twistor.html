Theorem 5.7.

The closed complex two-forms
\begin{equation*} \sigma_t=\sigma+t f^*\beta,\qquad t\in\C, \end{equation*}
define integrable complex structures \(I_t\) on the underlying smooth manifold of \(X\text{,}\) with \(\sigma_t\) holomorphic symplectic. The map \(f:(X,I_t)\to\PP^n\) is holomorphic Lagrangian and has fibres isomorphic to those of \(f\text{.}\) This family is isomorphic over \(\PP^n\) to the family of twists obtained from \(H^1(B,f_*T_{X/B})\simeq\C\text{.}\)
