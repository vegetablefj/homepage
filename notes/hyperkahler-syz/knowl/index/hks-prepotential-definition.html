Since \(\tau\) is symmetric, the holomorphic one-form \(\sum_iw_i dz_i\) is closed. There is a local holomorphic function \(F\text{,}\) called a prepotential, such that
\begin{equation*} w_i=\frac{\partial F}{\partial z_i},\qquad \tau_{ij}=\frac{\partial^2F}{\partial z_i\partial z_j}. \end{equation*}
The closedness of the period forms makes \(\partial_k\tau_{ij}\) symmetric in all three indices. This is the local cubic condition on the period map. It is an extra condition on a family of polarized abelian varieties: a general such family, even with base dimension \(n\text{,}\) need not arise from a holomorphic Lagrangian system [Fre99, Remark 3.6].
