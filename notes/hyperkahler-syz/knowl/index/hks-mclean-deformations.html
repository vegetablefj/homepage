Theorem 7.9 (McLean).

Let \(F\) be a compact embedded special Lagrangian submanifold in a Calabi–Yau manifold. Its nearby special Lagrangian deformations, modulo reparametrization, form a smooth manifold. The tangent space is naturally the space of harmonic real one-forms on \(F\text{,}\) so its dimension is \(b_1(F)\text{.}\)
