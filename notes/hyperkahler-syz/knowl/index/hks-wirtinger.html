Lemma 2.4.

Let \(\omega_I\) be a Kähler form on a Hermitian vector space. On every real oriented \(2m\)-plane \(P\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} \left|\frac{\omega_I^m}{m!}\bigg|_P\right|\leq\vol_P. \end{equation*}
Equality holds exactly when \(P\) is invariant under \(I\text{.}\) For the complex orientation, the equality has positive sign.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The orthogonal projection of \(I|_P\) to \(P\) is skew-adjoint. Choose an orthonormal basis in which its two-dimensional blocks have coefficients \(a_1,\ldots,a_m\text{,}\) each of absolute value at most one. The coefficient of \(\omega_I^m/m!\) on the volume form is \(\prod_j a_j\text{.}\) Its absolute value is at most one. Equality means that the projection preserves the length of every vector in \(P\text{,}\) so \(I(P)\subset P\text{.}\) The converse follows from a unitary basis of a complex plane.
