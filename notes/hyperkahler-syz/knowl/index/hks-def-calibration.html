Definition 2.1.

A calibration on a Riemannian manifold is a closed real \(r\)-form \(\varphi\) such that \(\varphi(v_1,\ldots,v_r)\leq1\) for every positively oriented orthonormal \(r\)-frame. An oriented \(r\)-dimensional submanifold \(F\) is calibrated by \(\varphi\) if \(\varphi|_F=\vol_F\text{.}\)
