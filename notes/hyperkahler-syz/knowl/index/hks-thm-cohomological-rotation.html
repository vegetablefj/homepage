Theorem 2.5.

Let \(F\subset(M,J)\) be a compact connected special Lagrangian submanifold. The following conditions are equivalent:
  1. The underlying submanifold is \(I\)-holomorphic Lagrangian.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The restricted class \([\omega_K|_F]\) vanishes in \(H^2(F,\R)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. With the calibration orientation,
    \begin{equation*} \int_F(\omega_K+i\omega_I)^n=i^n\int_F\omega_I^n. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗

Proof.
If \(F\) is \(I\)-holomorphic Lagrangian, then \(\omega_K|_F=0\text{,}\) giving (ii). If (ii) holds, every term containing a factor \(\omega_K\) in the expansion of the left side of (iii) integrates to zero: the other factors are closed, and the restriction of \(\omega_K\) is exact. Thus (ii) implies (iii).
🔗
Suppose (iii) holds. Calibration gives
\begin{equation*} \vol(F)=\left|\int_F\Omega_J\right| =\left|\int_F\frac{\omega_I^n}{n!}\right|\leq\vol(F). \end{equation*}
Equality in the Wirtinger inequality implies that every tangent space of \(F\) is \(I\)-invariant. Thus \(F\) is an \(I\)-complex submanifold. Since \(F\) was Lagrangian for \(\omega_J\text{,}\) and
\begin{equation*} \omega_K(v,w)=-\omega_J(Iv,w), \end{equation*}
the form \(\omega_K\) also vanishes on its tangent bundle. Hence \(\sigma_I|_F=(\omega_J+i\omega_K)|_F=0\text{,}\) proving (i).
