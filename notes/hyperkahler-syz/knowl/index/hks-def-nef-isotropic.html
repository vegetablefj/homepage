Definition 1.1.

A holomorphic line bundle \(L\) on a compact Kähler manifold is nef if \(c_1(L)\) belongs to the closure of the Kähler cone. On an IHS manifold it is isotropic if \(q_X(c_1(L))=0\text{.}\) Unless stated otherwise, a nef isotropic line bundle in these notes has \(c_1(L)\ne0\text{.}\)
