Theorem 5.13.

Let \(f:X\to B\) be a Lagrangian fibration with smooth base. Suppose \(X\) is of \(\mathrm{K3}^{[n]}\)-, generalized Kummer, OG6, or OG10 type. The group scheme \(\mathcal K\to U\) extends to a constant finite group scheme acting on \(X\text{.}\) Its group \(G\) preserves \(f\) and acts trivially on \(H^2(X,\Z)\text{.}\) The quotient
\begin{equation*} \check f:\check X=X/G\longrightarrow B \end{equation*}
is a compact hyperkähler orbifold with a Lagrangian fibration. Over \(U\) it is the quotient \(X_U/\mathcal K\text{.}\)
