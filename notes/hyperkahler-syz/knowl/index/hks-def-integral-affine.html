Definition 8.2.

An integral affine structure on a real \(r\)-manifold is an atlas whose coordinate changes are \(q\mapsto Aq+b\text{,}\) with \(A\in\mathrm{GL}(r,\Z)\) and \(b\in\R^r\text{.}\) Equivalently, it is a full lattice in the real cotangent bundle locally generated by closed one-forms.
