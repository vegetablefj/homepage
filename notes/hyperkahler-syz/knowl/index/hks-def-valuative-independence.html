Definition 9.2.

Let \(V\subset H^0(Y_K,\mathcal A_K^{\otimes \ell})\) be a finite-dimensional \(K\)-vector space. A basis \(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_N\) is valuatively independent on the essential skeleton if, for every \(x\in\operatorname{Sk}(\mathcal Y^*)\) and every \(a_1,\ldots,a_N\in K\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} v_x\left(\sum_{j=1}^N a_j\theta_j\right) =\min_{j:a_j\ne0}\bigl(v_K(a_j)+v_x(\theta_j)\bigr).\tag{9.1} \end{equation}
The value of the zero section and the minimum over the empty set are \(+\infty\text{.}\)
