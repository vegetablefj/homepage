Theorem 3.7 (OpenAI).

Let \(M\) be of IHS type, fix a connected component of its marked Teichmüller space, and let \(e\in H^2(M,\Z)\) be primitive, nonzero, and isotropic. There is a semistable projective degeneration \(\pi:\mathcal Y\to\Delta\) of IHS manifolds of dimension \(d=2n\text{,}\) obtained from a family over an algebraic curve, with the following properties.
  1. Its smooth fibers belong to the chosen marked component. A line bundle \(\mathcal A\) on \(\mathcal Y\) restricts to an ample bundle of class \(kh\) on every smooth fiber, for a positive integer \(k\) and a positive integral \(h\perp e\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. There are rational \(P,e,f,v\) as in (3.1). The normalized marked period is \(p(\tau)\) in (3.2), and the full monodromy on \(H^2(M,\Q)\) is \(U_w\) in (3.3) for some \(w\in\Q_{>0}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. There is a single-valued relative holomorphic symplectic form \(\sigma_t\) with \(q([\sigma_t],e)=1\text{.}\) The form \(\eta_t=\sigma_t^n\) extends to the logarithmic relative canonical bundle of the semistable model. For \(s=-\log|t|\text{,}\) there are constants \(0<c\leq C\) such that
    \begin{equation*} c s^d\leq\int_{Y_t}|\eta_t|^2\leq C s^d \end{equation*}
    for all sufficiently small \(t\ne0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. At some maximal crossing \(o\text{,}\) there are coordinates in which
    \begin{equation} t=z_0\cdots z_d,\qquad \eta_t=G(z)\frac{dz_1}{z_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\frac{dz_d}{z_d}, \qquad G(o)\ne0.\tag{3.6} \end{equation}
    The functions \(z_1,\ldots,z_d\) can be taken to be restrictions of rational functions on the algebraic total space.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. For any prescribed irrational \(\alpha\in\R\text{,}\) a path approaching the puncture can be chosen with \(\tau=\alpha+iy\) and \(y\to+\infty\text{.}\)
