Proposition 3.2.

Suppose that a projective degeneration of IHS manifolds of dimension \(2n\) has type III monodromy and
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{im}N_2^2=\Q e. \end{equation*}
Then \(q_X(e)=0\text{,}\) and
\begin{equation*} W_0H^2_{\lim}=\Q e,\qquad W_0H^{2n}_{\lim}=\Q e^n. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a nilpotent operator with \(N_2^3=0\text{,}\) the monodromy filtration centered at two has \(W_0=\operatorname{im}N_2^2\text{.}\) Skew-adjointness gives
\begin{equation*} q_X(N_2^2x,N_2^2y)=q_X(x,N_2^4y)=0, \end{equation*}
so \(e\) is isotropic.
🔗
The cup-product theorem of Verbitsky identifies \(\Sym^nH^2(Y_t)\) with its image \(\SH^{2n}(Y_t)\subseteq H^{2n}(Y_t)\text{;}\) see The cohomology algebra in General HK. This is an inclusion of variations of Hodge structure. Since the limiting \(H^2\) is effective and its weight-zero part is \(\Q e\text{,}\) the weight-zero part of its \(n\)th symmetric power is exactly \(\Q e^n\text{.}\)
🔗
A polarization splits off the subvariation \(\SH^{2n}\text{.}\) Its complement has no Hodge summands of type \((2n,0)\) or \((0,2n)\text{:}\) the line generated by the \(n\)th power of a holomorphic symplectic form already lies in \(\SH^{2n}\text{.}\) All Hodge bidegrees of the complement therefore have both coordinates at least one. Twisting this complement by \((1)\) gives an effective variation. Its limit mixed Hodge structure remains effective, so its weights are nonnegative. Before this twist, the weights are at least two. The complement contributes nothing to \(W_0H^{2n}_{\lim}\text{.}\) This proves the assertion; compare [EM26, Lemma 3.3].
