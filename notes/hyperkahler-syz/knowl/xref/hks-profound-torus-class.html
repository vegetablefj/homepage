Proposition 8.4.

In the situation of Proposition 8.2, the Poincaré dual of every profound torus satisfies
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{PD}[F_{t,o}]\in W_0H^{2n}_{\lim}=\Q e^n. \end{equation*}
If \(F_{t,o}\) is isotopic to a special Lagrangian torus \(F\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{PD}[F]=c e^n\qquad\text{for some }c\in\Q\setminus\{0\}. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \(S_d\) be the logarithmic monodromy on \(H_d(Y_t)\text{,}\) where \(d=2n\text{.}\) The topological theorem of Clemens, in [Har19, Theorems 3.5 and 3.8], gives
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{im}S_d^d =\operatorname{span}_\Q\{[F_{t,o}]:o\text{ is a maximal crossing}\}. \end{equation*}
In particular, each such torus class lies in this image. Poincaré duality intertwines the monodromy actions, up to taking the inverse, which does not change the image of a logarithmic power. It sends \(\operatorname{im}S_d^d\) to \(\operatorname{im}N_d^d=W_0H^d_{\lim}\text{.}\) Equivalently, its source has homological weight \(-2d=-4n\) and Poincaré duality shifts the weight by \(4n\text{.}\) Apply Proposition 8.2.
🔗
An isotopy preserves the homology class, up to the chosen orientation. For the positive calibration orientation of a compact special Lagrangian \(F\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} \left|\int_F\Omega_J\right|=\vol_g(F)>0. \end{equation*}
A null-homologous cycle would have zero integral against every closed form. Thus its class, and the coefficient \(c\text{,}\) are nonzero.
