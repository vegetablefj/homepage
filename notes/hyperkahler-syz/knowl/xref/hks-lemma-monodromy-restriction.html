Lemma 7.8.

Let \(\pi:\mathcal Y\to S^1\) be a smooth fiber bundle with compact fibers, and let \(F\) be a compact smooth manifold. Suppose there is a smooth map \(j:F\times S^1\to\mathcal Y\) over \(S^1\) whose restriction to each fiber is an embedding. Fix a base point, write \(Y\) for its fiber, \(i:F\hookrightarrow Y\) for the embedding, and \(T\) for the cohomological monodromy. Then
\begin{equation*} i^*\circ T=i^*,\qquad \operatorname{im}(T-\id)\subset\ker\bigl(i^*:H^k(Y,\R)\to H^k(F,\R)\bigr) \end{equation*}
for every \(k\text{.}\) If \(T\) is unipotent, then \(\operatorname{im}(\log T)=\operatorname{im}(T-\id)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Pullback along \(j\) is a map from the local system \(R^k\pi_*\R\) to the constant local system \(H^k(F,\R)\text{.}\) It commutes with transport around the circle, and transport in the target is the identity. Thus \(i^*T=i^*\text{.}\) This argument can also be seen by trivializing the bundle over an interval: the two endpoint embeddings differ by the bundle monodromy and are homotopic through the given parametrized embeddings.
🔗
Put \(U=T-\id\text{.}\) If \(U\) is nilpotent, then
\begin{equation*} \log T=U\left(\id-\frac{U}{2}+\frac{U^2}{3}-\cdots\right). \end{equation*}
The second factor is a finite polynomial in \(U\) with constant term one. It is invertible and commutes with \(U\text{,}\) proving equality of the two images.
