Theorem 6.3.

Let \(f_0:X_0\to B\) be a projective Lagrangian fibration from a compact IHS manifold, with \(B\) projective, and let \(\lambda=c_1(f_0^*H_B)\) for an ample bundle \(H_B\text{.}\) In every sufficiently small neighbourhood of \(X_0\) in \(\Def(X_0,\lambda)\text{,}\) there is a projective deformation \(X'\) carrying Lagrangian fibrations \(f':X'\to B\) and \(g:X'\to C\text{.}\) Under the marking, \(c_1((f')^*H_B)=\lambda\text{.}\) For an ample bundle \(H_C\) on \(C\text{,}\) the class \(\mu=c_1(g^*H_C)\) satisfies \(q_{X'}(\lambda,\mu)>0\text{.}\)
