Proposition 6.2.

Let \(X\) be projective IHS of dimension \(2n\text{,}\) and let \(f:X\to B\) and \(g:X\to C\) be Lagrangian fibrations with normal projective bases. Choose ample line bundles \(H_B,H_C\text{,}\) and set \(\lambda=c_1(f^*H_B)\) and \(\mu=c_1(g^*H_C)\text{.}\) If \(q_X(\lambda,\mu)>0\text{,}\) then for every smooth fibre \(A\) of \(g\text{,}\) the restriction \(f|_A:A\to B\) is finite and surjective.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Both classes are nef and isotropic. Compare the coefficient of \(t^n\) in the Fujiki identity for \(\lambda+t\mu\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \binom{2n}{n}\int_X\lambda^n\mu^n=c_X\,2^nq_X(\lambda,\mu)^n>0. \end{equation*}
Intersecting \(n\) general divisors in sufficiently high powers of \(H_C\) shows that
\begin{equation*} \mu^n=\left(\int_Cc_1(H_C)^n\right)[A]. \end{equation*}
Therefore \(\int_A(\lambda|_A)^n>0\text{.}\)
🔗
A nef line bundle on an abelian variety with positive top self-intersection is ample. To see this, represent its real first Chern class by the unique translation-invariant real \((1,1)\)-form. Approximation by ample classes makes its Hermitian matrix positive semidefinite. Positive top self-intersection makes the determinant positive, so every eigenvalue is positive. The line bundle has positive curvature and is ample.
🔗
Apply this to \(f^*H_B|_A\text{.}\) A positive-dimensional fibre of \(f|_A\) would contain a curve, whose degree against this ample bundle would be zero. Thus \(f|_A\) is proper with finite fibres and is finite. Its image is closed of dimension \(n=\dim B\text{,}\) so it equals \(B\text{.}\)
