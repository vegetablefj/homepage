Proposition 1.7.

Let \(L\) be a nonzero nef isotropic line bundle on \(X\text{.}\) If \(L\) is semiample, then some power has the form \(L^{\otimes r}\cong f^*A\text{,}\) where \(f:X\to B\) is a Lagrangian fibration and \(A\) is ample on \(B\text{.}\) Conversely, the pullback of an ample line bundle under a fibration from \(X\) is nonzero, nef, isotropic, and semiample.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose \(r>0\) such that \(L^{\otimes r}\) is generated by global sections. Its morphism to projective space has Stein factorization \(X\xrightarrow{f}B\to\PP^N\text{.}\) The second map is finite onto the image. Thus \(B\) is normal projective, \(f_*\cO_X=\cO_B\text{,}\) and \(L^{\otimes r}=f^*A\) for an ample line bundle \(A\) on \(B\text{.}\)
🔗
Let \(d=\dim B\text{.}\) A positive multiple of \(c_1(f^*A)\) is represented by the pullback of a Fubini–Study form after an embedding of \(B\text{.}\) It is semipositive, has rank \(d\) at general points, and its power \(d+1\) vanishes. Consequently its numerical dimension is \(d\text{.}\) By Proposition 1.3, we obtain \(d=n\text{.}\) In particular \(f\) is a fibration, and Theorem 1.6 applies.
🔗
Conversely, let \(f:X\to B\) be a fibration and \(A\) ample. Pulling back a sufficiently high very ample power shows semiampleness and nefness of \(f^*A\text{.}\) Its first Chern class has positive intersection with \(\omega^{2n-1}\text{,}\) hence is nonzero. Since \(\dim B<2n\text{,}\) its top power vanishes; the Fujiki relation gives \(q_X(c_1(f^*A))=0\text{.}\)
