Proposition 5.4.

The line bundle \(E_Z\) on \(Z\) with \(c_1(E_Z)=e\) is semiample.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Existence of \(E_Z\) follows because the integral class \(e\) has type \((1,1)\text{;}\) uniqueness follows from \(H^1(Z,\cO_Z)=0\text{.}\) Apply Theorem 5.3. Choose an ample bundle \(H\) on \(B\) and let \(A=f^*H\text{,}\) with \(b=c_1(A)\text{.}\) A positive power of \(A\) is globally generated, so \(A\) is semiample and nef.
🔗
The class \(b\) is nonzero. For example, the pullback of a Fubini–Study representative for a sufficiently high power of \(H\) is semipositive and has positive trace on the open set where \(f\) has positive rank. Its integral against a Kähler form to the power \(2n-1\) is positive. Since \(\dim B=n\text{,}\) we have \(b^{2n}=0\text{.}\) Fujiki’s identity implies \(q(b)=0\text{.}\) By Proposition 5.2, \(b=ke\) for an integer \(k\ne0\text{.}\)
🔗
Both \(b\) and \(e\) pair positively with \([\omega_{I'}]\text{:}\) for \(b\) this is the positivity of a nonzero nef null vector, and for \(e\) it is Proposition 5.1. Thus \(k>0\text{.}\) Injectivity of the first Chern class map gives
\begin{equation*} A\simeq E_Z^{\otimes k}. \end{equation*}
A globally generated positive power of \(A\) is a globally generated positive power of \(E_Z\text{.}\)
