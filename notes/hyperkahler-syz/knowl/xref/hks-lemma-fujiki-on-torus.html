Lemma 7.6.

Let \(X\) be an IHS \(2n\)-fold and let \(F\) be an oriented smooth real \(2n\)-dimensional submanifold. Suppose
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{PD}[F]=c\lambda^n,\qquad q_X(\lambda)=0, \end{equation*}
with \(\lambda\in H^2(X,\R)\) and \(c\in\R\text{.}\) Then, for every \(\alpha\in H^2(X,\C)\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} \int_F\alpha^n= c\,\frac{2^nc_X}{\binom{2n}{n}}q_X(\lambda,\alpha)^n.\tag{7.1} \end{equation}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Take the coefficient of \(t^n\) in
\begin{equation*} \int_X(\alpha+t\lambda)^{2n} =c_X\bigl(q_X(\alpha)+2tq_X(\lambda,\alpha)\bigr)^n. \end{equation*}
The coefficient on the left is \(\binom{2n}{n}\int_X\lambda^n\alpha^n\text{;}\) the coefficient on the right is \(2^nc_Xq_X(\lambda,\alpha)^n\text{.}\) Multiply by \(c\) and use Poincaré duality. The identity for complex classes follows by complexifying the polynomial identity for real classes.
