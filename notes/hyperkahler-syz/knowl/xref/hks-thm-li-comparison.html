Theorem 9.6.

Let \((\mathcal Y^*,\mathcal A)\to\Delta^*\) be a polarized meromorphic maximal degeneration of Calabi–Yau manifolds, with a nowhere vanishing total holomorphic volume form inducing the fibrewise forms \(\Omega_t\text{.}\)
  1. If every \(H^0(Y_K,\mathcal A_K^{\otimes\ell})\) admits a valuatively independent basis, the weak comparison property holds after semistable reduction.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If the weak comparison property holds for a semistable model, then for every \(0<\delta<1\) and all sufficiently small \(t\ne0\text{,}\) an open subset \(V_{t,\delta}\subset Y_t\) admits a special Lagrangian real \(d\)-torus fibration and satisfies \(\mu_t(V_{t,\delta})\ge1-\delta\text{.}\) The Calabi–Yau structure is \((s^{-1}\omega_t^{(0)},\Omega_t)\text{.}\)
