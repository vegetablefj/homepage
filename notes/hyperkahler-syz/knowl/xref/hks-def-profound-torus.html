Definition 8.3.

At a point where \(d+1\) components of a semistable central fiber meet, choose coordinates with \(t=z_0\cdots z_d\text{.}\) A profound torus in \(Y_t\) is an embedded real \(d\)-torus in the isotopy class of
\begin{equation*} F_{t,o}=\{\,|z_j|=r_j\ (0\leq j\leq d),\ z_0\cdots z_d=t\,\},\qquad \prod_{j=0}^d r_j=|t|, \end{equation*}
where all radii are small enough to stay in the coordinate chart.
