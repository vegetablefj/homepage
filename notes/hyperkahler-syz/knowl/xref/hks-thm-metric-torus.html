Theorem 4.8.

Let \(\pi:\mathcal Y\to\Delta\) be the restriction of a projective algebraic family over a smooth curve. Assume that \(\mathcal Y\) is smooth, \(Y_0\) is reduced with simple normal crossings, and each \(Y_t\) for \(t\ne0\) is a smooth connected projective Calabi–Yau manifold of dimension \(d\ge2\text{.}\) Let \(\mathcal A\) be a line bundle on \(\mathcal Y\) whose restriction to every \(Y_t\text{,}\) \(t\ne0\text{,}\) is ample. Suppose that the nowhere vanishing forms \(\Omega_t\) extend as a section of the relative logarithmic canonical bundle and satisfy
\begin{equation*} c\,s^d\le\int_{Y_t}|\Omega_t|^2\le C\,s^d, \qquad s=-\log|t|, \end{equation*}
for constants \(c,C>0\text{.}\) Suppose that, at a point \(o\in Y_0\text{,}\) exactly \(d+1\) components meet and
\begin{equation*} z_0\cdots z_d=t,\qquad \Omega_t=f(z)\frac{dz_1}{z_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge\frac{dz_d}{z_d}, \qquad f(o)\ne0. \end{equation*}
Assume that \(z_1,\ldots,z_d\) are restrictions of rational functions on the algebraic total space. For every sufficiently small \(t\ne0\text{,}\) the Ricci-flat metric in \(c_1(\mathcal A_t)\) has an embedded special Lagrangian real \(d\)-torus \(F\subset Y_t\) isotopic in \(Y_t\) to an angular coordinate torus \(F_{t,o}\) in this chart.
