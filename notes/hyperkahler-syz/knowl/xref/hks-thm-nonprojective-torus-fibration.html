Theorem 10.3 (Greb–Lehn–Rollenske).

Let \(Z\) be a nonprojective compact IHS manifold of dimension \(2n\) containing a holomorphic Lagrangian complex torus \(F\text{.}\) There is a holomorphic algebraic reduction \(f:Z\to B\) which is a Lagrangian fibration with \(F\) as a fiber. The base can be chosen normal and projective, and has dimension \(n\text{.}\)
