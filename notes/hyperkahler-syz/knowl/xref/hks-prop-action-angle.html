Proposition 3.1.

After shrinking \(U\) about any point, there is a holomorphic Lagrangian section of \(f\) and a holomorphic symplectic isomorphism
\begin{equation*} Z\simeq T^*U/\Lambda \end{equation*}
over \(U\text{.}\) Each local section of \(\Lambda\subset T^*U\) is a closed holomorphic one-form. The holomorphic symplectic form on the quotient is induced by \(\sigma_{\rm can}=-d\vartheta\text{,}\) where \(\vartheta\) is the tautological one-form.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose a local holomorphic section \(s\) of the submersion. Exponentiating the vertical fields gives a local covering \(\Phi:T^*U\to Z\) whose kernel in each fibre is \(\Lambda\text{.}\) The contraction identity for \(V_\alpha\) gives
\begin{equation*} \Phi^*\sigma=\sigma_{\rm can}+\pi^*(s^*\sigma), \end{equation*}
where \(\pi:T^*U\to U\) is the projection. Both sides have the same contraction with every vertical vector, so their difference is pulled back from the base; restriction to the zero section determines that difference.
🔗
Translation by a local lattice section \(\alpha\) does not change \(\Phi\text{.}\) On the cotangent bundle, that translation sends \(\sigma_{\rm can}\) to \(\sigma_{\rm can}-\pi^*d\alpha\text{.}\) Thus \(d\alpha=0\text{.}\) The closed holomorphic two-form \(s^*\sigma\) is locally \(d\gamma\) by the holomorphic Poincaré lemma. Replacing \(s\) by its translate by \(\gamma\) makes its pullback of \(\sigma\) zero. The covering therefore descends to the asserted symplectic isomorphism.
