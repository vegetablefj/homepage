Theorem 10.5 (Soldatenkov–Verbitsky).

The space \(\mathcal T^\circ_e\) is connected. If \(\mathcal T^\circ_{e,\mathrm{sa}}\ne\varnothing\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \mathcal T^\circ_{e,\mathrm{nef}}=\mathcal T^\circ_{e,\mathrm{sa}}. \end{equation*}
In a smooth connected family of compact IHS manifolds carrying a line bundle, if one member is semiample and another member is nef and isotropic, the latter line bundle is semiample.
