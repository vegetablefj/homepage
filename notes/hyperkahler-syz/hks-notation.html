    Appendix A. Notation

    \(X,M,2n\)
    An IHS manifold, its underlying smooth manifold, and its dimension. A general Calabi–Yau degeneration has dimension \(d\text{;}\) the hyperkähler application has \(d=2n\text{.}\) See the conventions.
    \(q_X,c_X\)
    The primitive integral BBF bilinear form and the Fujiki constant in \(\int_X\alpha^{2n}=c_Xq_X(\alpha)^n\text{.}\) We write \(q_X(\alpha)=q_X(\alpha,\alpha)\text{.}\)
    \(L,\lambda,e\)
    The specified nef isotropic line bundle, its first Chern class, and the positive primitive integral generator of its ray; \(\lambda=m e\) for an integer \(m>0\text{.}\) See Section 1.1.
    \(\nu(L),\kappa(L)\)
    Numerical dimension and Iitaka dimension, defined in Definition 1.2. For nonzero nef isotropic \(L\text{,}\) \(\nu(L)=n\text{;}\) semiampleness gives \(\kappa(L)=n\text{.}\)
    \(g,I,J,K;\ \omega_A\)
    A hyperkähler metric, a quaternionic triple, and \(\omega_A(u,v)=g(Au,v)\text{.}\) Thus \(IJ=K\text{,}\) \(\sigma_I=\omega_J+i\omega_K\text{,}\) and \(\sigma_J=\omega_K+i\omega_I\text{.}\) See Section 2.2.
    \(\Omega_J,\theta\)
    The normalized volume form \(\sigma_J^n/n!\) and the phase of a special Lagrangian submanifold, measured by its positive calibration. See Definition 2.2.
    \(F,F_{t,o},\operatorname{PD}[F]\)
    A special Lagrangian or holomorphic Lagrangian torus, an angular coordinate torus near a maximal crossing, and its Poincaré dual with a specified orientation. See Definition 3.3 and Proposition 3.4.
    \(T_k,N_k,W_\bullet\)
    Monodromy on \(H^k\text{,}\) its logarithm after unipotent base change, and the limiting weight filtration centered at \(k\text{.}\) In degree two the prescribed degeneration has \(\operatorname{im}N_2^2=\Q e\text{.}\) See Section 3.1.
    \(h,P,e,f,v,a\)
    The polarization class, a rational ternary space of signature \((2,1)\text{,}\) and a basis with \(q(e,f)=1\text{,}\) \(q(e)=q(f)=0\text{,}\) \(v\perp\langle e,f\rangle\text{,}\) and \(a=q(v)>0\text{.}\) These are local to the period construction in Section 3.2.
    \(p(\tau),\tau=\alpha+iy\)
    The normalized period \(f+\tau v-a\tau^2e/2\text{.}\) The real part \(\alpha\) is chosen irrational in the main proof; \(y\to\infty\) approaches the prescribed rational boundary point.
    \(s,\mu_t\)
    The parameter \(s=-\log|t|\) and the probability measure obtained by normalizing \(|\Omega_t|^2\text{.}\) See Section 4.1. A measure estimate uses this measure, whereas metric convergence requires a specified rescaling.
    \(Y_K^{\mathrm{an}},\operatorname{Sk}(\mathcal Y^*)\)
    The analytification of the generic fiber over \(K=\C((t))\) and its essential skeleton. Valuative independence and weak comparison are defined in Definition 4.2 and Definition 4.5.
    \(\mathcal T^\circ_e\)
    The positive locus in a fixed marked Teichmüller component on which \(e\) is of type \((1,1)\text{.}\) Its nef and semiample loci are compared in Theorem 5.5.
