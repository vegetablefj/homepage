Proposition 3.1.

Let \(d\geq2\text{.}\) If a smooth complex projective \(d\)-fold has a Hodge atom which occurs on no smooth projective variety of dimension at most \(d-2\text{,}\) then it is irrational.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
A weak factorization to \(\mathbb P^d\) would express the difference of their atomic compositions as a signed sum of atomic compositions of the centers. Every such contribution comes from dimension at most \(d-2\text{.}\) The atoms of projective space come from points. The asserted atom therefore cannot occur in a rational \(d\)-fold. This is the criterion of [KKPY26, Proposition 5.30].
