Theorem 6.1.

There is an integer \(d_0\) such that, for every admissible discriminant \(d>d_0\text{,}\) a very general \(X\in\mathcal C_d\) is irrational and has an exact \(\C\)-linear equivalence
\begin{equation*} \Ku(X)\simeq D^b(S) \end{equation*}
for a smooth projective K3 surface \(S\text{.}\)
