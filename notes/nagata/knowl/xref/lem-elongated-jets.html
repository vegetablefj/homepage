Lemma 11.2.

Let \(n,r\geq1\) and \(\ell\geq0\) be integers. Let \(c_1,\ldots,c_r\in k^\times\) be distinct, and define
\begin{equation*} E_{r,\ell}=\left\{(\beta,b)\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}^{n-1}\times\mathbb Z_{\geq0}:|\beta|+\frac br<\ell+1\right\},\qquad |\beta|=\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\beta_j. \end{equation*}
Then \(\mathcal M(E_{r,\ell})\) separates jets through degree \(\ell\) at
\begin{equation*} q_i=(1,\ldots,1,c_i)\in\mathbb T_k^n,\qquad 1\leq i\leq r. \end{equation*}
For \(n=1\text{,}\) the tuple \(\beta\) is empty and \(|\beta|=0\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Write the coordinates as \((x',Y)\text{,}\) with \(x'=(x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1})\text{.}\) A prescribed jet through degree \(\ell\) at \(q_i\) has a unique expression
\begin{equation*} \sum_{|\alpha|\leq\ell}(x'-1)^\alpha h_{i,\alpha}(Y-c_i),\qquad\deg h_{i,\alpha}\leq\ell-|\alpha|. \end{equation*}
For each \(\alpha\text{,}\) the ideals \((Y-c_i)^{\ell+1-|\alpha|}\) are pairwise comaximal. Their product is generated by a monic polynomial of degree \(r(\ell+1-|\alpha|)\text{.}\) The Chinese remainder theorem therefore gives a polynomial \(f_\alpha(Y)\) such that
\begin{equation*} f_\alpha(Y)\equiv h_{i,\alpha}(Y-c_i)\pmod{(Y-c_i)^{\ell+1-|\alpha|}}\qquad(1\leq i\leq r) \end{equation*}
and \(\deg f_\alpha<r(\ell+1-|\alpha|)\text{.}\)
🔗
The polynomial
\begin{equation*} f(x',Y)=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq\ell}(x'-1)^\alpha f_\alpha(Y) \end{equation*}
has all the prescribed jets. Every monomial \((x')^\beta Y^b\) in its \(\alpha\)-summand satisfies \(\beta_j\leq\alpha_j\) for every \(j\) and \(b<r(\ell+1-|\alpha|)\text{.}\) Hence
\begin{equation*} |\beta|+\frac br\leq|\alpha|+\frac br<\ell+1. \end{equation*}
Thus \(f\in\mathcal M(E_{r,\ell})\text{.}\) The proof uses coefficients of powers of local parameters, so it applies in every characteristic.
