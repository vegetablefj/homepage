Proposition 11.3.

Let \(n\geq2\) and \(r\geq1\) be integers, let \(w>0\text{,}\) and let
\begin{equation*} P=\Delta(w,\ldots,w,rw). \end{equation*}
Suppose that finite sets \(F_N\subset NP\cap\mathbb Z^n\text{,}\) for every integer \(N\geq1\text{,}\) satisfy
\begin{equation*} F_N+F_{N'}\subset F_{N+N'}\qquad(N,N'\geq1) \end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*} \#F_N=\operatorname{vol}(P)N^n+o(N^n). \end{equation*}
For every real \(0<\theta<w\) and every choice of distinct \(c_1,\ldots,c_r\in k^\times\text{,}\) the space \(\mathcal M(F_N)\) separates jets through degree \(\lfloor N\theta\rfloor\) at the points \((1,\ldots,1,c_i)\) for all sufficiently large \(N\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose \(\lambda\) with \(\theta/w<\lambda<1\text{.}\) Choose a vector \(a\in\mathbb R_{>0}^n\) satisfying
\begin{equation*} \frac{a_1+\cdots+a_{n-1}}w+\frac{a_n}{rw}<1-\lambda. \end{equation*}
Then \(a+\lambda P\subset\operatorname{int}P\text{.}\) Choose \(a_N\in N^{-1}\mathbb Z^n\) with \(a_N\to a\text{.}\) For large \(N\text{,}\) all translated simplices \(a_N+\lambda P\) lie in a single compact subset \(K\) of \(\operatorname{int}P\text{.}\) By Lemma 11.1,
\begin{equation*} Na_N+(N\lambda P\cap\mathbb Z^n)\subset F_N \end{equation*}
for all sufficiently large \(N\text{.}\)
🔗

Aside

Contraction and positive translation place the interpolation simplex in a compact subset of the interior. The interior-filling lemma applies there. The translation becomes a Laurent monomial factor. This factor is a unit at every torus point, so it gives an automorphism of each jet algebra.
🔗
Let \(\ell=\lfloor N\theta\rfloor\text{.}\) Since \(\theta<\lambda w\text{,}\) we have \(\ell+1\leq N\lambda w\) for large \(N\text{.}\) For every \((\beta,b)\in E_{r,\ell}\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} \frac{|\beta|}{\lambda w}+\frac{b}{r\lambda w}<\frac{\ell+1}{\lambda w}\leq N. \end{equation*}
Thus \(E_{r,\ell}\subset N\lambda P\cap\mathbb Z^n\text{,}\) and Lemma 11.2 proves jet separation by the monomials with these exponents. Multiplication by the Laurent monomial of exponent \(Na_N\in\mathbb Z^n\) induces an isomorphism of each jet algebra at a torus point, because this monomial is a unit there. The translated monomials belong to \(\mathcal M(F_N)\text{,}\) which proves the required surjectivity.
