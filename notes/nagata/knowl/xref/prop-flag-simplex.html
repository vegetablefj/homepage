Proposition 9.1.

Choose an integer \(D\geq1\) such that \(DL\) is very ample, and let
\begin{equation*} P_0=\Delta(1/D,\ldots,1/D,D^{n-1}V). \end{equation*}
There are finite sets \(S_N\subset NP_0\cap\mathbb Z_{\geq0}^n\text{,}\) for all integers \(N\geq1\text{,}\) such that
\begin{equation*} S_N+S_{N'}\subset S_{N+N'}\qquad(N,N'\geq1). \end{equation*}
Their cardinalities satisfy
\begin{equation*} \#S_N=h^0(X,NL)=\frac{V}{n!}N^n+O(N^{n-1}). \end{equation*}
For every \(N,r\geq1\) and \(\ell\geq0\text{,}\) if \(\mathcal M(S_N)\) separates jets through degree \(\ell\) at some \(r\) distinct points of \(\mathbb T_k^n\text{,}\) then \(H^0(X,NL)\) separates jets through degree \(\ell\) at some \(r\) distinct points of \(X\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose sections \(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}\in H^0(X,DL)\) whose successive zero loci form a smooth integral flag
\begin{equation*} X=X_0\supset X_1\supset\cdots\supset X_{n-1}=C,\qquad X_i=X_{i-1}\cap\{\sigma_i=0\}. \end{equation*}
Bertini’s theorem for a very ample embedding gives such sections in every characteristic. At each step the variety being cut has dimension at least two. The smooth integral hyperplane sections are [Sta] (Tag 0G4G).
🔗
Choose \(p\in C(k)\) and a local frame \(e\) of \(L\) near \(p\text{.}\) The functions \(z_i=\sigma_i/e^D\text{,}\) for \(1\leq i<n\text{,}\) form part of a regular system of parameters at \(p\text{.}\) Complete them by \(z_n\text{.}\) These parameters give
\begin{equation*} \widehat{\mathcal O}_{X,p}=k[[z_1,\ldots,z_n]]. \end{equation*}
Write each section of \(NL\) in the frame \(e^N\text{,}\) expand it in this ring, and let \(S_N\) be the set of least exponents of nonzero expansions for the lexicographic order that compares the first coordinate first.
🔗
The expansion map is injective. The local ring injects into its completion, and a section with zero germ vanishes on a nonempty open subset of the integral variety \(X\text{,}\) hence vanishes on \(X\text{.}\) The frames in different degrees are compatible with multiplication. By Lemma 2.3,
\begin{equation*} \#S_N=h^0(X,NL),\qquad S_N+S_{N'}\subset S_{N+N'}. \end{equation*}
The Hilbert polynomial of the ample line bundle gives the stated asymptotic formula.
🔗
Let \(\alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n)\in S_N\) be the least exponent of a nonzero section \(s\text{.}\) We prove the simplex bound by division along the flag. Suppose that a nonzero residual section on \(X_{i-1}\) has least exponent \((\alpha_i,\ldots,\alpha_n)\) in its formal expansion at \(p\text{.}\) If \(\alpha_i>0\text{,}\) every term of its expansion is divisible by \(z_i\text{.}\) Its restriction to \(X_i\) therefore has zero formal expansion. The local ring of \(X_i\) injects into its completion, and \(X_i\) is integral, so this restriction is identically zero. The exact sequence for the effective Cartier divisor \(X_i\subset X_{i-1}\) permits division by \(\sigma_i|_{X_{i-1}}\text{.}\)
🔗
Repeat this division \(\alpha_i\) times and restrict to \(X_i\text{.}\) The coefficient of the original least term remains nonzero, so the resulting section is nonzero and has least exponent \((\alpha_{i+1},\ldots,\alpha_n)\text{.}\) Each division subtracts \(DL\) from its line bundle. Induction gives a nonzero section on \(C\) of
\begin{equation*} \left(N-D\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\alpha_i\right)L|_C \end{equation*}
with vanishing order exactly \(\alpha_n\) at \(p\text{.}\) Since \(\deg(L|_C)=D^{n-1}V\text{,}\) the degree of its zero divisor gives
\begin{equation*} \alpha_n\leq\left(N-D\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\alpha_i\right)D^{n-1}V. \end{equation*}
Equivalently,
\begin{equation*} D\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\alpha_i+\frac{\alpha_n}{D^{n-1}V}\leq N. \end{equation*}
This proves \(S_N\subset NP_0\text{.}\) The product of the intercepts of \(P_0\) is \(V\text{,}\) so \(\operatorname{vol}(P_0)=V/n!\text{.}\)
🔗
Fix \(N\) and choose a basis of \(H^0(X,NL)\) with distinct least exponents, as in Lemma 2.3. By Lemma 2.4, one positive integral weight vector makes each of these exponents the unique least-weight term of its expansion. If \(\mathcal M(S_N)\) separates the specified jets at a torus tuple, Lemma 2.5 applies to the basis sections in the frame \(e^N\text{.}\) It gives surjectivity for their jets at some distinct tuple in the trivializing neighborhood of \(p\text{,}\) which is the asserted surjectivity for \(H^0(X,NL)\text{.}\)
