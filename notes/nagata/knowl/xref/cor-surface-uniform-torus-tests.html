Corollary 8.4.

There is an integer \(r_0\) such that, for each \(r\geq r_0\text{,}\) one can choose one \(t>T\) and one subgroup \(G\subset\mathbb T\) with \(r\) points for which the \(J^{m-1}\) evaluation on \(\mathcal M(NP_t)\) is injective for every pair of positive integers \(N,m\) satisfying \(m>N\sqrt{V/r}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For fixed \(r\geq r_0\text{,}\) choose \(t,u\) by Lemma 8.3 and apply Proposition 8.2. The choices of \(t,G\) precede the choices of \(N,m\text{.}\)
