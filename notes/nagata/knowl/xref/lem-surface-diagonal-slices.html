Lemma 7.3.

Let \(a,b,c>0\) satisfy \(c/a+c/b>1\text{,}\) and let \(Q=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(a,0),(c,c),(0,b)\}\text{.}\) Its slice on \(\alpha-\beta=q\) has length in the \(\beta\) coordinate
\begin{equation*} \ell(q)=\begin{cases}c(1+q/b),&-b\leq q\leq0,\\c(1-q/a),&0\leq q\leq a.\end{cases} \end{equation*}
There are no slices outside \([-b,a]\text{.}\) Consequently, the shear followed by vertical compression gives the triangle
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{conv}\{(-b,0),(a,0),(0,c)\}=\{(q,h):-b\leq q\leq a,\ 0\leq h\leq\ell(q)\}. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
In coordinates \((q,\beta)=(\alpha-\beta,\beta)\text{,}\) the vertices are \((0,0)\text{,}\) \((a,0)\text{,}\) \((0,c)\text{,}\) and \((-b,b)\text{.}\) For \(0\leq q\leq a\text{,}\) the slice runs from zero to \(c(1-q/a)\text{.}\) For \(-b\leq q\leq0\text{,}\) it runs from \(-q\) to \(c+(c-b)q/b\text{.}\) Subtracting the endpoints gives the asserted lengths. The region between zero and these lengths is the stated triangle.
