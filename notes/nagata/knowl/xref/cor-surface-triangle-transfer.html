Corollary 7.5.

Let \(N,m,r\) be positive integers. If the \(J^{m-1}\) evaluation on \(\mathcal M(NP_t)\) is injective at some \(r\) distinct torus points, then the \(J^{m-1}\) evaluation on \(H^0(S,NL)\) is injective at some \(r\) distinct points of \(S\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
If \(H^0(S,NL)=0\text{,}\) every tuple works. Otherwise, Proposition 7.4 gives an injective test on \(\mathcal M(NQ_t)\text{.}\) Restrict sections to germs in the fixed chart at \(p\text{.}\) This restriction is injective, and Proposition 7.2 gives distinct initial monomials in \(NQ_t\text{.}\) Their span has injective jets at the torus tuple. The weighted initial-monomial transfer Lemma 2.6 gives injective jets for the section germs at distinct points of the chart, hence for global sections at those points.
