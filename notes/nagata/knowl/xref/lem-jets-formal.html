Lemma 2.5.

Fix integers \(1\leq h\leq n\text{.}\) Let \(Y\) be a smooth integral \(h\)-dimensional \(k\)-variety, and choose formal coordinates \(z\) at \(y\in Y(k)\text{.}\) Let \(f_1,\ldots,f_q\) be regular on a common affine neighborhood \(U\) of \(y\text{,}\) and let \(\gamma_j\in\Z^{n-h}\text{.}\) Suppose \(c_jz^{e_j}\text{,}\) with \(c_j\ne0\text{,}\) is the unique least term of \(f_j\) for one common positive integral weight vector \(\lambda\text{.}\) If the span of \(Z^{e_j}u^{\gamma_j}\) separates jets through degree \(\ell\geq0\) at some distinct torus tuple, then the span of \(f_ju^{\gamma_j}\) does so at a distinct tuple in \(U\times\mathbb T_k^{n-h}\text{.}\) This tuple may be chosen in any prescribed dense open subset of that product. The assertion applies to sections in a local frame.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Let \((Q_i,U_i)\) be a separating tuple for the monomials. Put \(R=k[[s]]\text{,}\) \(F=k((s))\text{,}\) and
\begin{equation*} B=R[\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_n]/(\delta_1,\ldots,\delta_n)^{\ell+1}. \end{equation*}
For each point substitute
\begin{equation*} z_a=s^{\lambda_a}(Q_{i,a}+\delta'_a),\qquad u_b=U_{i,b}+\delta''_b. \end{equation*}
These substitutions are defined on the completed local ring: every \(\lambda_a\) is positive and \(B\) is \(s\)-adically complete. The Laurent substitutions are defined because each \(U_{i,b}\) is nonzero.
🔗

Aside

In characteristic \(p\text{,}\) ordinary derivatives cannot detect the coefficient of \(x^p\) at the origin. Its class in the truncated local ring is nevertheless nonzero when \(\ell\geq p\text{.}\) The argument uses these coefficient classes, with no factorial denominators.
🔗
Use the classes of the monomials in \(\delta\) of total degree at most \(\ell\) as an \(R\)-basis. The images of the functions give a matrix over \(R\text{.}\) Divide its \(j\)th column by \(c_js^{\lambda\cdot e_j}\text{.}\) All normalized entries still lie in \(R\text{,}\) and their reductions modulo \(s\) are the monomial jet matrix. Its surjectivity gives a maximal minor nonzero modulo \(s\text{.}\) The original matrix is therefore surjective over \(F\text{.}\)
🔗
Setting \(\delta=0\) gives actual \(F\)-points of the affine neighborhood. The substitutions factor through their jet algebras after inverting \(s\text{.}\) These algebras and \(B[1/s]\) each have dimension \(\binom{n+\ell}{n}\text{;}\) the surjectivity just proved forces the induced maps to be isomorphisms. Repeated centers would make two target blocks factor through one jet algebra and contradict surjectivity. Thus the functions separate jets at distinct \(F\)-points.
🔗
The open locus of separating tuples is consequently nonempty. In the integral configuration space it meets the tuples in any prescribed dense open subset. Such an intersection has a \(k\)-point because \(k\) is algebraically closed. A local frame gives the line-bundle assertion. This proves the formal transfer in [OAI-P, Lemma 2.4].
