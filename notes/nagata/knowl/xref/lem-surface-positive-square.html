Lemma 8.5.

Let \(Y\) be a smooth integral complex projective surface, let \(A\) be a nef real divisor class, and let \(F\) be a rational Cartier divisor. If \(F^2>0\) and \(A\cdot F>0\text{,}\) then \(H^0(Y,\cO_Y(qF))\ne0\) for every sufficiently large positive integer \(q\) divisible by the denominators of \(F\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For such integers, Serre duality gives
\begin{equation*} h^2(Y,\cO_Y(qF))=h^0(Y,\cO_Y(K_Y-qF)). \end{equation*}
For large \(q\text{,}\) \(A\cdot(K_Y-qF)<0\text{.}\) An effective divisor has nonnegative intersection with \(A\text{,}\) so this \(h^2\) is zero. Surface Riemann–Roch gives
\begin{equation*} h^0(Y,\cO_Y(qF))\geq\chi(Y,\cO_Y(qF))=\frac{q^2F^2-qF\cdot K_Y}{2}+\chi(Y,\cO_Y)>0 \end{equation*}
for sufficiently large \(q\text{,}\) because \(F^2>0\text{.}\) Serre duality is [Sta] (Tag 0FVV); the surface Riemann–Roch formula is [Har77, Theorem V.1.6].
🔗

Aside

Only \(h^2\) must vanish. Then \(h^0=\chi+h^1\geq\chi\text{,}\) and the positive square gives a positive quadratic term. The auxiliary divisor itself need not be nef; nefness of \(A\) and \(A\cdot F>0\) exclude sections of \(K_Y-qF\text{.}\)
