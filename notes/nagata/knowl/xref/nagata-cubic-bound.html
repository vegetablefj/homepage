Lemma 3.4.

If \(r\geq10\) and \(\mathsf U_r(d,m)\) holds, then \(d>3m\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For \(d<3m\text{,}\) every possible exponent is below \(m\text{,}\) and the displayed bundle has degree \(3(d-3m)<0\text{.}\) Thus all coefficients vanish, contrary to Proposition 3.2. If \(d=3m\text{,}\) choose the nine points so that \(A=3L-P\) is nontorsion. Each coefficient with \(j<m\) would be a section of \((m-j)A\) and hence vanishes. The normal polynomial is then a nonzero constant times \(w^m\text{.}\) Give a tenth point a nonzero normal displacement. This polynomial does not vanish there, again contradicting Proposition 3.2.
