Lemma 12.2.

Let \(Y\) be smooth, integral and projective, let \(A\) be a line bundle, and let \(q_1,\ldots,q_r\) be distinct points. Suppose \(H^0(Y,A)\) separates jets through degree \(\ell\geq1\) at these points. Every integral curve \(C\subset Y\) meeting at least one marked point satisfies
\begin{equation*} A\cdot C\geq\ell\sum_{i=1}^r\mult_{q_i}C. \end{equation*}
The multiplicity is zero at points outside \(C\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Choose a marked point \(q_j\in C\text{.}\) Let \(R=\cO_{C,q_j}\) and let \(\mathfrak n\) be its maximal ideal. The vector space \(\mathfrak n^\ell/\mathfrak n^{\ell+1}\) is nonzero. Otherwise Nakayama’s lemma would give \(\mathfrak n^\ell=0\text{,}\) contrary to \(R\) being a one-dimensional local domain. In a local frame of \(A\text{,}\) lift a nonzero class in this vector space to an ambient jet in the image of \(\mathfrak m_{Y,q_j}^\ell\text{.}\) Prescribe that jet at \(q_j\) and the zero jet at every other marked point. Separation gives a section \(\sigma\) that vanishes to order at least \(\ell\) at all marked points and has nonzero restriction to \(C\text{.}\)
🔗

Aside

Vanishing at the marked points would not suffice if the section contained the entire curve. Prescribing a nonzero degree-\(\ell\) class in the curve’s local ring ensures that the restriction is nonzero. Its zeros then have total length equal to the degree of the restricted line bundle.
🔗
For a one-dimensional Noetherian local domain \((R,\mathfrak n)\) and \(0\ne f\in\mathfrak n^\ell\text{,}\) let \(e(R)\) be its Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity. Since \(f\) is a nonzero divisor, multiplication by its powers gives
\begin{equation*} t\operatorname{length}_R(R/(f))=\operatorname{length}_R(R/(f^t))\geq\operatorname{length}_R(R/\mathfrak n^{\ell t})=\ell t\,e(R)+O(1). \end{equation*}
Dividing by \(t\) and letting \(t\to\infty\) gives
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{length}_R(R/(f))\geq\ell e(R). \end{equation*}
Apply this estimate to the local equations of \(\sigma|_C\) at the marked points on \(C\text{.}\) Its zero scheme is an effective Cartier divisor on the integral projective curve, whose total length is \(\deg(A|_C)=A\cdot C\text{.}\) The required inequality follows by summing the local lengths. The Hilbert–Samuel length polynomial and multiplicity are discussed in [Sta] (Tag 0AZU).
