Definition 6.1 Point and stalk.

A point of a topos \(\mathcal E\) is a geometric morphism
\begin{equation*} p:\Set\longrightarrow\mathcal E. \end{equation*}
Its inverse image \(p^{-1}:\mathcal E\to\Set\) is called its stalk or fiber functor. We write \(F_p=p^{-1}F\text{.}\) The functor has a right adjoint \(p_*:\Set\to\mathcal E\text{.}\)
