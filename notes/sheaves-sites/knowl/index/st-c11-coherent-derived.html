Proposition 11.12.

Write \(\D^b_{\mathrm{coh}}(\mathcal O)\) for the full subcategory of \(\D(\mathcal O)\) with bounded coherent cohomology objects, using the intrinsic coherence of Definition 10.4. It is closed under shifts and cones. If \(\mathcal O\) is coherent, an object of \(\D^-(\mathcal O)\) is pseudo-coherent exactly when all its cohomology objects are coherent. In particular every object of \(\D^b_{\mathrm{coh}}(\mathcal O)\) is then pseudo-coherent. It is perfect precisely when it additionally has locally finite Tor amplitude.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Coherent modules are closed under kernels and cokernels of morphisms between them, and under extensions. Apply these properties to the long exact sequence of cohomology to obtain the first assertion.
🔗
Suppose now that the ring object is coherent and that \(K\) is bounded above with coherent cohomology. To construct an approximation through degree \(m\text{,}\) start at the largest degree with nonzero cohomology, choose finitely many local generators, and lift them to cycles in a representative of \(K\text{.}\) These choices are possible after a covering. The corresponding map from a shifted finite free module surjects onto the top cohomology. Its cone has its new top cohomology an extension of the next coherent cohomology object by the kernel of that finite free surjection; this is coherent. Repeat this finite procedure down to degree \(m\text{.}\) Attaching the shifted finite free modules gives the required strictly perfect approximation. Only finitely many choices of covering are needed for any fixed \(m\text{.}\)
🔗
Conversely, approximate a pseudo-coherent \(K\) below any given degree. It agrees in that degree with the cohomology of a strictly perfect complex. Finite free modules, their summands, and kernels and cokernels between coherent modules are coherent, so that cohomology object is coherent. The final assertion is Theorem 11.11.
