Definition 7.4 Torsor.

Let \(G\) be a group object of \(\mathcal E\text{.}\) A right \(G\)-torsor is an object \(P\) with a right \(G\)-action such that \(P\to1\) is epic and
\begin{equation*} P\times G\longrightarrow P\times P,\qquad (p,g)\longmapsto(p,pg) \end{equation*}
is an isomorphism. Its morphisms are \(G\)-equivariant maps. The trivial torsor is \(G\) with its right translation action.
