Definition 12.3.

For an epimorphism \(U\to A\text{,}\) its Čech nerve has
\begin{equation*} U_n=\underbrace{U\times_A\cdots\times_A U}_{n+1\text{ factors}}, \end{equation*}
with faces omitting a factor and degeneracies repeating a factor. It is \(\cosk_0(U/A)\text{.}\) All its positive matching maps are isomorphisms, so it is a hypercover.
