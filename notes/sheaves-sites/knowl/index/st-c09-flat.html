Definition 9.8 Flat modules.

An \(\mathcal O\)-module \(M\) is flat if \((-)\otimes_{\mathcal O}M\) is exact. Since tensor is right exact, it suffices to require preservation of monomorphisms. A ring morphism \(\mathcal O\to\mathcal B\) is flat if \(\mathcal B\) is flat as an \(\mathcal O\)-module.
