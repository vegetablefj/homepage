Definition 7.1 Descent datum.

A descent datum relative to \(q\) consists of an object \(X\to U\) and an isomorphism
\begin{equation*} \theta:p_2^*X\xrightarrow{\sim}p_1^*X \quad\text{in }\mathcal E/U^{[1]} \end{equation*}
such that its pullbacks to \(U^{[2]}\) satisfy
\begin{equation*} \theta_{12}\theta_{23}=\theta_{13}. \end{equation*}
We also require the pullback of \(\theta\) along the diagonal \(U\to U^{[1]}\) to be the identity; this follows already from the displayed relation because \(\theta\) is invertible. A morphism of descent data is a map over \(U\) commuting with these identifications. Denote the resulting category by \(\operatorname{Desc}(q)\text{.}\) A datum is effective if it is isomorphic to the datum obtained from some \(Y\to S\) by pullback.
