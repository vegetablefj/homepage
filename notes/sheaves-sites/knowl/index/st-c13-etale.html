Definition 13.1.

A morphism of schemes is étale if it is flat, locally of finite presentation, and unramified. Equivalently, it is smooth of relative dimension zero. These scheme-theoretic conditions are understood in their usual local sense. A basic algebraic model is a standard étale algebra
\begin{equation*} B=(A[t]/(P))_g, \end{equation*}
where \(P\) is monic and its derivative \(P'\) is invertible in \(B\text{.}\)
