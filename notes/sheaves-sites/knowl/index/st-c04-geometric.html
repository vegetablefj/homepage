Definition 4.4 Geometric morphism.

A geometric morphism \(f:\mathcal E\to\mathcal F\) is an adjunction
\begin{equation*} f^{-1}:\mathcal F\rightleftarrows\mathcal E:f_* \end{equation*}
whose left adjoint \(f^{-1}\) preserves finite limits. The functors are its inverse image and direct image, respectively.
