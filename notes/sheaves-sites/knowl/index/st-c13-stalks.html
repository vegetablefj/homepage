Definition 13.10.

For a sheaf \(F\) on the small étale site, define
\begin{equation*} F_{\bar x}=\colim_{(U,\bar u)}F(U), \end{equation*}
with transition maps given by restriction to smaller neighborhoods. A germ is represented by a triple \((U,\bar u,s)\text{.}\) Two representatives agree if they agree after pullback to a common neighborhood. This functor is the inverse image of a point of the étale topos.
