Definition 13.6.

The big étale site \((\operatorname{Sch}/X)_{\mathrm{\acute et}}\) has schemes \(T\to X\) as objects and arbitrary \(X\)-morphisms as arrows. Its coverings are jointly surjective étale families \((T_i\to T)\text{.}\) The structure map \(T\to X\) is not required to be étale.
