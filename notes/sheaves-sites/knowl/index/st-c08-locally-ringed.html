Definition 8.13 Locally ringed topoi.

A ringed topos is locally ringed if zero and one have initial equalizer, and locally every section \(a\) of \(\mathcal O\) satisfies the alternative that \(a\) is invertible or \(1-a\) is invertible. In a site presentation, the alternative means that each section over each object has a covering on whose individual members one of the two alternatives holds.
🔗
A ringed morphism is a morphism of locally ringed topoi when its scalar map is local: the inverse image of the subobject of units of \(\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}\) under \(\theta_f\) is precisely the unit subobject of \(f^{-1}\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}\text{.}\)
