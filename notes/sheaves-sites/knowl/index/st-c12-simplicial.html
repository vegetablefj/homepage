Definition 12.1.

Let \(\Delta\) be the category of nonempty finite ordered sets \([n]=\{0,\ldots,n\}\) and order-preserving maps. A simplicial object is a functor \(U_\bullet:\Delta^{\op}\to\mathcal E\text{.}\) Its face maps \(d_i:U_n\to U_{n-1}\) delete a vertex, and its degeneracy maps \(s_i:U_n\to U_{n+1}\) repeat one. They obey the identities imposed by composition in \(\Delta\text{.}\) An augmentation to \(A\) is a compatible family of maps \(U_n\to A\text{;}\) equivalently, it is a simplicial object in \(\mathcal E/A\text{.}\)
🔗
The \(n\)-th matching object \(M_n(U)\) is the object of compatible boundaries of \(n\)-simplices, formed as a finite limit in \(\mathcal E/A\text{.}\) Set \(U_{-1}=A\) and regard \(d_0:U_0\to A\) as the augmentation. We set \(M_0(U)=A\) and \(M_1(U)=U_0\times_AU_0\text{,}\) so compatibility in degree one is equality of augmentations. For \(n\geq2\text{,}\) it consists of tuples of faces \((x_0,\ldots,x_n)\text{,}\) with \(x_i\in U_{n-1}\text{,}\) satisfying
\begin{equation*} d_i x_j=d_{j-1}x_i\qquad(0\leq i<j\leq n), \end{equation*}
and the common augmentation condition. The canonical map \(U_n\to M_n(U)\) sends a simplex to its faces.
