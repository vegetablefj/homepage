Definition 4.11 Cocontinuous functor.

A functor \(u:(\mathcal C,J)\to(\mathcal D,K)\) is cocontinuous if for every \(c\in\mathcal C\) and every covering sieve \(R\) on \(u(c)\text{,}\) the sieve
\begin{equation*} \{h:c'\to c:u(h)\in R\} \end{equation*}
is \(J\)-covering. In a covering-family presentation, this says that each cover of \(u(c)\) can be refined by the image under \(u\) of a cover of \(c\text{.}\)
