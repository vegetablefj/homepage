Definition 9.4 Internal Hom.

For \(M,N\in\Mod(\mathcal O)\text{,}\) define
\begin{equation*} \sheafHom_{\mathcal O}(M,N)(U)=\Hom_{\mathcal O_U}(M|_U,N|_U). \end{equation*}
Restrictions are localization of morphisms. This is the internal Hom, a sheaf of \(\mathcal O\)-modules. The module of global morphisms is written \(\Hom_{\mathcal O}(M,N)\text{,}\) without a sheaf symbol.
