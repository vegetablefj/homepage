Definition 13.3.

The small étale site \(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}}\) has as objects étale morphisms \(U\to X\text{,}\) and as arrows all \(X\)-morphisms. A family \((U_i\to U)\) is a covering if each map is étale and
\begin{equation*} |U|=\bigcup_i\im(|U_i|\to|U|). \end{equation*}
The topology is the one generated by these covering families. The small Zariski site \(X_{\mathrm{Zar}}\) has open immersions into \(X\) as objects, with ordinary open covers; it is equivalent to the site of open subsets of \(|X|\text{.}\)
