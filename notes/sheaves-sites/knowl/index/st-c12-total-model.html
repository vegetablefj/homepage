Definition 12.9.

Let \(K\in\D^+(\Ab(\mathcal E))\) and choose a bounded below injective resolution \(K\to I^\bullet\text{.}\) Define
\begin{equation*} T(U_\bullet,K)=\Tot\bigl(\Gamma(U_p,I^q)\bigr)_{p\geq0},\qquad d=\delta+(-1)^p d_I. \end{equation*}
The terms in total degree \(n\) are \(\bigoplus_{p+q=n}\Gamma(U_p,I^q)\text{.}\) This sum is finite because \(q\) has a fixed lower bound. The augmentation gives a map \(\Gamma(A,I^\bullet)\to T(U_\bullet,K)\text{.}\)
