Definition 13.8.

A geometric point of \(X\) is a morphism \(\bar x:\Spec\Omega\to X\) with \(\Omega\) algebraically closed. An étale neighborhood of \(\bar x\) is an étale map \(U\to X\) with a lift \(\bar u:\Spec\Omega\to U\text{.}\) A morphism of neighborhoods is an \(X\)-morphism carrying the chosen lift to the chosen lift.
