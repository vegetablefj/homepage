Definition 12.2.

An augmented simplicial object \(U_\bullet\to A\) is a hypercover, or hypercovering, if every matching map
\begin{equation*} U_n\longrightarrow M_n(U) \end{equation*}
is an epimorphism, including \(U_0\to A\text{.}\) Equivalently, \(U_0\to A\) is epic and
\begin{equation*} U_{n+1}\longrightarrow(\cosk_n\sk_n U)_{n+1} \end{equation*}
is epic for every \(n\geq0\text{.}\)
