Definition 6.4 Covering compatibility for a flat functor.

A covariant functor \(A:\mathcal C\to\Set\) is \(J\)-continuous if, for every covering sieve \(R\) on \(U\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} A(U)= \bigcup_{(v:V\to U)\in R}\im\bigl(A(V)\xrightarrow{A(v)}A(U)\bigr). \end{equation*}
Thus a neighborhood label over \(U\) lifts to some arrow in every cover. For a covering family, this is surjectivity of \(\coprod_iA(U_i)\to A(U)\text{.}\)
