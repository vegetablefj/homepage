Definition 2.1.

Give \(J(U)\) the inclusion order. Its opposite has an arrow \(R\to S\) if \(S\subseteq R\text{.}\) Define
\begin{equation*} F^+(U)=\colim_{R\in J(U)^{\op}}\Hom_{\PSh(\mathcal C)}(R,F). \end{equation*}
A class is represented by a matching family \(\phi:R\to F\text{.}\) Two representatives are equal when their restrictions agree on a covering sieve contained in both domains. The map \(\eta_F:F\to F^+\) uses the maximal sieve and Yoneda.
