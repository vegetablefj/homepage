Definition 8.9 Morphisms of ringed topoi.

A morphism
\begin{equation*} (f,f^\sharp):(\mathcal E,\mathcal O_{\mathcal E})\longrightarrow(\mathcal F,\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}) \end{equation*}
consists of a geometric morphism \(f\) and a ring morphism \(f^\sharp:\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}\to f_*\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}\text{.}\) By adjunction this is equivalently a ring morphism
\begin{equation*} \theta_f:f^{-1}\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}. \end{equation*}
A morphism of ringed sites includes a morphism of the associated topoi with this structure map; a bare functor of the underlying categories does not suffice.
