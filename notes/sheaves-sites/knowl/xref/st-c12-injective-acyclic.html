Corollary 12.8.

For an injective abelian sheaf \(I\text{,}\) the augmented cochain complex
\begin{equation*} 0\longrightarrow\Gamma(A,I)\longrightarrow\Gamma(U_0,I)\longrightarrow\Gamma(U_1,I)\longrightarrow\cdots \end{equation*}
is exact. Its differential is \(\delta=\sum_i(-1)^id_i^*\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Apply the exact contravariant functor \(\Hom(-,I)\) to Theorem 12.7. The free objects are not required to be projective.
