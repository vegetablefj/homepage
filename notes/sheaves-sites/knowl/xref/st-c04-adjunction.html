Proposition 4.3.

For continuous \(u\text{,}\) sheafification of left Kan extension gives an adjunction
\begin{equation*} u^s=a_{\mathcal D}L_u:\mathcal E \rightleftarrows\mathcal F:u_s. \end{equation*}
Here the input of \(L_u\) is the underlying presheaf of a sheaf. For every presheaf \(P\) on \(\mathcal C\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} a_{\mathcal D}L_uP\simeq a_{\mathcal D}L_u(a_{\mathcal C}P). \end{equation*}
In particular \(u^s(a_{\mathcal C}h_c)\simeq a_{\mathcal D}h_{u(c)}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For sheaves \(F,G\) on the two sites, respectively, the adjunctions give
\begin{equation*} \Hom_{\mathcal F}(a_{\mathcal D}L_uF,G) \simeq\Hom_{\PSh(\mathcal C)}(F,u^{\mathrm{pre}}G) =\Hom_{\mathcal E}(F,u_sG). \end{equation*}
Since \(u^{\mathrm{pre}}G\) is a sheaf, maps from \(P\) into it factor uniquely through \(a_{\mathcal C}P\text{.}\) The same bijections with arbitrary \(G\) prove the second assertion. Apply it to \(P=h_c\) and use the representable Kan extension formula for the last assertion.
