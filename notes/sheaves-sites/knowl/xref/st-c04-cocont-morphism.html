Theorem 4.12.

A cocontinuous functor gives a geometric morphism in the same direction:
\begin{equation*} g:\Sh(\mathcal C,J)\longrightarrow\Sh(\mathcal D,K), \end{equation*}
with
\begin{equation*} g^{-1}G=a_{\mathcal C}(u^{\mathrm{pre}}G),\qquad g_*F=R_uF. \end{equation*}
In the second formula the presheaf right Kan extension is already a sheaf.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
We first check that \(R_uF\) is a sheaf. For a covering sieve \(S\hookrightarrow h_d\text{,}\) its sheaf condition is, by the presheaf adjunction, the bijectivity of
\begin{equation*} \Nat(u^{\mathrm{pre}}h_d,F) \longrightarrow\Nat(u^{\mathrm{pre}}S,F). \end{equation*}
The inclusion \(u^{\mathrm{pre}}S\hookrightarrow u^{\mathrm{pre}}h_d\) is a monomorphism and is locally surjective after sheafification on \(\mathcal C\text{.}\) To see the latter, take an element \(\alpha:u(c)\to d\text{.}\) The pullback sieve \(\alpha^*S\) covers \(u(c)\text{;}\) cocontinuity gives a covering sieve of maps \(h:c'\to c\) such that \(\alpha u(h)\in S\text{.}\) Thus \(\alpha\) lifts locally. By Proposition 2.8, sheafification of the inclusion is an isomorphism, which proves the required bijection.
🔗
The presheaf adjunction now restricts to
\begin{equation*} \Hom_{\mathcal E}(a_{\mathcal C}u^{\mathrm{pre}}G,F) \simeq\Hom_{\mathcal F}(G,R_uF). \end{equation*}
Precomposition and sheafification both preserve finite limits. Hence the left adjoint is left exact and defines a geometric morphism.
