Theorem 9.12.

Every module is a quotient of a flat module. More precisely, the epimorphism constructed in Theorem 8.6 has flat source.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Each summand \(P_U\) is flat and their direct sum is flat. This gives enough flat modules without requiring projective objects or enough points. It is also Stacks, Lemma 18.28.8.
