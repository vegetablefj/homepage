Definition 2.7.

A morphism \(u:P\to Q\) of presheaves is locally injective if \(u(s)=u(t)\) implies that \(s\) and \(t\) agree on a covering sieve, for all sections on every object. It is locally surjective if, for every \(q\in Q(U)\text{,}\) the sieve
\begin{equation*} \{f:V\to U\mid q|_f\in\im(P(V)\to Q(V))\} \end{equation*}
covers. A local isomorphism of presheaves means a map having both properties.
