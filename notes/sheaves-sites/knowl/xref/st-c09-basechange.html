Proposition 9.6.

For a ringed geometric morphism \(f:(\mathcal E,\mathcal O_{\mathcal E})\to(\mathcal F,\mathcal O_{\mathcal F})\text{,}\) pullback is strong symmetric monoidal:
\begin{equation*} f^*(M\otimes_{\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}}N)\simeq f^*M\otimes_{\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}}f^*N,\qquad f^*\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}\simeq\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}. \end{equation*}
There is a natural comparison morphism
\begin{equation*} f^*\sheafHom_{\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}}(M,N)\longrightarrow\sheafHom_{\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}}(f^*M,f^*N). \end{equation*}
It is not asserted to be an isomorphism for arbitrary modules.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Underlying inverse image preserves finite products and all colimits. It therefore preserves the free constructions and coequalizer relations defining tensor products over the pulled-back ring. The associativity of extension of scalars then gives the first isomorphism. The unit formula is immediate from scalar extension. Apply \(f^*\) to evaluation and use this tensor isomorphism. Its adjoint under the closed structure is the internal-Hom comparison. The construction proves naturality. See Stacks, Lemma 18.26.2 and Remark 18.27.3.
