Proposition 2.9.

The functors \(F\mapsto F^+\) and \(a\) preserve finite limits. Sheafification of abelian-group, ring, and module presheaves is obtained by sheafifying the underlying sets and their operations. For abelian presheaves, sheafification is an exact functor to abelian sheaves.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a covering sieve \(R\text{,}\) the functor \(\Hom(R,-)\) preserves all limits. Filtered colimits of sets commute with finite limits. Use these facts with the same filtered index \(J(U)^{\op}\) for every presheaf in a finite diagram. This proves the plus assertion objectwise; composing it proves the assertion for \(a\text{.}\)
🔗
Algebraic operations of finite arity extend using the finite-product comparisons, and the equations between them extend by naturality. For a presheaf of modules over a ring presheaf \(A\text{,}\) the result is a module over \(aA\text{.}\) Their universal properties are inherited from the underlying-set adjunction. In abelian groups, preservation of finite limits gives kernels; the left-adjoint property gives cokernels in abelian sheaves. Thus sheafification is exact.
