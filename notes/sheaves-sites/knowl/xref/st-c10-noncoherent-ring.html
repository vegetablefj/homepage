Example 10.8.

Let \(V\) be an infinite-dimensional vector space over a field \(k\text{,}\) and let \(R=k\oplus V\) with \(V^2=0\text{.}\) On the one-point ringed topos with ring \(R\text{,}\) the module \(R\) is finite free and finitely presented, but not coherent.
🔗
Solution.
For a nonzero \(v\in V\text{,}\) multiplication \(R\xrightarrow{v}R\) has kernel \(V\text{.}\) The \(R\)-action on this kernel factors through \(k\text{,}\) so finite generation would make \(V\) finite-dimensional. This contradicts its construction. Thus coherence fails for one finite list in \(R\text{,}\) although the identity presentation of \(R\) has no relations.
