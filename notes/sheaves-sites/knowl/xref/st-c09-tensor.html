Definition 9.1 Tensor product.

For \(M,N\in\Mod(\mathcal O)\text{,}\) their tensor product is the sheafification of
\begin{equation*} U\longmapsto M(U)\otimes_{\mathcal O(U)}N(U). \end{equation*}
It is denoted \(M\otimes_{\mathcal O}N\text{.}\) Equivalently, it represents \(\mathcal O\)-balanced biadditive morphisms from \(M\times N\) into an \(\mathcal O\)-module.
