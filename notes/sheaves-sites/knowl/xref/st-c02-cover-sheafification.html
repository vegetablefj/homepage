Proposition 2.10.

A sieve \(R\subseteq h_U\) covers \(U\) if and only if \(aR\to ah_U\) is an isomorphism. Consequently the full subcategory \(\Sh(\mathcal C,J)\subseteq\PSh(\mathcal C)\) determines \(J\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
The inclusion is already locally injective. If \(R\) covers and \(f\in h_U(V)\text{,}\) then \(f^*R\) covers \(V\) and makes \(f\) locally an arrow in \(R\text{.}\) Hence the inclusion is locally surjective and its sheafification is an isomorphism. Conversely, if that sheafification is an isomorphism, the local-surjectivity criterion applied to \(\id_U\in h_U(U)\) says exactly that \(R\) covers. The full reflective subcategory determines its reflector up to the unique unit-compatible isomorphism, and this criterion then determines the covering sieves.
