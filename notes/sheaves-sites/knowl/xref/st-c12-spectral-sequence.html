Theorem 12.11.

The total model has a natural convergent spectral sequence
\begin{equation*} E_1^{p,q}=H^q(U_p,K)\ \Longrightarrow\ H^{p+q}(A,K),\qquad p\geq0. \end{equation*}
The first differential is the alternating sum of pullbacks by the faces, and \(d_r\) has bidegree \((r,1-r)\text{.}\) If \(U_p=\coprod_{\alpha}a h_{V_{p,\alpha}}\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} E_1^{p,q}=\prod_\alpha H^q(V_{p,\alpha},K). \end{equation*}
Each abutment degree has a finite filtration. If all positive cohomology of a sheaf \(F\) on all the \(U_p\) vanishes, its underived hypercover cochain complex \(\Gamma(U_\bullet,F)\) computes \(H^*(A,F)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Take vertical cohomology of the double complex in Definition 12.9. Restriction of an injective sheaf to a slice is injective, so it computes the displayed local cohomology. For coproducts the section complex is a product of complexes of groups, and products of groups are exact. Boundedness below of \(I\) gives the finite filtration. For a sheaf acyclic on the terms, the spectral sequence has only its row \(q=0\text{.}\) A classical site version is Stacks, §25.5.
