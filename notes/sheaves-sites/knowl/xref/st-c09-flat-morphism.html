Definition 9.15 Flat ringed morphisms and relative flatness.

A ringed morphism \(f:(\mathcal E,\mathcal O_{\mathcal E})\to(\mathcal F,\mathcal O_{\mathcal F})\) is flat if its scalar map \(f^{-1}\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}\to\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}\) is flat. An \(\mathcal O_{\mathcal E}\)-module \(N\) is flat over \((\mathcal F,\mathcal O_{\mathcal F})\) if it is flat as an \(f^{-1}\mathcal O_{\mathcal F}\)-module.
