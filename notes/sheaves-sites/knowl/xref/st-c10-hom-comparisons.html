Proposition 10.18.

The pullback comparison for internal Hom is an isomorphism if its first argument is finite locally projective. It is also an isomorphism for a locally finitely presented first argument when the ringed morphism is flat. Pullback along every ringed morphism preserves dualizable modules and their duals.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
For a finite locally projective \(P\text{,}\) use \(\sheafHom(P,N)=P^\vee\otimes N\text{.}\) Strong monoidality of \(f^*\) preserves the duality diagrams, and uniqueness of a dual identifies \(f^*P^\vee\) with \((f^*P)^\vee\text{.}\) This gives the first and third assertions. For a locally finitely presented module, use a finite presentation and express internal Hom as the kernel of \(N^n\to N^r\text{.}\) Flat pullback is exact and preserves finite sums, so it preserves this kernel presentation and gives the second assertion.
