Proposition 8.4.

The category \(\Mod(\mathcal O)\) is Abelian. The forgetful functor to \(\Ab(\mathcal E)\) creates kernels and cokernels and preserves all limits and colimits. Filtered colimits in \(\Mod(\mathcal O)\) are exact. A module morphism is a monomorphism if it is injective on sections over each site object, and is an epimorphism if its sections admit lifts locally.
🔗
🔗

Proof.
A kernel is the sectionwise kernel, with the restricted action. The presheaf quotient by the image is a presheaf of modules; its associated Abelian sheaf has a unique induced \(\mathcal O\)-action. It is the cokernel in modules as well. Since image and coimage agree for Abelian sheaves, they agree in modules. Limits carry the componentwise action. A presheaf colimit carries an action and sheafification extends it to the sheaf colimit. These constructions have the required universal properties in modules.
🔗
The underlying Abelian sheaf functor consequently preserves the exact sequences and filtered colimits in question. Exactness of filtered colimits follows from the local sheafification criterion, as in Proposition 3.4. The mono and epi descriptions are the same local criteria for the underlying Abelian sheaves. See Stacks, Lemmas 18.14.1–18.14.2.
