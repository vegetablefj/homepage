# Mathematical writing guides

Jie Fu's guidelines for mathematical writing with AI.

- [Paper writing and revision](paper-revision.md): drafting, editing, proof review, literature, and references.
- [Study notes](study-notes.md): the author's standards for structure, supporting theories, and integration of mathematical literature.
- [Language and word preferences](style-reference.md): detailed style guidance and complete word lists.

To reuse:

> Use the relevant writing guide and style preferences. Complete the requested text, then check its mathematical scope, sources, notation, and language. Correct actual problems and report substantive unresolved issues honestly.

The [online index](https://fujiemath.com/downloads/prompts/README.md) links to the published Markdown files. Edit the sources in `prompts/` and rebuild to update their copies in `downloads/prompts/`.
